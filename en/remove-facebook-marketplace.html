How to remove Marketplace from the Facebook app on iPhone (2026)

To get Marketplace off your screen, open Facebook in minulo, which hides it for free while notifications and messages keep working. In the Facebook app itself, you can take Marketplace off the tab bar: open Settings & privacy, then Settings, Tab bar, Customize the bar, and set Marketplace to Hide.

That only changes the tab bar. We found no setting in Facebook's help that turns Marketplace off for your account. If you want it gone from the screen, minulo hides Marketplace for free when you open Facebook inside it, and notifications and messages keep working.

  1. In the Facebook app, tap Menu on the tab bar and choose Settings & privacy, then Settings, then Tab bar.
  2. Tap Customize the bar, tap the dropdown next to Marketplace and select Hide.
  3. In minulo, tap Add a service, choose Facebook, tap the gear and turn on Marketplace.
  4. In minulo's Block tab, choose the official Facebook app, free for up to 2 hours per block.

Hide the Marketplace shortcut from the tab bar

Facebook's help on personalizing the tab bar covers the iPhone and Android apps. Tap Menu on the tab bar, choose Settings & privacy, tap Settings, then Tab bar, and tap Customize the bar. Each shortcut has a dropdown with Pin, Auto and Hide. Choose Hide for Marketplace.

Facebook says you can't remove Notifications or Facebook home from the tab bar. If what bothers you is the red dot, Facebook says you can tap and hold a shortcut and tap Turn off notification dots.

What hiding the shortcut doesn’t do

The help page is about the tab bar only. It doesn't say Marketplace disappears from the rest of the app, and we found no setting in Facebook's help that turns Marketplace off for your account. Facebook's Marketplace help lists a separate article on turning Marketplace notifications on or off, which we could not open, so we don't give those steps here.

Can you remove Marketplace from the Facebook app?

You can hide its shortcut from the tab bar: Settings & privacy, Settings, Tab bar, Customize the bar, then Hide. To get Marketplace off your screen, open Facebook in minulo, which hides it while notifications and messages keep working (hiding is free). We found no setting in Facebook's help that turns Marketplace off entirely.

Why can’t I remove Notifications or Home from the tab bar?

Facebook's help says those two can't be removed. The other shortcuts can be pinned, set to Auto or hidden.

How do I get rid of the red dot on Marketplace?

Facebook says you can tap and hold a shortcut and tap Turn off notification dots.

Does hiding Marketplace delete my listings or messages?

Hiding the shortcut changes only the tab bar, and hiding it in minulo changes only what you see inside minulo. Neither is described as deleting anything.

Can I hide Marketplace and Reels at the same time?

Yes, in minulo. Marketplace, Reels and Watch, suggested posts and the Home feed are separate switches in the same menu, and hiding them is free.
