minulo vs WallHabit: hold to unlock, or hide Reels? (2026)

WallHabit and minulo both try to stop endless scrolling, in different ways.

WallHabit makes you hold a button for a few seconds before a distracting app opens, and says it can also block parts of apps such as reels and shorts. minulo opens the networks inside its own app with Reels, Shorts and Explore removed, while DMs keep working. For YouTube Shorts, WallHabit works on the official apps themselves, while minulo doesn't change the official YouTube app: you watch YouTube inside minulo, where the Shorts shelf and the Shorts tab are hidden and Shorts links open in the normal player.

Updated 2026-11-03

Pick WallHabit if

  • you want a short pause before every distracting app, rather than a different way to view it;
  • you use Android, or want the same on Chrome on a computer;
  • you want it to act on the apps themselves rather than in a separate browser;
  • you prefer an app that has been on the App Store longer (since October 2025) and has 35 US ratings.

Pick minulo if

  • you want to know exactly which networks are covered and what is hidden on each;
  • you want Instagram DMs and your following feed without Reels in front of you;
  • you want hiding to be free;
  • you use a Mac.

minulo shows each network's website rather than the official app, so screens look a little different from the app. While minulo locks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive.

Is WallHabit free?

The download is free, and its website says it's free, forever. If you only want Reels and Shorts gone and DMs kept, hiding them in minulo is free. The US App Store lists three in-app purchases: Monthly Plan $5.99, Yearly Plan $39.99 and WallHabit Lifetime $99.99 (checked 2026-11-02). Its App Store page does not explain which features need a plan.

Can WallHabit remove Reels inside the official Instagram app?

Its description says it can block parts of apps, like reels and shorts, but it does not say which apps or how, so we could not confirm it. minulo does not change the official app: it shows Instagram's website inside minulo with Reels hidden, and it can lock the official app with Screen Time.

Is WallHabit safe to use?

Its App Store privacy label (checked 2026-11-02) lists purchases, identifiers and usage data as Data Linked to You. minulo's label lists purchases, identifiers, usage data, diagnostics and other data, all as Data Not Linked to You. Privacy labels are written by each developer.

Is WallHabit still updated?

On 2026-11-02, its latest App Store version was 1.1.5, released 2026-08-28. It needs iOS 18 or later, and its App Store page lists English only.
