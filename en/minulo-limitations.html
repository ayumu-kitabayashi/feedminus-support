What are the downsides of minulo?

minulo can't change the official apps, so Reels and Shorts are hidden only inside minulo. While it blocks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. Posting Stories, voice messages and calls aren't available. It is iPhone, Mac, Chrome and Safari only, free blocks last up to 2 hours, and it is new, with few ratings.

Can minulo remove Reels from the official Instagram app?

No. On iPhone, no app can change what another app shows. minulo hides Reels when you open Instagram inside minulo, and it can lock the official app with Screen Time so tapping its icon sends you to minulo instead.

Will I still get Instagram notifications if I use minulo?

Only if the official Instagram app is not blocked. Notifications come from the official app, and they don't arrive while minulo blocks it. minulo itself sends no push notifications.

What is free in minulo and what needs Pro?

Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts on all 10 networks and blocking the official apps for up to 2 hours at a time are free. Pro adds schedules, blocks longer than 2 hours, Hard Mode, blocking a network completely and multiple block lists. In the US it is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year, with a 7-day free trial on the yearly plan.

Does minulo work on Android?

Not yet. minulo is for iPhone and Mac, with Chrome and Safari extensions. The Android version is in testing, and you can join the waitlist to get one email when it is out.
