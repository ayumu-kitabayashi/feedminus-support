Instagram has no setting that removes the Explore tab. We could not find one in Instagram's help as of October 2026. Inside the Instagram app you can only change what Explore shows: tap Not interested on posts, set Sensitive content to Less, or reset suggested content. How to see less of what you don't want in Explore

These steps change what Explore shows. They do not remove the tab.

  1. Tap Explore (the magnifying glass) at the bottom of Instagram.
  2. Touch and hold a post you don't want to see, then tap Not interested. You can also tap Options (the three dots) on a post and choose Not interested.
  3. To cut back on posts that may be upsetting, go to your profile, tap Menu at the top right, tap Content preferences, then Sensitive content.
  4. Tap Select next to Less, then tap Confirm.

Instagram's help says Sensitive content applies to Explore, Search, Reels, comments, accounts you might follow, hashtag pages and recommendations in your feed. Teen Accounts are set to a 13+ content setting automatically.

If Explore is full of things you no longer want, Reset suggested content under Content preferences starts your recommendations over across Explore, Reels and Feed. It cannot be undone and does not change who you follow. See how to reset Instagram recommendations.

What does not remove Explore

  • The Following feed removes suggested posts from your feed while you are in it. The Explore tab is still there. See how to see only people you follow.
  • Snoozing suggested posts pauses suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days. It does not cover Explore. See how to hide suggested posts.
  • Your Algorithm lets you see more or less of certain topics in Reels and Explore. Meta says it is available in English-speaking countries. It changes the topics, not the tab.
  • iPhone Screen Time works on the whole Instagram app, so it cannot block only Explore. Blocking the app blocks DMs too.

No third-party app can change the official Instagram app itself.

How do I clean up my Instagram Explore page?

In Explore, touch and hold a post you don't want and tap Not interested. You can also set Sensitive content to Less under Content preferences, or use Reset suggested content to start your recommendations over. A reset cannot be undone.

Does resetting suggested content clear Explore?

It clears your recommendations across Explore, Reels and Feed at the same time, so Explore starts fresh. The tab itself stays, and Instagram's help says suggestions become personalized again as you like, share and interact with content.

Can Screen Time block only Explore on Instagram?

No. Screen Time limits or blocks the whole Instagram app. It has no option for one tab inside the app, and blocking Instagram this way blocks your DMs too.
