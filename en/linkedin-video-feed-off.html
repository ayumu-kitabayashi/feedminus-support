Turn off video autoplay in the LinkedIn app

  1. Tap your profile photo.
  2. Tap Settings & Privacy.
  3. Tap Account preferences.
  4. Scroll down to General Preferences and tap Autoplay Videos.
  5. Choose Never autoplay videos. The other choices are Wi-Fi connections only and On mobile data and Wi-Fi connections.

On a computer, the same setting is under Me › Settings & Privacy › Account preferences › General preferences › Autoplay videos, as an on and off toggle. LinkedIn says the setting applies as you scroll your feed, and that browser extensions for video players can interfere with it.

Can you hide the LinkedIn video feed?

Not in LinkedIn's settings, as far as we could find. LinkedIn calls it the full-screen vertical video experience. On the mobile app you enter it by tapping Video in the navigation bar at the bottom, by tapping any video in your feed, or from Videos for you in the feed. Inside, you swipe up for more videos and leave with the Back arrow or another tab.

LinkedIn's help says the feed is chosen by the quality and relevance of videos and that there are no options to customize it. It also says the full-screen feed, the video tab and the video carousel may not be available to all members, and that the video tab and carousel are not on the desktop site.

Why short videos keep looping

LinkedIn says that, for now, there is no on/off toggle for auto-looping on videos under 30 seconds. Turning off autoplay changes whether videos start on their own as you scroll. It is not described as stopping the loop once a short video is playing.

Limit LinkedIn with Screen Time

On iOS 26, go to Settings › Screen Time › App Limits (called Time Allowances in iOS 27) › Add Limit, choose LinkedIn and set a daily time. Apple notes that by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once they are reached. This limits the whole app, so messages and job search stop with the video feed.
