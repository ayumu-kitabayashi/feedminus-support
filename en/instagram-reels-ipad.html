Where Reels appear on iPad

When Instagram announced the iPad app, it wrote that when you open the app you'll drop into Reels. Stories sit at the top, messages are one tap away, and the comments on a reel can open beside it while the reel stays full size. The announcement says the app is rolling out on iPad models with iPadOS 15.1 and later.

The iPad app also has a Following feed with three views. Instagram describes All as recommended posts and reels from accounts you follow, Friends as posts and reels from accounts you follow that follow you back, and Latest as posts and reels from accounts you follow in time order, newest first.

Can you turn off Reels on iPad?

We could not find a way in Instagram's help or in the iPad announcement as of October 2026. Neither mentions an option to turn off Reels or to open the app somewhere else. What you can do is go to Following each time and keep suggested posts out of your Feed for a while.

Put Latest first in Following

  1. Tap the Following icon on the left of Home.
  2. Tap All, Friends or Latest at the top to switch views.
  3. To change the order, tap the adjust icon at the top right, then touch and hold a view and drag it up or down.
  4. Tap Done at the top right to save.

This changes the order of the views inside Following. It doesn't change the screen Instagram opens to.

Snooze suggested posts for 30 days

  1. In your Feed, tap Options above a suggested post.
  2. Tap Not interested.
  3. Tap Snooze to pause all suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days.

The same screen also lets you stop seeing similar suggested posts from that creator or posts with certain words. Instagram's help lists the iPad app among the versions this article covers. It is about your Feed, so it doesn't remove Reels.

Set a daily time limit

  1. Tap your profile picture on the left to go to your profile.
  2. Tap Menu at the top right.
  3. Under How you use Instagram, tap Time management.
  4. Tap Daily limit and choose an amount of time.

Instagram's help says Teen Accounts start with a 1 hour daily limit, and a parent or guardian can choose to block Instagram once it is reached.

Why does Instagram on iPad open to Reels?

That is how Instagram designed the iPad app. When it announced the app, Instagram said that when you open it you drop into Reels. It is not a bug and not a test.

Can I turn off Reels in the Instagram iPad app?

We could not find a setting for that in Instagram's help as of October 2026. You can switch to Following and choose Latest, snooze suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days, and set a daily time limit, but Reels stay in the app.

How do I see only people I follow on Instagram for iPad?

Tap the Following icon on the left of Home and choose Latest. It shows posts and reels from accounts you follow, newest first. All also shows only accounts you follow, but in recommended order.

Can I make Latest the default view in Following on iPad?

You can put it first. Tap the adjust icon at the top right of Following, drag Latest to the top and tap Done. This sets the order of the views, not the screen Instagram opens to.

Does minulo work on iPad?

No. minulo is an iPhone app and has no iPad version. On iPhone it opens Instagram's website with the Reels tab and suggested posts hidden, and your DMs keep working. Hiding is free.
