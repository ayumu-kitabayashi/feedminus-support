ScreenZen alternatives if you want Reels and Shorts gone, not the whole app (2026)

ScreenZen is a free, donation-supported app that slows you down before you open a distracting app: a wait that can grow each time, interruptions while you scroll, daily goals and optional strict blocks. It works on whole apps, so once Instagram or YouTube is open, Reels and Shorts are there too. If you want to keep using Instagram for DMs without Reels, an app that hides only those parts fits better: minulo, SocialLite and UNDOOMED do it in their own view, and ScrollGuard does it in Safari. If you like ScreenZen's approach but want other options, one sec and Opal also limit whole apps.

What is a good ScreenZen alternative if I only want to stop Reels and Shorts?

Use an app that hides those parts instead of slowing down the whole app. minulo, SocialLite and UNDOOMED show social networks in their own view with Reels and Shorts hidden, and ScrollGuard does the same in Safari. In minulo, hiding is free on 10 networks, and it can lock the official Instagram and TikTok apps so you land in minulo instead.

Does ScreenZen block Instagram Reels?

Not on their own. ScreenZen works on whole apps: it can make you wait before Instagram opens, interrupt you while you scroll, or block Instagram completely. Once Instagram is open, Reels are there as usual. Its App Store description does not mention hiding Reels or Shorts.

Is ScreenZen free?

Yes. ScreenZen is a free download and its website describes it as donation-supported with no subscription. minulo is also free for hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts and for locking the official apps for up to 2 hours at a time; Pro is a subscription for schedules and longer blocks.

Can I use ScreenZen and minulo together?

They do different jobs. ScreenZen can add a wait or a limit to apps that are not social media, while minulo removes Reels, Shorts and For You inside the social networks you keep for messages.

What does ScreenZen do better than an app that hides Reels?

ScreenZen works with any app and with websites, and it has 52,088 US ratings averaging 4.9. Its wait that grows each time is a good fit if your problem is opening many different apps on autopilot. If your problem is Reels and Shorts in particular, hiding them lets you keep using DMs without waiting.
