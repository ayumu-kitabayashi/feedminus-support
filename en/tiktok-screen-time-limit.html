How to set a daily screen time limit on TikTok

  1. Open TikTok and tap Profile at the bottom.
  2. Tap the Menu button (☰) at the top, then Settings and privacy.
  3. Tap Time and well-being.
  4. Tap Screen time and turn on Daily screen time.
  5. Pick a limit from the options, or set your own.

On the web, click your profile picture, then Settings, Screen time and Daily screen time. On the web you can also set a different custom limit for each day. TikTok says any change to screen time settings applies to both the app and the web version.

What happens when you reach the limit

TikTok's help puts it this way: TikTok notifies you, and "you can choose to close TikTok or enter the on-screen passcode to return to it." The code you need is printed on the same screen, so getting past it takes a few seconds.

That is why so many people search for "TikTok screen time limit not working". Nothing is broken. The limit is there to make you notice the time, and you still decide whether to keep watching.

Other screen time settings in TikTok

Sleep hours (same Screen time page): after your sleep time starts, TikTok mutes push notifications and shows a reminder in the app to consider taking a break. TikTok works out the end time from your age, for example at least 7 hours of sleep for people 18 and over.

Weekly screen time updates sends you a weekly summary of your time and how it compares with the week before. The screen time dashboard (Menu ☰ › Activity center) shows your time on TikTok for each day of the current week, and you can look back up to 3 weeks.

How to make the limit actually stop you (iPhone Screen Time)

If you want TikTok to close for real at the limit, the block has to come from the iPhone, not from TikTok.

  1. Open Settings and tap Screen Time, then App Limits and Add Limit (iOS 26).
  2. Select TikTok, tap Next and set the time.
  3. Set a Screen Time passcode with Lock Screen Time Settings.
  4. Open the TikTok limit again and turn on Block at End of Limit.

On iOS 27, Apple replaces App Limits with Time Allowances, which set time for a category such as Social Media and also cover websites the app declares as associated. On iOS 26, tiktok.com in Safari needs its own entry under Never Allow in Content & Privacy Restrictions. If you chose the passcode yourself, you can still type it and lift the limit, so ask someone else to set it. All of this blocks TikTok messages too. Step-by-step: how to block TikTok on iPhone.

Where is the screen time limit on TikTok?

In the TikTok app, tap Profile, then the Menu button (☰), then Settings and privacy, Time and well-being and Screen time, and turn on Daily screen time. On the web, click your profile picture, then Settings, Screen time and Daily screen time. TikTok says the setting applies to both the app and the web.

Why is my TikTok screen time limit not working?

It is working the way TikTok describes it. When you reach the limit, TikTok notifies you and you can either close the app or type the passcode shown on the screen to keep watching. Because the passcode is right there on the screen, the limit works as a reminder, not a lock.

Why does the TikTok screen time limit keep popping up?

TikTok turns Daily screen time on by default, at 1 hour, for users aged 13 to 17. If you set a limit yourself, the notice appears each day you reach it. You can switch the limit off in Settings and privacy, Time and well-being, Screen time. Daily screen time can also be set through Family Pairing.

How do I remove the TikTok screen time limit?

Go to Profile, Menu (☰), Settings and privacy, Time and well-being, Screen time, open Daily screen time and switch the time limit off. On the web it is under your profile picture, Settings, Screen time, Daily screen time.

Can TikTok lock me out when the time is up?

We could not find a lock in TikTok's help. Its own limit lets you keep watching with an on-screen passcode. To be blocked at the limit, use iPhone Screen Time: on iOS 26, add an App Limit for TikTok, set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at End of Limit. It only holds if someone else knows the passcode. This blocks TikTok messages too.
