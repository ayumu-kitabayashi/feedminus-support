What Jomo does

The US App Store page for Jomo - Screen Time Blocker says it can block individual apps and websites in all browsers, or whole categories such as social media, games and shopping, on the schedule you choose. Its 2.0 release notes describe Locks that block an app continuously, limit how many times a day you can open it, and can ask for an exercise before unlocking. Strict Mode keeps your rules locked for a number of days. Jomo says it uses Apple's Screen Time API and can't see your messages or passwords.

Where an app blocker leaves students stuck

Class group chats, club plans and friends' messages often live in Instagram DMs or Snapchat. A blocker treats Instagram as one app, so blocking Reels means blocking those chats too, and many people end up turning the block off to reply. minulo splits the app: you keep DMs, your following feed and search, and Reels, Explore and suggested posts are not shown.

Jomo is the better pick if you want websites blocked, categories blocked in one tap, or a strict limit on how often you open an app. minulo doesn't block websites.
