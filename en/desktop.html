How to install minulo for Mac

  1. Download. Click Download for macOS to get minulo.dmg.
  2. Move it to Applications and open it. Open minulo.dmg, drag minulo into your Applications folder, and open it from Applications. On the app's first screen (Getting started), click Turn on next to the step that takes you to a useful page when you open a feed. The first time you use each browser, macOS asks whether minulo may control it. Click OK.
  3. Turn on the Safari extension. In Safari, go to Settings, then Extensions, and check both "minulo Blocker" and "minulo Extension". minulo Blocker is the one that hides Reels and Shorts inside the page.
  4. Use it in Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc. For Chrome, click Open in Chrome under Getting started in the Mac app to add the extension, so Reels and Shorts inside the page disappear too. In Edge, Brave and Arc, opening a feed or short-video page takes you to your DMs or another useful page.

What minulo for Mac can't do

  • There is no Windows version.
  • There is no Android version.
  • You choose what to hide in the Mac app. The browser extension has no settings of its own.
  • It needs macOS 26.5 or later.

Can I hide only Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts on a computer?

On a Mac, yes. Install minulo for Mac and it hides only the feeds and short videos on 10 networks, such as Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, in Safari and Chrome. In Edge, Brave and Arc, opening a feed or short-video page takes you to your DMs instead. Your DMs and regular videos keep working. It hides the same things as the iPhone app.

Is minulo for Mac free?

Yes. The Mac app has no paid features, and you don't need an account.

Which browsers and macOS versions does it support?

Safari, Chrome, Edge, Brave and Arc on macOS 26.5 or later. Safari and Chrome hide feeds and short videos inside the page; in Edge, Brave and Arc, opening a feed or short-video page takes you to your DMs instead. In every browser, install the Mac app first. Does it work on Windows or Android?

No. minulo has no Windows or Android version. It runs only on Mac and iPhone.

Does minulo for Mac send any data?

No. Its only network use is checking once a day whether a new version is out.
