How to hide Recommendations on Pinterest and keep your saved Pins (iPhone)

With minulo, you keep using Pinterest while hiding only the parts that take up your time, such as Recommendations. Here is what you can hide and how to set it up.

Updated September 27, 2026

What can I hide on Pinterest?

When you open Pinterest inside minulo, you can hide the following, one item at a time. You can change what is hidden at any time.

  • Recommendations
  • Video pins

What still works after hiding?

Saved and Messages open straight from the minulo home screen. Everything you have not hidden works like the Pinterest website.

How do I set it up?

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. On the home screen, tap "Add a service" and choose Pinterest.
  3. Tap the gear next to Pinterest and turn on what you want to hide.
  4. To stop the official app too, open the "Block" tab, allow Screen Time, and choose Pinterest in "Apps to block".

What happens to the official Pinterest app?

minulo uses iPhone Screen Time to stop the official Pinterest app from opening. When you try to open it, a "Blocked" screen appears, and its "Open in minulo" button takes you to minulo. Blocking is free.

Is it free?

Hiding content on Pinterest and blocking the official app are free. Schedules that switch automatically by day and time, Hard Mode and multiple block lists are part of minulo Pro.
