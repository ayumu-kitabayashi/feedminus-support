How to hide Spotlight and Discover on Snapchat and keep your chats (iPhone)

Snapchat has no setting that removes Spotlight and Discover, but you can hide them by opening Snapchat in another app. minulo is the iPhone app that opens Snapchat with only Spotlight and Discover hidden, so you keep using Chat. Here is what you can hide and how to set it up.

Hiding stays free. Pro (optional) adds Sleep mode, which blocks social apps from your bedtime until morning, Work mode, which locks the apps you pick during your work hours on the days you choose, plus Hard Mode, a daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.

What can I hide on Snapchat?

When you open Snapchat inside minulo, you can hide the following, one item at a time. You can change what is hidden at any time.

  • Spotlight and Discover

What still works after hiding?

Chat opens straight from the minulo home screen. Everything you have not hidden works like the Snapchat website.

How do I set it up?

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. On the home screen, tap "Add a service" and choose Snapchat.
  3. Tap the gear next to Snapchat and turn on what you want to hide.
  4. To stop the official app too, open the "Block" tab, allow Screen Time, and choose Snapchat in "Apps to block".

What happens to the official Snapchat app?

minulo uses iPhone Screen Time to stop the official Snapchat app from opening. When you try to open it, a "Blocked" screen appears, and its "Open in minulo" button takes you to minulo. Blocking is free.

Is it free?

Hiding content on Snapchat and blocking the official app are free. Sleep mode (set bed and wake times), Work mode (set workdays and hours), Hard Mode and a Daily limit are part of minulo Pro.

How to turn off Snapchat notifications you don't want

  1. In Snapchat, tap your profile icon at the top.
  2. Tap ⚙️ to open Settings.
  3. Tap Notifications.
  4. Turn off the toggle for each notification type you don't want.

To choose whose Stories you hear about, tap Manage Story Notifications on the same screen and pick the people you still want. You can also do it per friend: swipe right to the Chat screen, press and hold a chat, tap Story Settings and turn Story Notifications off. If the alerts are about comments on Snaps you posted to Spotlight, turn off Comments in Notifications.

To silence Snapchat completely, open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, tap Snapchat and turn off Allow Notifications. That stops chat notifications as well.

What doesn't remove Spotlight

  • Hiding or skipping Snaps. Snapchat says it uses hiding, skipping and reporting as signs you don't enjoy something, and shows less like it. Spotlight still fills with other Snaps.
  • Turning off personalization. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Snapchat lets you turn off personalized recommendations in Spotlight and Discover or reset suggested content. Snapchat says you will still see content, only less tailored to you.
  • Hide Sensitive Content in Family Center. A parent can turn this on for a teen. It limits sensitive or suggestive content in Stories and Spotlight, but Spotlight stays.
  • Changing the Home Tab. With Snapchat+ you can open the app on a different tab, but the Spotlight button is still at the bottom.

What Snapchat lets you do

Hide one item: on the Stories screen, press and hold a Story or Show tile and tap Hide this Content. To bring it back, tap the three dots at the top of the Stories screen, tap View Hidden Stories and tap Un-Hide. Snapchat adds: "Not all Stories content can be hidden, and you can't completely disable a certain topic, theme, or the Discover section."

Why Discover keeps finding things for you: Snapchat says it ranks Discover using your viewing history, how long you watch, whether you favorite or share, and whether you subscribe. Skipping, reporting and hiding count as signs you don't enjoy something.
