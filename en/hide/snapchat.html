How to hide Spotlight and Discover on Snapchat and keep your chats (iPhone)

With minulo, you keep using Snapchat while hiding only the parts that take up your time, such as Spotlight and Discover. Here is what you can hide and how to set it up.

Updated September 27, 2026

What can I hide on Snapchat?

When you open Snapchat inside minulo, you can hide the following, one item at a time. You can change what is hidden at any time.

  • Spotlight and Discover

What still works after hiding?

Chat open straight from the minulo home screen. Everything you have not hidden works like the Snapchat website.

How do I set it up?

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. On the home screen, tap "Add a service" and choose Snapchat.
  3. Tap the gear next to Snapchat and turn on what you want to hide.
  4. To stop the official app too, open the "Block" tab, allow Screen Time, and choose Snapchat in "Apps to block".

What happens to the official Snapchat app?

minulo uses iPhone Screen Time to stop the official Snapchat app from opening. When you try to open it, a "Blocked" screen appears, and its "Open in minulo" button takes you to minulo. Blocking is free.

Is it free?

Hiding content on Snapchat and blocking the official app are free. Schedules that switch automatically by day and time, Locked Mode and multiple block lists are part of minulo Pro.
