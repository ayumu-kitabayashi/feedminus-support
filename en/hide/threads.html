How to hide Recommendations on Threads and keep your Following feed (iPhone)

With minulo, you keep using Threads while hiding only the parts that take up your time, such as Recommendations. Here is what you can hide and how to set it up.

Updated September 27, 2026

What can I hide on Threads?

When you open Threads inside minulo, you can hide the following, one item at a time. You can change what is hidden at any time.

  • Recommendations (opens Following)
  • Posts in the feed

What still works after hiding?

Following, Notifications and Feed open straight from the minulo home screen. Everything you have not hidden works like the Threads website.

How do I set it up?

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. On the home screen, tap "Add a service" and choose Threads.
  3. Tap the gear next to Threads and turn on what you want to hide.
  4. To stop the official app too, open the "Block" tab, allow Screen Time, and choose Threads in "Apps to block".

What happens to the official Threads app?

minulo uses iPhone Screen Time to stop the official Threads app from opening. When you try to open it, a "Blocked" screen appears, and its "Open in minulo" button takes you to minulo. Blocking is free.

Is it free?

Hiding content on Threads and blocking the official app are free. Schedules that switch automatically by day and time, Hard Mode and multiple block lists are part of minulo Pro.
