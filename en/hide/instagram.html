How to hide Reels and Explore tabs on Instagram and keep your messages (iPhone)

To hide the Reels and Explore tabs on Instagram, open Instagram in minulo, an iPhone app, and turn on "Reels and Explore tabs" under the gear next to Instagram. Your messages keep working, and hiding is free. Instagram itself has no setting for this. Here is what you can hide and how to set it up.

Updated November 11, 2026

What can I hide on Instagram?

When you open Instagram inside minulo, you can hide the following, one item at a time. You can change what is hidden at any time.

  • Reels and Explore tabs
  • Suggested posts
  • Stories
  • Every tab but DMs

What still works after hiding?

Messages, Feed and My posts open straight from the minulo home screen. Everything you have not hidden works like the Instagram website. If you want a feed of only people you follow, see how to see only people you follow on Instagram.

Which items should I turn on?

Pick by what pulls you in. You can combine them.

  • Only Reels and Explore bother you: turn on the switch that hides the Reels and Explore tabs. The two go together; there is no separate switch for Explore alone. Your feed, Stories and DMs stay.
  • Your feed fills with accounts you don't follow: add Suggested posts. If you leave the Reels and Explore switch off, Explore stays.
  • Stories take up your time too: add Stories. Friends' Stories won't show inside minulo while it is on.
  • You only open Instagram to message: turn on Every tab but DMs. Only DMs and notifications stay, and Reels, Explore and the feed are hidden.

If something looks wrong

  • The screen is blank. You are probably not signed in yet. Tap Sign in and Instagram's own login page opens. minulo never receives or stores your password.
  • Reels showed up again. When Instagram changes its screens, hidden parts can come back for a while. minulo re-checks its hiding rules on its own each week. If you spot one, write to workflowai.japan@gmail.com and it is fixed once confirmed.
  • Instagram can't be picked in Apps to block. Blocking needs Screen Time permission, so check that Settings › Screen Time is on. iOS shows the permission prompt only once; if you missed it, tap Open Settings inside minulo.

Other limits, such as no Stories posting or calls, are in what minulo can't do.

How do I set it up?

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. On the home screen, tap "Add a service" and choose Instagram.
  3. Tap the gear next to Instagram and turn on what you want to hide.
  4. To stop the official app too, open the "Block" tab, allow Screen Time, and choose Instagram in "Apps to block".

What happens to the official Instagram app?

minulo uses iPhone Screen Time to stop the official Instagram app from opening. When you try to open it, a "Blocked" screen appears, and its "Open in minulo" button takes you to minulo. Blocking is free for up to 2 hours per block. While the official app is blocked, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo doesn't send any.

Is it free?

Hiding content on Instagram is free, with no subscription and no time limit, and blocking the official app is free for up to 2 hours per block. Sleep mode (set bed and wake times), Work mode (set workdays and hours), Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks over 2 hours are part of minulo Pro, which is optional.
