How to hide Video and suggested posts on LinkedIn and keep your messages (iPhone)

With minulo, you keep using LinkedIn while hiding only the parts that take up your time, such as Video and suggested posts. Here is what you can hide and how to set it up.

Updated November 6, 2026

What can I hide on LinkedIn?

When you open LinkedIn inside minulo, you can hide the following, one item at a time. You can change what is hidden at any time.

  • Video and suggested posts
  • The news feed

What still works after hiding?

Messages, Notifications, Jobs and Feed open straight from the minulo home screen. Everything you have not hidden works like the LinkedIn website.

How do I set it up?

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. On the home screen, tap "Add a service" and choose LinkedIn.
  3. Tap the gear next to LinkedIn and turn on what you want to hide.
  4. To stop the official app too, open the "Block" tab, allow Screen Time, and choose LinkedIn in "Apps to block".

What happens to the official LinkedIn app?

minulo uses iPhone Screen Time to stop the official LinkedIn app from opening. When you try to open it, a "Blocked" screen appears, and its "Open in minulo" button takes you to minulo. Blocking is free.

Is it free?

Hiding content on LinkedIn and blocking the official app are free. Sleep mode (set bed and wake times), Work mode (set workdays and hours), Hard Mode and a Daily limit are part of minulo Pro.

Turn off video autoplay in the LinkedIn app

  1. Tap your profile photo.
  2. Tap Settings & Privacy.
  3. Tap Account preferences.
  4. Scroll down to General Preferences and tap Autoplay Videos.
  5. Choose Never autoplay videos. The other choices are Wi-Fi connections only and On mobile data and Wi-Fi connections.

On a computer, the same setting is under Me › Settings & Privacy › Account preferences › General preferences › Autoplay videos, as an on and off toggle. LinkedIn says the setting applies as you scroll your feed, and that browser extensions for video players can interfere with it.

Why short videos keep looping

LinkedIn says that, for now, there is no on/off toggle for auto-looping on videos under 30 seconds. Turning off autoplay changes whether videos start on their own as you scroll. It is not described as stopping the loop once a short video is playing.

What LinkedIn's own settings do

LinkedIn Help's page on customizing your feed lists these options: unfollow or mute people and companies whose posts you don't want to see, choose I don't want to see this on a post, report inappropriate posts, follow new people and companies, and, on desktop only, choose whether top or recent updates come first.

All of these change what is in the feed. None of them removes it. When you open LinkedIn to answer a recruiter or check a job alert, the feed is still the first thing you see.
