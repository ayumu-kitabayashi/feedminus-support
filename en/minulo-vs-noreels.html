minulo vs NoReels: a NoReels alternative that hides Reels and keeps DMs (2026)

NoReels and minulo are close: both hide Reels and Shorts for free, keep your messages, and use Screen Time to lock the real apps. NoReels focuses on Instagram and YouTube and adds daily limits and weekday schedules for the locked apps. minulo covers 10 networks, including TikTok, and its DMs-only setting is free. Pick NoReels if you only use Instagram and YouTube and want time budgets. Pick minulo if you need more networks or a Mac app.

NoReels, listed on the App Store as “NoReels - Block Reels & Shorts” and made by DaDaMa UG (haftungsbeschränkt), removes Reels, Shorts, the Explore grid and “Suggested for you” from Instagram and YouTube, while your messages, the people you follow and videos people send you stay. If that isn't enough, it uses Screen Time to lock the real apps with daily limits and schedules. Two other apps have similar names, “NoReels” by Thomas Bale and “Noreel: Scroll without reels”; this page is about the one by DaDaMa.

In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free, and Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year.
