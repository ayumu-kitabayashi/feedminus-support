minulo vs Nope: Two Apps That Hide Instagram Reels and Shorts (2026)

Pick minulo if you use more than Instagram and YouTube: you can hide Reels and Shorts on 10 networks, including TikTok, X and Threads, for free with no time limit, and your DMs keep working. Pick Nope if Instagram and YouTube are all you need and you want an app with nothing to buy at all.

Nope is completely free, with nothing to buy, and hides Reels, Shorts and recommendations on Instagram and YouTube. On iPhone and iPad it opens those two inside its own app and can take over the tap on their icons, and it also comes with an optional Safari extension. minulo opens 10 networks inside its own app, keeps your DMs, can lock the official apps with Screen Time, and has a Mac app. If Instagram and YouTube are all you use, Nope fits; if you also use TikTok or other networks, minulo fits.

Pick Nope if

  • you want an app with nothing to buy at all, and an App Store privacy label of “Data Not Collected”;
  • you only need Instagram and YouTube;
  • you want finer YouTube switches (comments, hover previews, the home feed), grayscale or a pause before a site opens;
  • you also want the same switches on a Windows or Linux computer through the Chrome extension.

Pick minulo if

  • you also use TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn or Snapchat;
  • you want an Instagram view with nothing but DMs, for free (Every tab but DMs);
  • you keep opening the official Instagram app out of habit, and want it locked with Screen Time and a button that takes you to the Reels-free view;
  • you also want a Mac app;
  • you want the app in Japanese, Korean or another language besides English.

What is Nope for Instagram Reels?

Nope (“Nope: Reels & Shorts Blocker”, by Juste Apps) is an iPhone and iPad app that hides Reels, Shorts and recommendations on Instagram and YouTube. It opens those two inside its own app, so you don’t need to set anything up in Safari, and it also comes with an optional Safari extension. It can take over the tap on your Instagram or YouTube icon so you land in Nope instead. minulo does a similar job for 10 networks, including TikTok. On the US App Store (checked 2026-11-02) its privacy label says Data Not Collected, and on 2026-11-26 it had 8 ratings in the US, 3 in Korea and 1 in Japan.

What is the difference between Nope and minulo?

You can hide Reels and Shorts with either one for free. Nope covers Instagram and YouTube only and has nothing to buy at all. minulo covers 10 networks, including TikTok, X and Threads, has a free DMs-only view for Instagram and TikTok, and can lock the official apps with Screen Time; Sleep mode, Work mode and Hard Mode are in the paid Pro plan. Nope has iPad support and a Chrome extension; minulo has a Mac app.

Is Nope really free?

Yes. Its App Store page says Nope is actually free, not a free trial, and lists no in-app purchases (version 2.7.0, checked 2026-11-26). With minulo you can also hide Reels, Shorts and suggested posts for free; Sleep mode, Work mode and Hard Mode are part of Pro ($9.99/month or $39.99/year in the US).

Do I need an account?

No, for either app. Nope says there is no Nope account and no Nope server. minulo has no account or sign-up either; you sign in on each network’s own page, so minulo never sees your password.

Does Nope work inside the official Instagram app?

No, but it can take over the tap on your Instagram or YouTube icon, so you land in Nope instead of the official app. minulo does not change the official app either, but it can lock it with Screen Time, and the block screen’s Open in minulo button takes you to Instagram without Reels.

Which one covers TikTok?

minulo. It hides TikTok’s For You feed and keeps TikTok messages. Nope covers Instagram and YouTube only.

Is there an Android version?

minulo does: it has been on Google Play since November 23, 2026. Nope has no Android version; its website says the iOS app is the only mobile option today.
