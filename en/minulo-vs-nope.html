minulo vs Nope: Free Apps That Hide Reels and Shorts (2026)

If you want an alternative to Nope that also covers TikTok, X, Threads and other networks, pick minulo: hiding Reels and Shorts is free and there is no account. Nope is a Safari extension that is completely free, with no paid plan at all, and hides Reels, Shorts and recommendations on instagram.com and youtube.com in Safari. minulo opens 10 networks inside its own app, keeps your DMs, and can lock the official apps with Screen Time. If you only use Instagram and YouTube in Safari, Nope fits; if you use other networks or keep opening the official apps, minulo fits.

Pick Nope if

  • you want an app with no paid plan at all, and an App Store privacy label of “Data Not Collected”;
  • you only need Instagram and YouTube and are happy to use them in Safari;
  • you want finer YouTube switches (comments, hover previews, the home feed), grayscale or a pause before a site opens;
  • you also want the same switches on a Windows or Linux computer through the Chrome extension.

Pick minulo if

  • you also use TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn or Snapchat;
  • you want an Instagram view with nothing but DMs, for free (Every tab but DMs);
  • you keep opening the official Instagram app out of habit, and want its icon to lead to the Reels-free view instead;
  • you want the app in Japanese, Korean or another language besides English.

What is the difference between Nope and minulo?

Both hide Reels and Shorts and are free to use for that. Nope is a Safari extension for Instagram and YouTube, so it works when you open those sites in Safari, and it has no paid plan at all. minulo opens 10 networks, including TikTok, X and Threads, inside its own app, has a free DMs-only view for Instagram and TikTok, and can lock the official apps with Screen Time. Nope has iPad support and a Chrome extension; minulo has a Mac version.

Is Nope really free?

Yes. Its App Store description says “No subscriptions, no in-app purchases, no ads”, and its website says an optional Pro plan may come later (checked 2026-10-27). minulo is also free for hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts; schedules and Hard Mode are part of Pro ($9.99/month or $39.99/year in the US).

Do I need an account?

No, for either app. Nope says there is no Nope account and no Nope server. minulo has no account or sign-up either; you sign in on each network’s own page, so minulo never sees your password.

Does Nope work inside the official Instagram app?

No. Nope is a Safari extension, so it works on instagram.com and youtube.com in Safari. minulo does not change the official app either, but it can lock it with Screen Time, and the block screen’s Open in minulo button takes you to Instagram without Reels.

Which one covers TikTok?

minulo. It hides TikTok’s For You feed and keeps TikTok messages. Nope covers Instagram and YouTube only.

Is there an Android version?

Neither has one. Nope’s website says the iOS app is the only mobile option today, and minulo has no Android version.
