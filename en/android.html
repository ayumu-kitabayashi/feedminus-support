What you can do on Android until then

Until the Android version is out, the apps' own settings and Android itself can help a little.

  • YouTube: in the app, go to You → Settings → Time management → Shorts feed limit and choose 0 minutes. For adult accounts the limit can be dismissed, and Shorts in Subscriptions and in links people send you remain.
  • Instagram: tap the Instagram logo and choose Following to see only posts from accounts you follow, newest first, without suggestions. The Reels tab stays.
  • Android: App timers in Digital Wellbeing set a daily limit for each app. But they stop the whole app, so your DMs stop too. Where the menu is depends on the phone.

None of these removes just the entrance to Reels or Shorts. On iPhone, minulo hides only Reels, Shorts and TikTok For You and keeps your DMs.
