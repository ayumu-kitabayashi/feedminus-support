“Does it work on Android?” Yes, minulo is on Google Play

minulo for Android has been on Google Play since November 23, 2026. It hides Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok For You, and your DMs keep working. If a friend asked you, “Does it work on Android?”, send them this page.

What minulo does on Android

It works with the same 10 networks as on iPhone: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Reddit, Snapchat, Pinterest and LinkedIn. Open one inside minulo and Reels, Shorts and suggested posts are hidden, while your DMs, posts from people you follow and search keep working. Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free.

How it differs from the iPhone version

  • Blocking: on Android, when you open an app you chose to block, minulo shows its own screen on top. Tap “Open in minulo” to use that network without Reels or Shorts.
  • Notifications: on Android, notifications from a blocked app still arrive. On iPhone, while Screen Time blocks an app, its notifications do not arrive.
  • Accounts: switching between several accounts and inviting people are on iPhone only for now.

What Android and the apps can do on their own

  • YouTube: in the app, go to You → Settings → Time management → Shorts feed limit and choose 0 minutes. For adult accounts the limit can be dismissed, and Shorts in Subscriptions and in links people send you remain.
  • Instagram: tap the Instagram logo and choose Following to see only posts from accounts you follow, newest first, without suggestions. The Reels tab stays.
  • Android: App timers in Digital Wellbeing set a daily limit for each app. The whole app stops, so your DMs stop too. Where the menu is depends on the phone.

None of these removes only the Reels or Shorts. minulo hides Reels, Shorts and TikTok For You on Android too, and keeps your DMs.

Do notifications from a blocked app still arrive?

On Android, yes. minulo shows its screen when you open an app you chose to block, but it does not stop that app’s notifications. On iPhone, notifications do not arrive while Screen Time blocks the app.
