How to start Snapchat on a Mac

  1. Update the Snapchat app on your phone. Snapchat asks for the latest version.
  2. On your Mac, open the latest Safari, Chrome or Edge and go to web.snapchat.com.
  3. Log in with the same Snapchat account you use on your phone.
  4. Click a friend's name in the Chat feed to message them. Use the video camera icon for a video call or the phone icon for a voice call.
  5. To send a Snap, open the Camera, click the lens button to take a photo, or hold it to record a video.

Allow the camera, microphone and notifications

Safari: open the Safari menu, choose Settings for web.snapchat.com, and set Camera and Microphone to Allow. For notifications, open Safari › Settings › Websites › Notifications and set web.snapchat.com to Allow.

Chrome or Edge: click the lock icon in the address bar, turn on Camera, Microphone and Notifications, and refresh the page. If you don't see the toggles, turn them on in Site settings.

Put Snapchat in your Dock (Chrome or Edge)

Click the link at the top of your feed to install the desktop app, or click your Bitmoji at the top right and choose Create Desktop Shortcut. To remove it, open Snapchat for Web, click the three-dot menu at the top right, click Uninstall Snapchat, then Remove.

What doesn't work on a Mac

  • One computer at a time. Logging in to Snapchat for Web on another computer logs you out of the first.
  • App-only features. Snapchat lists viewing photo and video Snaps in Memories, managing your Stories and their analytics, changing your username and updating your privacy settings as still only in the mobile app.
  • Spotlight is still there. Snapchat says you can view Spotlight on Snapchat for Web, so using Snapchat on a computer doesn't keep you away from short videos by itself.

Is there a Snapchat app for Mac?

We could not find one in Snapchat's help. Snapchat's device page lists iOS, Android and Fire OS. On a Mac, use Snapchat for Web at web.snapchat.com in Safari, Chrome or Edge. In Chrome or Edge you can add it to your Dock with Create Desktop Shortcut.

Does Snapchat work in Safari on a Mac?

Yes. Snapchat's help lists the latest Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari for Snapchat for Web. To use the camera and microphone in Safari, open the Safari menu, choose Settings for web.snapchat.com and set both to Allow.

How do I get Snapchat notifications on my Mac?

In Safari, open Safari, Settings, Websites, Notifications and set web.snapchat.com to Allow. In Chrome or Edge, click the lock icon in the address bar, turn on Notifications and refresh the page.

Can I be logged in to Snapchat on my Mac and another computer?

No. Snapchat says you can only be logged in to Snapchat for Web on one computer at a time, and logging in on another computer logs you out of the first.
