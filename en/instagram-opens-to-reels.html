Why Instagram is pushing Reels to the front

On October 1, 2025, Instagram head Adam Mosseri wrote: "Reels and DMs have driven most all our growth at Instagram over the last few years, so we are exploring making them the first two tabs." He said it was an option for a small group of people in India, that photos from accounts you follow are still in the Home tab, and that people who opt in can opt right out.

On October 5, 2026, Social Media Today reported a new test, found by app researcher Radu Oncescu, that lets users choose to open Instagram to the Reels feed. According to the in-app explainer it reported, Stories stay at the top, you swipe across to the regular feed, and you can go back to the regular feed whenever you want. In February 2026 the same site reported a redesign test that opens to Reels with a new "Your Feeds" center.

How to get back to your feed

  1. If you turned on a Reels-first option, look for the prompt or setting that switches back to the regular feed. Mosseri and the 2026 explainer both said you can leave it, but we could not find the exact menu in Instagram's help.
  2. Otherwise, tap Home (the house icon) to get to your feed.
  3. To skip suggested posts, tap Instagram at the top left and choose Following.

Following shows posts from people you follow, newest first. It doesn't stay, so Instagram opens to the regular view again next time. See how to see only people you follow.

Why Instagram on iPad opens to Reels

When Instagram launched its iPad app in September 2025, it said: "Now, when you open the app, you'll drop into Reels." That was a design choice for bigger screens, not a bug. The iPad app added a Following tab with All, Friends and Latest views. Latest is in time order.
