Best apps to limit screen time on iPhone: app and social media blockers (2026)

Apps that limit screen time on iPhone fall into three groups. Some block whole apps (Screen Time, Opal, ScreenZen), some add a pause before an app opens (one sec, ScreenZen), and some hide only Reels and Shorts inside social media (minulo, SocialLite). If you only want to stay off your phone while studying, the Forest focus timer is another option. All of them are free to download.

This page is written by the developer of minulo. Facts about other apps come only from their App Store descriptions and US rating counts as of October 9, 2026. Facts about iPhone Screen Time come from Apple Support pages checked the same day.

How to choose

Block social media or your phone completely: Screen Time (ask someone else to set the passcode), Opal's Deep Focus, or ScreenZen's strict block. While an app is blocked, its DMs are blocked too.

Keep using social media but stop Reels and Shorts: minulo or SocialLite. Screen Time and Opal work on whole apps and cannot block only part of one.

Focus while studying: Forest's focus timer, Screen Time Downtime (Screen Time Schedules on iOS 27), or an Opal schedule.

Open apps less often: one sec or ScreenZen add a pause instead of a block.

Are there free apps to limit screen time on iPhone?

Yes. iPhone Screen Time is built in and free, and every app in this table is free to download. What is free differs by app: one sec is free for one app, and in minulo hiding Reels and Shorts and blocking the official apps for up to 2 hours at a time are free.

What is the best social media blocker app?

It depends on what you want to keep. To block social media entirely, use Screen Time with a category limit or an app blocker like Opal. To keep DMs and block only Reels and Shorts, use an app that hides those parts, such as minulo or SocialLite.

Which app helps you focus on studying?

Forest is a focus timer that blocks apps outside your Allow List during a session (iOS 16 or later). Without another app, you can schedule study hours with Screen Time Downtime.

How do I stop myself from deleting the blocker app?

In Screen Time, go to Content & Privacy Restrictions, then iTunes & App Store Purchases, and set Deleting Apps to Don't Allow (iOS 26 screens). If someone else sets your Screen Time passcode, you cannot change it back yourself.
