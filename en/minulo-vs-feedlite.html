minulo vs FeedLite: a FeedLite alternative that hides Reels and keeps DMs (2026)

If you want Reels and Shorts gone but still need your DMs, both apps can help. FeedLite does it for Instagram and YouTube, and its listing describes subscriptions of $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year. minulo is a FeedLite alternative that hides Reels, Shorts and suggested posts on 10 networks, including TikTok, while your DMs keep working, and hiding is free.

FeedLite, listed on the App Store as “FeedLite - Block Reels&Shorts” and made by RCF LLC, calls itself a digital filter rather than a blocker. Instead of locking you out of Instagram and YouTube, it removes Reels, Stories, Explore and Shorts so you can still use Instagram for messages and YouTube for searches. Its settings let you turn each part on or off separately.

Pick FeedLite if

  • you only use Instagram and YouTube;
  • you also want to hide Instagram Stories, Instagram videos or your YouTube subscriptions;
  • you want to see a count of the scrolls you avoided;
  • you use an iPad.

Pick minulo if

  • you need TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn or Snapchat without the feed;
  • you want hiding to be free;
  • you want to lock the official apps with Screen Time and open the Reels-free version instead;
  • you use a Mac, or want the app in a language other than English.

minulo shows each network's website rather than the official app, so screens look a little different from the app. While minulo locks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive.

Is minulo a good FeedLite alternative?

If you use more than Instagram and YouTube, yes. Both apps hide Reels and Shorts and let you keep using Instagram DMs, but minulo covers 10 networks, including TikTok, Threads, X and Reddit, and hiding is free. If you only care about Instagram and YouTube and like FeedLite's extra switches, such as hiding Instagram Stories or YouTube subscriptions, FeedLite does that. If you want the opposite, Stories without Reels, see how to watch just Stories on Instagram.

Is FeedLite free?

The download is free. FeedLite's App Store listing describes a monthly subscription at $9.99 and a yearly one at $39.99 with a 3-day free trial, and the in-app purchase list also shows other “Feedlite Pro” prices from $4.99 to $29.99. The listing doesn't say which features work without paying. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free, and Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

Which apps does FeedLite work with?

Instagram and YouTube. Its listing talks about Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, and its settings screenshot has switches for Instagram (Reels, Stories, Videos, Explore) and YouTube (Shorts, Subscriptions). TikTok and other networks are not mentioned (checked 2026-11-04). minulo works with Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn and Snapchat.
