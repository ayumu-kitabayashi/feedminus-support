How to block only Instagram during Downtime and keep LINE (iPhone, 2026)

minulo can lock just the official Instagram app with Screen Time for free, up to 2 hours per block, while LINE stays open, and it hides Reels and keeps DMs if you open Instagram inside it. Downtime works the other way round: Apple says that during downtime only calls, messages and apps you choose to allow are available, so LINE is blocked too unless you allow it. To stop only Instagram, add LINE and any other app you need at night to Always Allowed, keep Instagram off that list, and turn on Block at Downtime. On iOS 27, Screen Time Schedules work by category, and Always Allowed apps stay available regardless.

Why Downtime stops LINE too

Downtime is not a list of apps to block. It is a time when most apps are unavailable. Apple's guide says: during downtime, only calls, messages, and apps you choose to allow are available. LINE is an app like any other, so unless you allow it, it is blocked along with Instagram. The fix is to turn the list around: allow what you need, and leave Instagram out.

Keep LINE with Always Allowed (iOS 26)

  1. Open Settings and tap Screen Time, then Always Allowed.
  2. Under Choose Apps, tap the add button next to LINE. Add any other app you want at night too.
  3. Check Allowed Apps. If Instagram is there, remove it.
  4. Go back, tap Downtime, turn on Scheduled and set the days and times.
  5. Turn on Block at Downtime. Apple says you can choose this once you have set a Screen Time passcode.

Apple describes Always Allowed as the apps you can use at all times, even during downtime. With Block at Downtime off, you only see a reminder and can keep using Instagram. If Instagram still opens, see Downtime not blocking Instagram.

Or limit only Instagram with App Limits

  1. Go to Settings, Screen Time, App Limits, then tap Add Limit.
  2. Tap a category name to see its apps, and select only Instagram.
  3. Tap Next and set the time allowed. Customize Days sets a different amount for each day.

This touches Instagram alone, so LINE is never in the way. The catch is that Apple's steps set an amount of time per day, not a time of night, so it doesn't stop Instagram at a set hour. And by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once reached. See how to stop Ignore Limit.

On iOS 27: Screen Time Schedules

In Apple's iOS 27 guide, you go to Screen Time, Screen Time Schedule, turn on Schedule, tap Add Schedule, and for each time window choose which Time Allowance categories, such as Social Media, are available. Apple says these features need iOS 27, iPadOS 27, macOS 27, watchOS 27, visionOS 27 or later on all your Apple devices.

Because this works by category, we couldn't confirm whether Instagram and LINE fall in the same one. You can check under Time Allowances, Manage Categories, by tapping a category to see its apps. The sure way is Always Allowed: tap Screen Time, Always Allowed, then Add Apps and add LINE. Apple says those apps stay available regardless of any schedules or time allowances.

Where is Always Allowed on iPhone?

Settings, Screen Time, Always Allowed. On iOS 26, add apps under Choose Apps. On iOS 27, tap Add Apps. Apps there stay available during downtime and schedules.

Can App Limits block only Instagram at night?

App Limits can target only Instagram, but Apple's steps set an amount of time per day, not a time of night. By default the limit can also be ignored once reached.

Does Downtime block Instagram DMs too?

Yes. Downtime works on the whole app, so DMs stop along with Reels. To keep DMs and drop Reels, open Instagram in minulo, which hides Reels for free.

How does this work on iOS 27?

Use Screen Time Schedule and choose which Time Allowance categories each time window allows. We couldn't confirm which category LINE and Instagram are in, so add LINE to Always Allowed. All your Apple devices need iOS 27 or later.
