Does YouTube Premium remove Shorts? What it changes and what it doesn't (2026)

YouTube Premium doesn't remove Shorts, and its list of benefits has nothing that hides them, while minulo hides the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab for free. YouTube's help describes Premium as watching without ads before and during videos, downloads, background play, picture-in-picture and more, and it notes that some of these don't apply to Shorts: background play isn't available in the Shorts player, and Auto speed and the 1080p Premium option aren't offered for Shorts. The setting YouTube does offer is the Shorts feed limit in the YouTube app's settings, which can be set to zero but can be dismissed, and YouTube's help doesn't tie it to Premium.

What Premium includes, according to YouTube

YouTube's help page on using Premium benefits on iPhone and iPad lists: watching videos without ads, downloading videos to watch offline, background play, picture-in-picture, continue watching, on-the-go mode, queueing videos, Jump ahead, Auto speed, changing video and audio quality, Premium badges, and other benefits such as smart downloads and early access to new features.

None of these hides Shorts, turns off the Shorts tab or removes the Shorts shelf.

What the Premium help says about Shorts

  • Background play: not available in the YouTube Shorts player.
  • Picture-in-picture: for non-music long-form and Shorts content, available to all users on iOS and Android.
  • Jump ahead and Auto speed: not available on Shorts.
  • 1080p Premium: not offered for Shorts.

On ads, the help says Premium lets you watch videos without interruptions by ads before and during a video. It doesn't mention Shorts in that section, so we couldn't confirm how ads on Shorts work with Premium.

What reduces Shorts without paying

  1. In the YouTube app, tap the You tab, then Settings, Time management and Shorts feed limit.
  2. Pick a limit. YouTube says the list includes zero.

YouTube's help says a reminder appears when the limit is reached in the Shorts feed, with an option to dismiss or ignore it. Shorts also show in search, Subscriptions and channel pages; see Shorts in Subscriptions and search.
