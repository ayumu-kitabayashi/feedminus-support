How to stop watching YouTube Shorts in bed: settings that apply at night (iPhone, 2026)

YouTube has no Shorts setting that switches on only at night. Three things in the YouTube app help: the bedtime reminder (profile picture › Settings › General › Remind me when it's bedtime), which shows a reminder between the times you set; the Shorts feed limit (You › Settings › Time management › Shorts feed limit), which can be set to 0 but applies all day; and the sleep timer in the player, which pauses a video after a set time. All of them can be dismissed or reset. For a stop you can't tap past, schedule iPhone Downtime with Block at Downtime (iOS 26) or a Screen Time Schedule (iOS 27), which blocks the whole YouTube app. If you still want YouTube without Shorts, minulo hides Shorts for free, and minulo Pro's Sleep mode blocks apps every night at your bedtime.

Set YouTube's bedtime reminder

  1. In the YouTube app, tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Settings, then General.
  3. Turn on Remind me when it's bedtime, choose a start and end time, and tap OK.
  4. If you want to finish the current video first, choose Wait until I finish video to show reminder.

When the reminder appears you can close it or tap Remind Me Again for another reminder in 15 minutes. YouTube says this is on by default for users aged 13 to 17 and off for adults, and that it works on iPhone but not iPad. You can also reach it from your profile picture › Time Watched.

Set the Shorts feed limit to 0

  1. Sign in to the YouTube app.
  2. Tap the You tab, then Settings.
  3. Tap Time management, then Shorts feed limit.
  4. Pick a limit. 0 is in the list.

When you reach it, YouTube shows a message and lets you dismiss or ignore the limit. There is no time of day setting, so a limit of 0 also applies in the daytime. More on this in our Shorts feed limit guide.

Use the sleep timer if you fall asleep watching

While a video is playing, tap Settings in the player, tap Sleep timer and choose how long it runs. When the time is up the video pauses and you get a silent notification. It stops a video from running all night, but it does not stop you from scrolling.

Block YouTube at night on iPhone

iOS 26: go to Settings › Screen Time › Downtime, tap Scheduled, choose Every Day or Customize Days and set the times. Apple sends a reminder 5 minutes before Downtime starts. By default you can keep using apps after the reminder. To stop that, set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime. Then restricted apps appear dimmed with an hourglass and won't open. Anything in Always Allowed stays open.

iOS 27: go to Settings › Screen Time › Screen Time Schedule, turn on Schedule, tap Add Schedule, pick the days and set a night time window. Choose which Time Allowance categories are available in it. Apple notes this needs iOS 27 or later on all your Apple devices.

Both work on the whole YouTube app, so long videos and subscriptions are blocked too. For a wider night routine, see how to stop doomscrolling at night.

Can I turn off YouTube Shorts only at night?

YouTube's help describes no Shorts setting tied to a time of day. The bedtime reminder works on a schedule but can be dismissed, and the Shorts feed limit applies all day. To block YouTube only at night, use iPhone Downtime with Block at Downtime, or a Screen Time Schedule on iOS 27.

How do I set a bedtime reminder on YouTube?

Tap your profile picture, then Settings, then General. Turn on Remind me when it's bedtime, choose a start and end time and tap OK. You can choose to wait until the current video ends before the reminder shows.

Does the Shorts feed limit stop me from watching?

No. When you reach the limit, YouTube shows a reminder and lets you dismiss or ignore it. Setting it to 0 makes the reminder show as soon as you open the Shorts feed, but you can still tap past it.

How do I block YouTube at night on iPhone?

On iOS 26, schedule Downtime in Settings, Screen Time, Downtime, set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime. On iOS 27, use Screen Time Schedule with a night time window. Both block the whole YouTube app.

Can I watch YouTube at night without Shorts?

Yes, in minulo. It opens YouTube with the Shorts shelf and tab hidden for free, at any hour. If you want nothing at all at night, minulo Pro's Sleep mode blocks social media at your bedtime, even inside minulo.
