How to turn off recommendations on Pinterest on iPhone (2026)

minulo hides the Pinterest home feed on iPhone for free: open Pinterest inside minulo and the Recommendations switch hides the feed of picked-for-you Pins, while your saved Pins and messages stay.

Pinterest's own tools make recommendations smaller, not gone: See fewer like this on a Pin, unfollowing the board or account it came from, and Refine your recommendations, where you can turn off recommendations based on Pins you looked at. We found no setting in Pinterest's help that turns the home feed off.

  1. Install minulo, tap Add a service and choose Pinterest.
  2. Tap the gear next to Pinterest. Recommendations hides the home feed along with Today, Videos and Ideas. Video pins can be hidden the same way.
  3. Open Saved and Messages from the minulo home screen.
  4. In the Block tab, choose the official Pinterest app, free for up to 2 hours per block.

Where Pinterest recommendations come from

Pinterest calls the home feed "a feed of Pins that have been picked for you". Its help says the picks are based on the people you follow, the boards you create, the Pins you engage with and your searches. So every save and search adds to what the home feed shows.

What Pinterest lets you do

See fewer like this: tap the ellipsis icon on a Pin in your home feed and choose See less (or See fewer like this). Pinterest also suggests unfollowing the board, topic or account a Pin came from.

Refine your recommendations: in the app, tap your profile picture at the bottom right, tap the more-options icon at the top right and choose Refine your recommendations. The Activity tab lets you turn off recommendations based on Pins you have looked at. The AI content tab has a Show AI content switch and per-topic switches for AI-generated Pins. Pinterest says these settings don't change the ads you see.

None of these is described as turning the home feed off. You still get Pins picked for you, just fewer of a kind.

Can you turn off recommendations on Pinterest?

Not completely, as far as Pinterest's help shows in November 2026. You can turn off recommendations based on Pins you looked at in Refine your recommendations, and see fewer Pins like one. In minulo the Recommendations switch hides the home feed for free.

What does turning off Activity in Refine your recommendations do?

Pinterest describes it as turning off recommendations based on Pins you have looked at. Recommendations from your saves, boards and searches are not described as stopping.

How do I see fewer Pins like one on Pinterest?

Tap the ellipsis icon on the Pin in your home feed and choose See less (See fewer like this on the web).

Can I use Pinterest just for my saved Pins?

In minulo, yes. Keep Recommendations on, and open Saved and Messages from the minulo home screen. You can hide the search box too.

Does minulo change the official Pinterest app?

No. The home feed is hidden only inside minulo. To stay out of the official app, lock it in the Block tab, free for up to 2 hours per block.
