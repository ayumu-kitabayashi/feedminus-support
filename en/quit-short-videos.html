How to Quit Short Videos Without Deleting the Apps (YouTube, Instagram, TikTok on iPhone, 2026)

To quit short videos without deleting the apps, open YouTube, Instagram and TikTok in minulo, where Shorts, Reels and For You stay hidden while search, DMs and the people you follow keep working (hiding is free). This page covers all three short-video feeds together.

Short videos show up on the first screen when you open an app and after a video ends. They appear even when you didn't go looking for them, which is why they are hard to quit. Deleting the apps stops them, but you also lose YouTube for looking things up, Instagram DMs and TikTok messages. Here is what each app's official settings can do, and how to remove only the short videos.

Set YouTube Shorts to zero minutes (official setting)

  1. Sign in to the YouTube app.
  2. Tap the You tab, then Settings.
  3. Tap Time management, then Shorts feed limit.
  4. Choose a time from the list. Zero is one of the options.

According to YouTube Help, when you reach the limit a reminder appears in the Shorts feed and you can dismiss it or ignore the limit. It reminds you rather than stopping you. More on this in YouTube Shorts Feed Limit: How to Set It to 0.

Instagram and TikTok can't remove them with official settings

We could not find a setting in Instagram that turns off the Reels tab (How to turn off Instagram Reels). TikTok added a way to refresh the For You feed in March 2023. TikTok's announcement says a refresh lets you see For You content as if you had just signed up, and recommendations build up again from there. Settings you've already turned on and the accounts you follow are not affected. The For You feed itself stays (How to Turn Off the For You Page on TikTok).

Hide only the short videos with minulo

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. Open Instagram, YouTube or TikTok inside minulo and log in on each service's own login page. minulo does not receive your password.
  3. Tap the gear next to each service and turn on hiding: Reels and the Explore tab (and suggested posts) for Instagram, Shorts for YouTube, For You for TikTok. Hiding is free.
  4. In the Block tab, choose the official Instagram, YouTube or TikTok app and start a block. Tapping its icon shows a Blocked screen with an Open in minulo button. Free blocks last up to 2 hours each.

You keep search and normal videos on YouTube, DMs and posts from people you follow on Instagram, and messages on TikTok. Hiding stays on until you turn it off.

Can I quit short videos without deleting the apps?

You can make it much easier. YouTube lets you set the Shorts feed limit to zero, and in minulo YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels and TikTok For You stay hidden while search and DMs keep working (hiding is free).

If I set the YouTube Shorts feed limit to zero, can I still watch Shorts?

Yes. According to YouTube Help, when you reach the limit a reminder appears and you can dismiss it or ignore the limit.

Can I turn off Reels in Instagram?

We could not find a setting in the Instagram app that turns off the Reels tab. If you open Instagram inside minulo, Reels and the Explore tab are hidden and DMs keep working.

Can I remove TikTok For You?

TikTok lets you refresh For You, but the feed itself stays. If you open TikTok inside minulo, For You is hidden and messages keep working.
