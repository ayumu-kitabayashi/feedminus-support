Is there a free app to use Instagram for DMs only? (iPhone, 2026)

Yes, in minulo, the Every tab but DMs setting for Instagram is free: Reels, Explore, the home feed and suggested posts are hidden, and only DMs and the notifications tab stay.

Hiding stays free. Pro (optional) adds Sleep mode, which blocks social apps from your bedtime until morning, Work mode, which locks the apps you pick during your work hours on the days you choose, plus Hard Mode, a daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.

Some other apps also have a free messages-only view: Fluid Friction's DMs Only (Instagram, iPhone) and LoomWeb's Limit to DMs only are free. Messages Only in UNDOOMED is Pro (per its website), DMs only is Pro in Stillfeed and Premium in Reelmute, Intent puts Messages in Intent Pro, and Konvo needs a paid subscription to use Instagram DMs. ScrollGuard has a DMs only mode for Instagram and a Messages only mode for TikTok, but its listing doesn't say whether they are free (checked 2026-11-10). Instagram itself has no messages-only mode.

Set it up in minulo

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. Open Instagram inside minulo and sign in on Instagram's own page. minulo does not receive or store your password.
  3. Tap the gear next to Instagram and turn on Every tab but DMs.
  4. Optional: let minulo lock the official Instagram app with Screen Time. Tapping the usual icon then shows a block screen with an Open in minulo button that leads to your DMs. Locking is free for up to 2 hours per block.

What works and what doesn't

DMs and group chats work, since group chats are part of Instagram DMs, and so does the notifications tab. Posting Stories, voice messages and calls don't, because Instagram's website doesn't have them. When you need one of those, Take a break opens the official app for the minutes you choose.

Notifications come from the official Instagram app. If you leave it unblocked, they arrive as usual and you can reply in minulo. While minulo blocks it, they don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own.

What the free DMs-only setup includes, and what it doesn't do

Of the 9 apps in the table above that have a messages-only view, 3 offer it for free (minulo, Fluid Friction and LoomWeb), 5 charge for it, and ScrollGuard doesn't say. In minulo, the free part covers everything you need for DMs only:

  • Every tab but DMs on Instagram and TikTok, with no time limit and no minulo account.
  • Locking the official Instagram app, up to 2 hours per block. When a block ends, you can start the next one right away.
  • Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode, Work mode, longer blocks, Hard Mode and a Daily limit. None of these is needed for DMs only.

Things people expect that this view doesn't do:

  • Show friends' Stories. Only DMs and the notifications tab stay. To keep Stories, turn on Reels and Explore tabs and Suggested posts instead of Every tab but DMs.
  • Play a Reel a friend sends straight away. It pauses before the vertical feed starts; Open for 5 min lets you watch it for five minutes.
  • Change the official app. Opening Instagram from its own icon still shows Reels, which is why the lock exists. You don't need to delete the official app.

Is there a free app to use Instagram only for DMs?

Yes. minulo's Every tab but DMs setting is free. It hides Reels, Explore, the home feed and suggested posts, and leaves DMs and the notifications tab.

Do other apps charge for DMs only?

Some do, some don't. Fluid Friction and LoomWeb have a free DMs-only view for Instagram. Do group chats still work?

Yes. Group chats are part of Instagram DMs and keep working in the DMs-only view.

Can I make voice or video calls?

Not inside minulo, because Instagram's website doesn't have calls or voice messages. Take a break opens the official app for the minutes you choose.

Will I still get message notifications?

Only while the official Instagram app is not blocked. Notifications come from the official app, and they don't arrive while minulo blocks it.
