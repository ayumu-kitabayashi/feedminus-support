Is there a Spotlight time limit in Snapchat?

We could not find one. Snapchat's help lists what Family Center can do: see who a teen chatted with recently, see their friends, limit sensitive content, stop My AI from replying, request their location, set Place Alerts and see key privacy settings. A time limit is not on that list, and we found no Spotlight timer in Snapchat's settings help either.

See a teen's Spotlight time in Family Center

  1. On the Camera screen, tap your Bitmoji or Story icon at the top to open My Account.
  2. Scroll to Family Center under My Friends and tap it. (You can also type "family" in search, or go to Settings › App & Privacy › Family Center.)
  3. Open your teen's Time Spent.

Snapchat says Time Spent shows a daily average for the last seven days plus a bar for each day; tap a day to see its total. Time is grouped into Messaging, Camera, Spotlight & Stories, and Map, so Spotlight is not shown on its own. The data can take a few hours to appear and follows the device's time zone.

Family Center works only between a parent or caregiver aged 18 or over and a teen aged 13 to 17, the teen has to accept the invitation, and Snapchat says it is still rolling out by country.

Limit Snapchat with iPhone Screen Time

Screen Time sees Snapchat as one app. On iOS 27, go to Settings › Screen Time › Time Allowances and set time for a category such as Social Media. On iOS 26, use App Limits. Either way the limit covers chats, the camera and Stories as well as Spotlight.

Snapchat also says Spotlight can be watched on the web. Apple says a Time Allowance applies to a website the app declares as its own; we have not checked whether Snapchat declares its website, so test it before relying on it.

Can you set a time limit on Snapchat Spotlight?

Not as far as Snapchat's help shows in October 2026. There is no Spotlight timer for yourself, and Family Center shows a teen's time without capping it. iPhone Screen Time can limit the whole Snapchat app, chats included.

Can parents see how much time a teen spends on Spotlight?

Partly. Family Center's Time Spent groups time into Messaging, Camera, Spotlight & Stories, and Map, with a daily average for the last seven days. Spotlight is counted together with Stories.

Can Family Center block Spotlight?

No. Hide Sensitive Content in Family Center limits sensitive or suggestive content in places like Stories and Spotlight, but Spotlight stays in the app.

Can Screen Time limit only Spotlight?

No. Screen Time limits the Snapchat app as a whole, so chats stop at the limit too. To keep chats without Spotlight, open Snapchat in an app that hides it, such as minulo.

Who can use Snapchat Family Center?

Snapchat says parents and caregivers must be 18 or older and teens 13 to 17. The teen has to accept an invitation, and Family Center may not be available in every country yet.
