How to turn off Snapchat notifications you don't want

  1. In Snapchat, tap your profile icon at the top.
  2. Tap ⚙️ to open Settings.
  3. Tap Notifications.
  4. Turn off the toggle for each notification type you don't want.

To choose whose Stories you hear about, tap Manage Story Notifications on the same screen and pick the people you still want. You can also do it per friend: swipe right to the Chat screen, press and hold a chat, tap Story Settings and turn Story Notifications off. If the alerts are about comments on Snaps you posted to Spotlight, turn off Comments in Notifications.

To silence Snapchat completely, open the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, tap Snapchat and turn off Allow Notifications. That stops chat notifications as well.

What doesn't remove Spotlight

  • Hiding or skipping Snaps. Snapchat says it uses hiding, skipping and reporting as signs you don't enjoy something, and shows less like it. Spotlight still fills with other Snaps.
  • Turning off personalization. In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Snapchat lets you turn off personalized recommendations in Spotlight and Discover or reset suggested content. Snapchat says you will still see content, only less tailored to you.
  • Hide Sensitive Content in Family Center. A parent can turn this on for a teen. It limits sensitive or suggestive content in Stories and Spotlight, but Spotlight stays.
  • Changing the Home Tab. With Snapchat+ you can open the app on a different tab, but the Spotlight button is still at the bottom.

How do I turn off Spotlight notifications on Snapchat?

Tap your profile icon, tap the gear to open Settings, tap Notifications, and turn off each notification type you don't want. If the alerts are about comments on your own Spotlight Snaps, turn off Comments in the same Notifications screen. To stop every Snapchat notification, go to the iPhone Settings app, tap Notifications, tap Snapchat and turn off Allow Notifications.

Can I make Snapchat open on a tab other than Spotlight?

Only with Snapchat+. Subscribers can open My Account, tap their Snapchat+ membership card, tap Home Tab and choose the tab the app opens on. Spotlight is still one tap away in the tab bar.

Does hiding Snaps on Spotlight make it go away?

No. Snapchat says it treats hiding, skipping and reporting as signs that you don't enjoy that content and ranks it lower, but Spotlight keeps showing other Snaps. It works one Snap at a time.
