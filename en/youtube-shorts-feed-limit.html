How to set the Shorts feed limit

  1. Sign in to the YouTube app.
  2. Tap the You tab, then Settings.
  3. Tap Time management.
  4. Tap Shorts feed limit. A list of time limits appears, including the option to set it to zero.
  5. Select your time limit.

When you reach the limit in the Shorts feed, YouTube shows a reminder message. In the words of YouTube's help, you are then "prompted with an option to dismiss or ignore your set limit."

What YouTube's help page doesn't say

The help page only talks about the Shorts feed. It doesn't say whether the limit also applies to Shorts on Home, in search results or on channel pages, and it doesn't list the time options beyond saying that zero is one of them.

How do I set a time limit on YouTube Shorts?

In the YouTube app, tap the You tab, then Settings, then Time management, then Shorts feed limit, and pick a time from the list. YouTube's help page gives these steps for the YouTube app.

Can you set the YouTube Shorts limit to 0?

Yes. YouTube's help says the list of time limits includes the option to set your Shorts feed limit to zero.

Why is my YouTube Shorts limit not working?

Reaching the limit doesn't stop you from watching. YouTube shows a reminder and, according to its help page, gives you an option to dismiss or ignore your set limit. If you ignore it, the Shorts feed keeps going. To hide Shorts with no button to tap past, you can watch YouTube in minulo with Shorts turned off and block the official app with Screen Time. Both are free.

Does the Shorts feed limit work on youtube.com or on a computer?

YouTube's help page only gives steps for the YouTube app and doesn't describe the setting for youtube.com in a browser. On iPhone, minulo opens YouTube's website in its own view with Shorts hidden.

Can parents set a YouTube Shorts limit for their teen?

Yes, on a supervised teen account. In January 2026 YouTube said parents can set how much time a teen spends scrolling Shorts, and that an option to set the timer to zero was coming soon. YouTube's example was zero during homework and 60 minutes on a long car trip.
