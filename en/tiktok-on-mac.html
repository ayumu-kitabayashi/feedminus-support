How to use TikTok on a Mac

  1. Open Safari or Chrome on your Mac and go to www.tiktok.com.
  2. Log in with the same account you use in the TikTok app on your phone.
  3. To limit your time, click your profile picture, then Settings, Screen time and Daily screen time. TikTok says this limit applies to both the app and the web.

Steps with minulo on iPhone

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. Open TikTok inside minulo and log in on TikTok's own login page. minulo does not receive or store your password.
  3. Tap the gear next to TikTok and turn on Recommendations to hide For You, or Every tab but DMs to keep only your messages.
  4. Optional: in the Block tab, lock the official TikTok app with Screen Time. The block screen has an Open in minulo button.

Is there a TikTok app for Mac?

Not on the App Store. When we checked on 2026-11-04, TikTok's US App Store listing named iPhone, iPad, iPod touch and Apple Vision under Compatibility, and no Mac. On a Mac, go to www.tiktok.com in your browser and log in with the same account you use on your phone.

Can I watch TikTok in Safari or Chrome on a Mac?

Yes. TikTok calls tiktok.com its web app. In February 2025 TikTok said the web app has a For You feed, an Explore tab and full-screen LIVE, plus a Floating Player in Google Chrome.

Can I set a TikTok time limit on a Mac?

Yes. On the web, click your profile picture, then Settings, Screen time and Daily screen time. TikTok says the setting applies to both the app and the web.
