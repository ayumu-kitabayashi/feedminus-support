Why Reels keep coming after Not interested

Instagram's help says Not interested lets you see fewer posts like the one you marked. It does not remove Reels, and it works on one reel at a time. The Reels tab is built to recommend. Meta's system card for the Reels tab says the system collects reels from people you follow and reels similar to ones you have recently engaged with.

The same card lists some of what the system predicts for each reel: how likely you are to comment on it, reshare it, follow its author, or watch less than three seconds. So what you watch and do keeps shaping what comes next, alongside the reels you mark. We could not find in Meta's pages how much one Not interested tap weighs against the rest.

What actually reduces them

  1. Keep tapping Not interested on the kinds of reels you don't want: tap Options on the reel, then Not interested. In Explore you can also tap and hold a post.
  2. Add words. In the same menu tap Don't suggest posts with certain words, enter a word, phrase or emoji, and tap Done. It checks the caption and hashtags of suggested posts, including reels. It does not hide posts from accounts you follow.
  3. Tune your algorithm if you have it: from Reels, Explore, or Settings › Content preferences, add topics under what you want to see less of. Instagram's help says this is still being tested and depends on region.
  4. Reset suggested content if your Reels are full of one thing: Profile › Menu › Content preferences (under What you see) › Reset suggested content. It cannot be undone, and it does not change who you follow or delete your data.

All of these change which Reels you see. None of them changes how many. We could not find a setting in Instagram's help that turns the Reels tab off.

Why do I keep seeing Reels after tapping Not interested?

Not interested shows you fewer posts like the one you marked. It does not remove Reels. The Reels tab recommends reels from people you follow and reels similar to ones you recently engaged with, so what you watch keeps shaping it too.

Does Not interested work on Instagram Reels?

Yes. Instagram's help says you can mark a post as not interested in your feed, Reels, Explore or Search, and you will see fewer posts like it. It works one post at a time.

How do I stop seeing a certain kind of Reels?

Tap Options on a reel, then Not interested, then Don't suggest posts with certain words, and add the word, phrase or emoji. Suggested posts and reels with it in the caption or hashtags are hidden. Posts from accounts you follow are not.

Does Snooze stop suggested Reels?

Instagram's help says Snooze pauses all suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days. It does not say the Reels tab, and we could not confirm that it changes it.

Can I turn Reels off completely?

We could not find one in Instagram's help as of October 2026. To hide the Reels tab and Reels in the feed, open Instagram in an app that hides them. In minulo this is free and DMs keep working.
