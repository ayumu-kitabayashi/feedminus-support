minulo vs ScrollGuard: hiding Reels and Shorts on iPhone while keeping DMs (2026)

If you want Reels, Shorts and TikTok's For You hidden while your DMs keep working, both apps do it and both are free for the basics. minulo opens 10 networks inside its own app with those parts hidden, and hiding is free; Pro, for Sleep mode, Work mode, Hard Mode, a Daily limit and longer blocks, is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US. ScrollGuard works on iPhone inside its own app and through a Safari extension, covers 7 networks there, and also has an Android version that works inside the native apps.

ScrollGuard, made by BreakTheScroll OÜ, doesn't block whole apps. On iPhone it hides the addictive parts of social media websites: its App Store page describes a Safari extension with a switch for each part, and its website says it also creates clean web app versions of Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Facebook, X, Reddit and LinkedIn. Its website says the Android version runs on top of the native apps, and that blocking happens on your device. Its latest version, 1.2.5, adds Snapchat chats and calls inside ScrollGuard in beta, with a filter that hides Spotlight.

Pick ScrollGuard if

  • you want filtering in Safari as well as inside its own app;
  • you also use an Android phone, where it works inside the native apps;
  • you want Strict Mode, which locks your settings with a password you can give to a friend, or its adult content filter;
  • you prefer an app with more ratings behind it (129 in the US on 2026-11-11).

Pick minulo if

  • you need Threads or Pinterest without the feed;
  • you want all your networks in one app, with the official apps locked so you open them in minulo instead;
  • you want a DMs-only setting that is stated to be free (ScrollGuard has DMs only and Messages only modes, but doesn't say whether they are free);
  • you use a Mac.

Which is better for hiding Reels and Shorts but keeping DMs, minulo or ScrollGuard?

Both keep DMs and both hide Reels and Shorts without paying. Pick ScrollGuard if you want to stay in Safari, also use Android, or want its Strict Mode or adult content filter. Pick minulo if you want one app of its own for 10 networks, including Threads, Pinterest and Snapchat, and want the official apps locked so you open them in minulo instead.

Is there a free way to block YouTube Shorts on iPhone?

Yes, with either app. ScrollGuard's website says its core blocking is free, and on iPhone it hides Shorts in Safari and in the YouTube web app it creates. In minulo, hiding the Shorts shelf and the Shorts tab is free, and Shorts links can open in the normal player. Neither app changes the official YouTube app; minulo can lock it with Screen Time instead.

Is ScrollGuard free?

It is a free download, and its website says the core blocking works without paying. The US App Store lists in-app purchases named ScrollGuard Pro at $3.99, $19.99, $29.99 and $59.99 without saying what each one covers, and the listing says Premium adds unlimited custom filters (checked 2026-11-11). In minulo, hiding is free, and Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year.
