What is a good one sec alternative if I only want to stop Reels and Shorts?

Use an app that hides those parts instead of pausing the whole app. minulo, SocialLite and UNDOOMED show social networks in their own view with Reels and Shorts hidden, and ScrollGuard does the same in Safari. In minulo, hiding is free on 10 networks, and it can lock the official Instagram and TikTok apps so you land in minulo instead. Of the two iPhone apps called StopScroll, one blocks whole apps with Screen Time and the other filters Reels in a built-in browser; see minulo vs StopScroll. For Freedom, which blocks apps and websites in sessions, see Freedom app alternatives for iPhone that keep DMs.

Does one sec block Instagram Reels?

No, not on their own. one sec works on whole apps: when you open Instagram it shows a pause first, and after that Instagram opens normally, Reels included. Its App Store description does not mention hiding Reels or Shorts. It can send you out after a set time, which it calls Re-Intervention.

Is one sec free?

Using one sec with one app is free. For more apps you need one sec pro, which comes as a monthly, yearly or lifetime purchase. minulo is free for hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts and for locking the official apps for up to 2 hours at a time.

What does one sec do better than an app that hides Reels?

one sec works with any app, not only social media, and has a Safari extension, an Android app and a browser extension for computers, according to its website. It points to peer-reviewed studies and reports that app usage drops by 57% on average. It also has 23,786 US ratings. If your problem is opening apps on autopilot, a pause can be the better fit; if your problem is Reels and Shorts in particular, hiding them lets you keep using DMs without the pause. ClearSpace also makes you pause before opening an app, and Intent asks you to pick a purpose (messages, search or timed browsing) before each visit; see minulo vs ClearSpace and minulo vs Intent.
