one sec alternatives if you want Reels and Shorts gone, not just delayed (2026)

one sec adds a pause, such as a deep breath, before a distracting app opens, and it is free for one app. After the pause the whole app opens, Reels and Shorts included. If you want to keep using Instagram or YouTube without Reels and Shorts, an app that hides only those parts fits better: minulo, SocialLite and UNDOOMED do it in their own view and keep DMs, and ScrollGuard does it in Safari. If you want stronger limits on whole apps, ScreenZen and Opal are the large options.

What is a good one sec alternative if I only want to stop Reels and Shorts?

Use an app that hides those parts instead of pausing the whole app. minulo, SocialLite and UNDOOMED show social networks in their own view with Reels and Shorts hidden, and ScrollGuard does the same in Safari. In minulo, hiding is free on 10 networks, and it can lock the official Instagram and TikTok apps so you land in minulo instead.

Does one sec block Instagram Reels?

No, not on their own. one sec works on whole apps: when you open Instagram it shows a pause first, and after that Instagram opens normally, Reels included. Its App Store description does not mention hiding Reels or Shorts. It can send you out after a set time, which it calls Re-Intervention.

Is one sec free?

Using one sec with one app is free. For more apps you need one sec pro, which comes as a monthly, yearly or lifetime purchase. minulo is free for hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts and for locking the official apps for up to 2 hours at a time.

What does one sec do better than an app that hides Reels?

one sec works with any app, not only social media, and has a Safari extension, an Android app and a browser extension for computers, according to its website. It points to peer-reviewed studies and reports that app usage drops by 57% on average. It also has 23,716 US ratings. If your problem is opening apps on autopilot, a pause can be the better fit; if your problem is Reels and Shorts in particular, hiding them lets you keep using DMs without the pause.
