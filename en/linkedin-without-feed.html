How to use LinkedIn without the feed: just jobs and messages (iPhone, 2026)

What LinkedIn's own settings do

LinkedIn Help's page on customizing your feed lists these options: unfollow or mute people and companies whose posts you don't want to see, choose I don't want to see this on a post, report inappropriate posts, follow new people and companies, and, on desktop only, choose whether top or recent updates come first.

All of these change what is in the feed. None of them removes it. When you open LinkedIn to answer a recruiter or check a job alert, the feed is still the first thing you see.

Hide the feed with minulo

minulo opens LinkedIn's own website inside the app and hides the items you turn on. News feed is one of them, next to Video and suggested posts. Messages, Notifications and Jobs open straight from the minulo home screen, and everything you haven't hidden works like the LinkedIn website. Hiding is free with no time limit, and you can turn the feed back on any time.

Only your screen changes. Your profile, your connections and who sees your posts stay the same.

If you also want to stay out of the app

In minulo's Block tab, allow Screen Time and choose the official LinkedIn app. Its icon then shows a blocked screen with Open in minulo. Blocking is free for up to 2 hours per block. Keep in mind that the official app's notifications don't arrive while it is blocked, so if you are waiting on a recruiter, check Messages in minulo.
