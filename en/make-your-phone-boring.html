How to make your iPhone boring without switching to a dumb phone (2026)

Turn on grayscale, move social apps off the Home Screen, batch notifications and remove the feeds; minulo hides Reels, Shorts and TikTok For You while DMs, maps and the camera stay. Hiding is free.

A dumb phone makes scrolling boring by removing everything, including the group chat, maps and your banking app. You can get most of the effect on an iPhone with built-in settings, and keep the parts you need. The settings below come from Apple's iPhone User Guide for iOS 26.

1. Turn on grayscale

Go to Settings › Accessibility › Display & Text Size › Color Filters, turn on Color Filters and choose Grayscale. Apple notes that Night Shift removes color filter effects except grayscale, so it stays gray at night. More detail, including a triple-click shortcut: does grayscale reduce scrolling.

2. Empty the Home Screen

Touch and hold a social app, tap Remove App, then Remove from Home Screen. The app stays in App Library and still works. To push it further away, touch and hold it, tap Require Face ID, then Hide and Require Face ID. It moves to the Hidden folder at the bottom of App Library. Only apps you downloaded from the App Store can be hidden.

3. Make notifications boring

Go to Settings › Notifications › Scheduled Summary, turn it on, set the times and choose which apps go in it. For apps you never need to hear from, tap the app under Notifications and turn off Allow Notifications. For Instagram, see how to get notifications only for messages.

4. Lock the apps at set hours

On iOS 26, schedule Downtime under Settings › Screen Time, set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime. During downtime only calls, messages and apps you allow are available, and blocked apps appear dimmed with an hourglass. Without Block at Downtime, Apple shows a reminder and lets you continue. For office hours, see how to stop scrolling your phone at work. The night version is in how to lock Instagram, TikTok and YouTube automatically at night.

5. Remove the feeds, keep the DMs (minulo)

  1. Turn on grayscale. Settings, Accessibility, Display & Text Size, Color Filters, then Grayscale.
  2. Empty the Home Screen. Touch and hold each social app, tap Remove App and Remove from Home Screen, or hide it with Require Face ID.
  3. Batch notifications. Turn on Scheduled Summary under Settings and Notifications.
  4. Lock apps at set hours. Schedule Downtime, set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime.
  5. Hide Reels and Shorts in minulo. Install minulo, sign in to Instagram, YouTube or TikTok inside it, and turn off Reels, Explore, suggested posts, Shorts and For You. DMs keep working. Hiding is free.

How do I make my iPhone less addictive?

Turn on grayscale, remove social apps from the Home Screen, batch notifications with Scheduled Summary, and block apps at set hours with Downtime and Block at Downtime. minulo hides Reels and Shorts while DMs keep working.

Is there an app that makes your phone boring?

Most of it is built into iPhone settings. For the feeds themselves, minulo hides Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok For You inside its own view, and hiding is free.

Should I get a dumb phone instead?

A phone without apps also removes group chats that live in Instagram or other apps, maps and banking. If the problem is the feeds, removing the feeds on your iPhone keeps the rest.

Can I hide Instagram from my Home Screen?

Yes. Touch and hold it, tap Remove App, then Remove from Home Screen. Or tap Require Face ID, then Hide and Require Face ID to move it to the Hidden folder in App Library.

Is minulo free?

Downloading minulo and hiding Reels and Shorts are free. Locking the official apps is free for up to 2 hours per block. minulo Pro adds Sleep mode, Work mode, Hard Mode and blocks longer than 2 hours.
