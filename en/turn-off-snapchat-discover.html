What Snapchat lets you do

Hide one item: on the Stories screen, press and hold a Story or Show tile and tap Hide this Content. To bring it back, tap the three dots at the top of the Stories screen, tap View Hidden Stories and tap Un-Hide. Snapchat adds: "Not all Stories content can be hidden, and you can't completely disable a certain topic, theme, or the Discover section."

Why Discover keeps finding things for you: Snapchat says it ranks Discover using your viewing history, how long you watch, whether you favorite or share, and whether you subscribe. Skipping, reporting and hiding count as signs you don't enjoy something.

Turning off personalization (EEA, UK and Switzerland)

If you live in the European Economic Area, the UK or Switzerland, Snapchat lets you opt out of personalization on public content feeds: tap ... on a piece of content, then Why am I seeing this content?, or go to Settings and Personalization Controls. Snapchat says you will still see content, only more random and less relevant to your interests. Reset Suggested Content resets most signals, but not all.

So even there, Discover stays. It just stops being tailored to you.
