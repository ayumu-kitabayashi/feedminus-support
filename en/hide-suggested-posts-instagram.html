How to snooze suggested posts for 30 days

  1. In your Instagram Feed, find a suggested post and tap Options (the three dots) above it.
  2. Tap Not interested.
  3. Choose Snooze to pause all suggested posts in your Feed for 30 days.

On the same screen you can also choose Don't suggest posts from an account, Don't suggest posts with certain words, or Manage content preferences. Instagram's help does not describe a way to snooze for longer than 30 days, so you need to do this again when it runs out.

How to switch to the Following feed

Tap the Instagram logo at the top of your Feed and choose Following. Meta says this view shows posts only from accounts you follow, newest first, with no suggested posts, going back 30 days. Favorites in the same menu shows only the accounts you add to your Favorites list.

How do I snooze suggested posts on Instagram for 30 days?

Tap Options (the three dots) above a suggested post in your Feed, tap Not interested, then choose Snooze. All suggested posts in your Feed pause for 30 days.

Does the Following feed get rid of suggested posts?

Yes, while you are in it. Tap the Instagram logo and choose Following to see only posts from accounts you follow, newest first, going back 30 days. The Reels tab and Explore are still there.

Does resetting suggested content stop suggested posts?

No. Reset suggested content clears what Instagram has learned about you across Explore, Reels and Feed, but suggested posts keep appearing and become personalized again as you use the app. It cannot be undone.
