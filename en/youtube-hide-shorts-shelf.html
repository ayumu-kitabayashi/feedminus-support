Hide the Shorts shelf with the X

  1. Open YouTube and go to Home.
  2. Scroll to the Shorts shelf.
  3. If there is an X at the top right of the shelf, tap it. According to the reports, a message says the shelf will be hidden for 30 days.
  4. No X? Tap More at the top of the Shorts grid and choose Show fewer Shorts.

GeekChamp says whether the X appears depends on your account, region and app version, and tbreak says it works on the mobile website (m.youtube.com in Safari), not in the official apps. We couldn't confirm either in YouTube Help, so check your own Home screen.

How long does it stay hidden?

Every report we found says 30 days, after which the shelf comes back and you tap the X again (SlashGear, GeekChamp). YouTube's help gives no period for the X, and none for Show fewer Shorts or Not interested either; it describes those two as feedback that tunes your recommendations. To undo Show fewer Shorts, go to My Activity, select Other activity, and under Show fewer Shorts select Delete.

Where Shorts stay after you hide the shelf

The X only touches the shelf on Home. YouTube's help lists where viewers find Shorts: the Shorts tab at the bottom of the app, search results, the YouTube homepage, channel pages, Subscriptions and notifications. GeekChamp adds that Shorts still open from links people send you.
