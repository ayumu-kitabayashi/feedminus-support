What the studies found

In the Spanish study, published in 2020, students averaged 254 minutes of phone screen time on weekdays and 243 minutes on weekends over one week. Weekday use was 266 minutes for men and 238 minutes for women. The study counted total screen time and did not break it down by app.

The Chinese study, published in JMIR Mental Health in 2025, collected screenshots from 16,668 students between 2022 and 2024. The average was 48.8 hours a week, which works out to about 7 hours a day, and 271.6 unlocks a week. The two studies differ in country, year and method, so treat the range of roughly 4 to 7 hours as a rough picture, not a US figure.

How to see your own number on iPhone

On iOS 27, go to Settings › Screen Time › Daily Average. Tap Week for the weekly summary or Day for one day, and scroll down for Pickups and Notifications. Swipe left and right to see earlier weeks. If you also use an iPad or Mac with the same Apple Account, turn on Share Across Devices under Manage Screen Time to see the combined total. What to do with the number

The total mixes lecture slides, texts with classmates and Reels. Look at the most used apps first. If Instagram, TikTok or YouTube are near the top, the part worth cutting is usually the feed, not the messages. Setting a daily cap on the whole app also cuts the group chat, which is why many students give up on limits after a few days.

What is the average screen time for college students?

Two studies that measured it from students' phones found about 4 hours 14 minutes a day on weekdays (306 students in Spain, 2020) and about 7 hours a day (16,668 students in China, 2022 to 2024). We didn't find a comparable large US study.

Is 7 hours of screen time a day normal for a college student?

It was the average in the 2025 study of 16,668 Chinese college students. Whether it is too much for you depends on what the hours are spent on, because class work and messages count in the same total as Reels.

Is there a recommended screen time for college students?

We didn't find an official daily limit for adults from Apple or a public health agency. A practical target is the time you spend in feeds you didn't mean to open, which Screen Time's most used apps list shows.

How do I check my screen time on iPhone?

On iOS 27, go to Settings, Screen Time and Daily Average. Tap Week or Day, and scroll down to see Pickups and Notifications.

Does Screen Time count my iPad and Mac too?

On iOS 27, turn on Share Across Devices under Manage Screen Time. Your iPhone, iPad and Mac signed in to the same Apple Account then share usage data and limits.
