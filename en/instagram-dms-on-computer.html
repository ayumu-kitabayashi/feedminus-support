Use Instagram DMs on a computer

  1. Open instagram.com in a browser on your computer and log in.
  2. Click Messages on the left.
  3. To start a new chat, click Compose at the top left, choose the people, then click Chat.
  4. Type your message and click Send.

These click steps come from About Instagram, Instagram's official site. You can also go to someone's profile and click Message. Messages to people who don't follow you are sent as message requests. Does the computer website show Reels?

Yes. Instagram's website has the Reels and Explore tabs too, which is why apps that open the website, such as minulo, have switches to hide them. Using Instagram on a computer moves you away from the phone, but the feed and Reels are still a click away from your messages.

Read a DM on iPhone without opening the app

  1. Open Settings and tap Notifications.
  2. Below Notification Style, tap Instagram.
  3. Tap Show Previews (if it appears) and choose Always or When Unlocked.

Apple says previews can include things like the text of messages, and that not all apps support previews. Whether Instagram puts the message text in a DM alert, we couldn't confirm in Instagram Help. Tapping the alert opens the chat in the Instagram app, feed and Reels included.

Two more shortcuts are on opening Instagram straight to your DMs: touching and holding a notification for quick actions, and the Instagram icon's menu. To make every alert a DM, turn off the other kinds in Instagram, as described on Instagram notifications for messages only.
