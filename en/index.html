Frequently asked

How far does the free version go?
Hiding Reels, short-form video, recommended posts and ads is free. There is no limit on how many services you add, and locking the original apps is free too. Three things are paid: switching each rule one by one, days and hours and sleep, and Lock Mode.
Where do my login details go?
You sign in to each service on that service’s own login page. minulo never obtains or stores your ID or password. The contents of your DMs, your browsing, and your usage records all stay on your device.
Will it restrict sites in my browser?
No. We only touch pages opened inside minulo. iOS does offer a way for an app to restrict websites across every browser; minulo does not use it, and an automated check during development confirms it is never called.
Will it slow things down?
minulo opens each service’s website inside the app, so it is one beat slower than the original. Measured, it takes 1.8–2.4 seconds from opening to Instagram’s sign-in screen. After that it loads at normal speed.
Will I be unable to delete the app?
You can delete minulo itself at any time. Lock Mode takes three steps to leave, but the app is not designed to stand in the way of deleting it. It puts time before an impulsive unlock; it does not shut you in.
My service is not supported
Ten services are supported today (Instagram, YouTube, X, Threads, Pinterest, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, LinkedIn, Snapchat). When a service changes its screens, something that used to be hidden can come back. The rules are checked automatically every week — and you can tell us through support inside the app.
