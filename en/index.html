You do not want to quit social media. You cannot — the work, the messages and the audience are there.
What you want gone is only what arrives on its own.

What goes / What stays

What goes
  • Reels, Shorts and recommended video
  • Algorithmic “suggested” posts
  • Explore and discovery tabs
  • Ads, and posts shaped like ads
  • Stories (a switch you set)
What stays
  • Direct messages and conversations
  • Profiles you go to on purpose
  • Posts friends send you
  • Posting, and replying to your own posts
  • Search

Remove the feed. Then stop the app itself.

Choose what disappears

Reels, short-form video, the Explore tab and “suggested” posts are gone before the page finishes loading.

Stories, ads, saved posts, DMs-only and grayscale are switches you set for each service.

It stops the real apps too

Open a blocked app from your Home Screen and FeedMinus opens instead.

This needs Screen Time access on your iPhone.

Lock Mode

Four steps stand between you and turning blocking off. It takes 5–10 minutes.

Copy out a passage. Trace a shape. Answer arithmetic. Wait one more minute.

The point is not difficulty. It is distance between the impulse and the act.

Post Mode

Unlock one app for five minutes, publish, and get out. For people who post for a living.

Set the hours

Evenings only, weekdays only — blocking turns itself on when you said it should.

Records

Only what you used today. Nothing is compared with last week.
