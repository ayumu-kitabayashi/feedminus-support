How to take a social media break while traveling and keep maps and messages (iPhone, 2026)

To take a break from social media on a trip but keep maps and messages, download offline maps, keep the feed apps on Wi-Fi only, and use Downtime with your messaging and map apps in Always Allowed.

All three are built into iPhone. Apple Maps can give directions from an offline map without Wi-Fi or cellular service, turning off cellular data for single apps makes them work only on Wi-Fi, and Downtime (called Screen Time Schedule in iOS 27) leaves only calls, messages and the apps you allow.

Before you leave: download offline maps

  1. Open the Maps app.
  2. Tap your picture or initials next to the search field, tap Offline Maps, then Add Offline Map, and enter the place or tap Current Location.
  3. Adjust the area and tap Download.

Apple says that with an offline map you can still see place details such as hours and ratings, get turn-by-turn directions and see your arrival time, even without Wi-Fi or cellular service. Offline maps aren't available in all countries or regions, so check before the trip.

Keep the feeds on Wi-Fi only

  1. Open Settings and tap Cellular.
  2. Scroll down to the list of apps and turn off Instagram, TikTok and YouTube.

Apple says that when cellular data is off for an app, iPhone uses only Wi-Fi for it. Out on the street the feeds stop loading, while Maps and your messaging app keep working. Apple also suggests turning off Data Roaming under Cellular Data Options to avoid roaming charges, but that cuts cellular internet for every app abroad, so plan for Wi-Fi, an international plan or a travel eSIM.

Downtime for the trip

  1. In Settings, Screen Time, Always Allowed, add Maps, your messaging apps and anything you need for tickets.
  2. Tap Downtime (called Screen Time Schedule in iOS 27), then Turn On Downtime Until Tomorrow each morning, or turn on Scheduled for the trip's hours.
  3. Set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime if you want the other apps fully blocked. Apple's iOS 27 user guide doesn't list this option.

Apple says that during downtime only calls, messages and the apps you allow are available. For a longer break from the feeds, see a one-week digital detox without going offline.

How do I take a social media break on vacation but keep messaging?

Use Downtime with your messaging and map apps in Always Allowed, which keeps only those available, and turn off cellular data for the feed apps so they load only on Wi-Fi.

Can I use maps without data while traveling?

In Apple Maps, yes: download an offline map before you go. Apple says offline maps aren't available in all countries or regions.

How do I stop Instagram from using data abroad?

Go to Settings, tap Cellular, scroll to the list of apps and turn Instagram off. Apple says the app then uses only Wi-Fi.

Should I turn off Data Roaming?

Apple suggests turning off Data Roaming to avoid roaming charges while traveling. That stops cellular internet for every app outside your carrier's network, so plan for Wi-Fi, an international plan or a travel eSIM.

Will I get messages during Downtime?

Apple says that during downtime only calls, messages and the apps you choose to allow are available. Add the messaging apps you use to Always Allowed.

Does minulo work offline?

No. minulo opens each network's website inside the app, so it needs Wi-Fi or cellular data, the same as the website.
