minulo vs UNDOOMED: two iPhone apps that hide Reels and keep your DMs (2026)

UNDOOMED and minulo solve the same problem the same way: they open your social networks inside their own app with Reels, Shorts and suggested posts filtered out, while messages keep working. The differences are which networks they cover, whether messages-only costs money, and what happens when you tap the official app.

Is UNDOOMED safe?

Its US App Store privacy label (October 2026) says the data it may collect is not linked to your identity: a device ID and product interaction for analytics, and crash data. Apple notes that these labels are provided by the developer and not verified. UNDOOMED shows each network's mobile website in its own private browser, so you sign in on the network's own website inside the app, the same way you do in minulo.

What do UNDOOMED's reviews say?

On the US App Store it has 107 ratings with an average of 4.3 stars (October 2026). That is a small number compared with SocialLite's more than 14,000, so read recent reviews for the networks you care about, since website changes can break filters for a while in any app of this kind.

Is UNDOOMED free?

It is free to download. Its App Store listing says options vary by network and plan and that advanced filters require Pro, and its website lists Messages Only under Pro. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts and the DMs-only setting are free; Pro adds schedules and Hard Mode.

Which one should a student who needs Instagram for group chats use?

Both keep Instagram messages. If you want Instagram to show only messages without paying, minulo's Every tab but DMs setting is free. If you also need Reddit or LinkedIn messages, or you use Android, UNDOOMED fits better.

Which one covers TikTok?

minulo. UNDOOMED lists TikTok and Snapchat as unavailable. In minulo, TikTok's For You feed is hidden and messages keep working; there is also a DMs-only setting for TikTok.

What happens when I tap the real Instagram icon?

With UNDOOMED, the official app stays unchanged, so it opens as usual. minulo can lock the official app with Screen Time; tapping the icon shows a block screen with an Open in minulo button that leads to Instagram without Reels.

Which one is more established?

UNDOOMED has 107 US ratings (average 4.3) and has been on the App Store since October 2025. minulo launched in September 2026 and has no US ratings yet.
