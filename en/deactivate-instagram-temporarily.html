How to deactivate Instagram on iPhone

Instagram is moving accounts to Accounts Center, so you will see one of two layouts. If you see Manage accounts at the bottom of Accounts Center:

  1. Tap Profile or your profile picture at the bottom right.
  2. Tap Menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Accounts Center, then Manage account.
  4. Tap Manage next to the profile you want to deactivate.
  5. Tap Deactivation or deletion, then choose the profile.
  6. Tap Deactivate account, then Continue, and follow the steps to confirm.

If you see Accounts at the bottom instead, go to Accounts Center › Personal details › Account ownership and control › Deactivation or deletion, pick the profile and follow the steps. You need to be able to log in to do this, and you can also do it from a computer or a mobile browser.

What happens while it is deactivated

Instagram's help says your photos, comments and likes are hidden until you reactivate. There is no end date to pick: the account stays deactivated until you log back in. You can deactivate only once a week, so if you log back in on day two, you wait before you can deactivate again.

We couldn't find in Instagram's help what your DM threads look like to other people while you are deactivated, so we don't describe it here.

How to reactivate Instagram

Log back in with your username and password. That is the whole process, and it is also the weak point: if Instagram is still on your phone, one tap on the icon is enough to end the break.

Deactivate, delete or block: which fits?

Deactivation suits a real break from being seen, for example during exams or after a breakup. Deletion is for leaving for good: after 30 days from the request, your profile and information are permanently deleted (the number of days you can cancel varies by region, and Instagram says the whole process can take up to 90 days). If what you actually want is fewer hours on Reels, neither is needed. Screen Time can stop the app, and the account, followers and messages stay untouched.
