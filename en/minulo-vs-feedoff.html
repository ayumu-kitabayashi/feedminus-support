minulo vs FeedOff: which free app to hide Shorts and Reels on iPhone (2026)

Both apps hide YouTube Shorts and Instagram Reels on iPhone while your DMs and normal videos keep working. If you only use Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit and LinkedIn, FeedOff does this completely free, with no in-app purchases. If you also use TikTok, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest or Snapchat, or want the same on a Mac, pick minulo; hiding is free there too, and Pro is paid.

FeedOff: Block Reels & Shorts is an iPhone app by Peter Khalil, released on the App Store on 2026-10-01. It opens Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit and LinkedIn in its own browser with Reels, Shorts, Explore, For You, suggested posts, ads and autoplay hidden, and its Shield can block the real apps, including TikTok, with Screen Time. minulo works the same basic way, showing each network's website inside the app, but covers 10 networks and hides the feed on TikTok too instead of only blocking it.

Pick FeedOff if

  • you only need Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit and LinkedIn;
  • you want every feature free, with no subscription at all;
  • you want widgets in place of the real app icons, or grayscale per app;
  • you want notifications from blocked apps to keep arriving;
  • you want to read the filter rules yourself on GitHub.

Pick minulo if

  • you use TikTok, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest or Snapchat and want their feeds hidden, not the whole app blocked;
  • you want a DMs-only setting for Instagram or TikTok;
  • you use a Mac;
  • you want the app in Japanese, Korean or another language besides English.

Both apps show each network's website rather than the official app, so screens look a little different from the app. While minulo locks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive.

Is FeedOff free?

Yes. Its App Store listing says FeedOff has no subscription, no in-app purchases and no ads, and the US App Store lists it as free (checked 2026-11-04). In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is also free; schedules, blocks longer than 2 hours, Hard Mode and multiple block lists are part of Pro, which is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US.

Can FeedOff or minulo hide YouTube Shorts and keep normal videos?

Both can. FeedOff opens YouTube on your Subscriptions page with the Shorts shelf, Shorts tab, Home recommendations and autoplay hidden, and you can still search and watch any video. minulo shows YouTube's website inside the app with Shorts hidden, and normal videos and search keep working. Neither app changes the official YouTube app; both can block it with Screen Time instead.

Which one should I pick if I use TikTok, Threads or Facebook?

minulo. FeedOff's browser covers Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit and LinkedIn, and for TikTok it can only block the whole app with its Shield. minulo shows TikTok, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest and Snapchat with their feeds hidden, and keeps TikTok messages working. If you only use FeedOff's five networks, FeedOff does the job for free.
