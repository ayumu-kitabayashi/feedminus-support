Why can't an app remove Reels inside Instagram?

iOS keeps each app separate. A third-party app can block or limit another app through Screen Time, but it cannot change what that app shows.

Then how do people keep Instagram without Reels?

They use Instagram's website somewhere Reels are hidden, and make the official app hard to open. minulo does both: it hides Reels in its own view and, with Screen Time, sends taps on the official Instagram icon to that view.

Do I lose DMs?

No. In minulo, DMs, search, your own posts and posts from people you follow keep working. You can also switch Instagram to DMs only.
