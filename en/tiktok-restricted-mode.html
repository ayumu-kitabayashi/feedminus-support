What TikTok Restricted Mode does and doesn't do (iPhone, 2026)

Restricted Mode filters what TikTok shows you. TikTok's help says it limits content that may not be comfortable for everyone, such as mature or complex themes, and that some features stop working while it is on: the Following feed, going LIVE and gifting on LIVE. It does not remove the For You feed. For You keeps playing, just with some videos filtered out. Turn it on under Settings and privacy › Content preferences › Restricted Mode, with a passcode. How to turn Restricted Mode on or off

  1. In the TikTok app, tap Profile at the bottom.
  2. Tap the Menu ☰ button at the top, then Settings and privacy.
  3. Tap Content preferences, then Restricted Mode.
  4. Follow the steps to set or enter a passcode, then turn it on or off. The first time, you tap Turn on before setting the passcode.

If you have more than one account, TikTok says you have to turn Restricted Mode on for each one separately. Parents and guardians can turn it on for a teen's account through Family Pairing.

What Restricted Mode filters

TikTok says it uses automated systems and human moderators to decide what is left out. Its list of things you shouldn't see includes profanity, sexually suggestive content, realistic violence or threatening imagery, firearms or weapons in an inappropriate setting, illegal or controlled substances, and explicit references to mature or complex themes meant for older audiences. TikTok also says it is still improving the feature and asks you to report videos that shouldn't be there.

What Restricted Mode does not do

It does not turn off For You, cap your time or stop one video from rolling into the next. TikTok's help lists the features that stop working, and For You is not among them; the Following feed is. So with Restricted Mode on you can end up with only the recommended feed and no feed of people you follow, which is the opposite of what many people looking for this setting want.

For time, TikTok has a separate daily screen time setting, and iPhone Screen Time can limit the whole app. Neither one removes For You either.

Does TikTok Restricted Mode turn off the For You page?

No. Restricted Mode filters some content out of what TikTok shows, but For You keeps working. TikTok's help lists the Following feed, going LIVE and gifting on LIVE as the features that become unavailable.

Why did my Following feed disappear on TikTok?

If Restricted Mode is on, that is expected. TikTok's help says the Following feed is unavailable in Restricted Mode. Turn it off under Settings and privacy, Content preferences, Restricted Mode, using your passcode.

What does TikTok Restricted Mode block?

Content TikTok considers not comfortable for everyone, such as profanity, sexually suggestive content, realistic violence, weapons in an inappropriate setting, drugs, and explicit references to mature themes. TikTok uses automated systems and human moderators to decide.

Do I need to turn on Restricted Mode for each account?

Yes. TikTok says that if you have multiple accounts, you must turn Restricted Mode on for each account separately.
