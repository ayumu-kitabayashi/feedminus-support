How to stop scrolling Instagram Reels at night on iPhone (2026)

iPhone's Screen Time Downtime can stop Instagram at night, but it blocks the whole app, DMs included, and Instagram's own Sleep mode (once called Quiet mode) only mutes notifications.

Most guides for this search tell you how to pause a Reel. That doesn't help when the problem is the next one and the one after that. Instagram has no setting that turns off Reels on adult accounts, so at night you can either block the whole app with Screen Time or open Instagram somewhere Reels aren't there.

What iPhone and Instagram can do at night

Sleep mode is Instagram's own night setting. Go to your profile, tap Menu at the top right, then Time management › Sleep mode, and pick the days and hours. Meta launched it as Quiet mode in January 2023 and renamed it later. While it is on, Instagram pauses your notifications and sends an auto-reply to people who message you. It does not lock the app, so you can still open Reels at 1 a.m. Teen Accounts are put in Sleep mode from 10 PM to 7 AM by default.

To actually stop Instagram, use Downtime in iPhone Screen Time:

  1. Go to Settings › Screen Time › Downtime › Scheduled and set a start and end time, for example 11 PM to 7 AM.
  2. Set a Screen Time passcode, then turn on Block at Downtime. Without it, you only see a reminder.
  3. Make sure Instagram is not under Always Allowed.

On iOS 27 the same idea is called Screen Time Schedule. Either way it blocks the whole app, so group chats and DMs stop too, and if you chose the passcode yourself you can type it half asleep.

A normal night

You are a student, it is past midnight and the lights are off. A friend sends a message about tomorrow's class, you open Instagram to answer, and a Reel is already playing. You reply, then swipe once, then again. Sleep mode muted the notification, but you were the one who opened the app. Downtime would have stopped the Reels, but it would also have stopped the reply. In anonymous usage data from the app I make, most social media sessions happen between 8 pm and 1 am (Japan time, where most of the data comes from), so this is the hour to plan for.

If you want to keep DMs but lose Reels at night

Does Instagram have a bedtime mode?

Yes, Sleep mode, which used to be called Quiet mode. It pauses notifications and auto-replies to DMs during the hours you pick, but you can still open Instagram and watch Reels.

How do I block Instagram at night but keep DMs?

Screen Time can't do that, because it blocks the whole app. In minulo, open Instagram with Reels and Explore hidden, or with Every tab but DMs, and lock the official app at night. While it is locked, its notifications don't arrive.
