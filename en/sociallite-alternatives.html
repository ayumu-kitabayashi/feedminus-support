SocialLite (Block Reels & Shorts) alternatives for iPhone: apps that hide Reels and Shorts and keep your DMs (2026)

The closest SocialLite alternative on iPhone is minulo: like SocialLite it hides Reels and Shorts while DMs keep working, and hiding is free.

Hiding stays free. Pro (optional) adds Sleep mode, which blocks social apps from your bedtime until morning, Work mode, which locks the apps you pick during your work hours on the days you choose, plus Hard Mode, a daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.

This page is about SocialLite-Block Reels&Shorts, the iPhone app on the App Store, not the social media reader with a similar name.

It also locks the official apps with Screen Time and covers 10 networks including TikTok. SocialLite itself hides short-form video feeds, recommendations and Explore pages while keeping DMs, and it can block the official apps and send you to SocialLite. minulo, UNDOOMED, Awhile and LoomWeb work the same way: they open social networks in their own view with those parts hidden. Nope is a Safari extension instead, and ScrollGuard works both as a Safari extension and in its own app. minulo also has a Mac app that works in Safari and Chrome. SocialLite has far more ratings and adds push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat.

Does SocialLite block apps in the browser?

Not as far as its App Store page shows.
