ScrollGuard and Nope are Safari extensions instead. minulo also covers TikTok, Threads, Pinterest and LinkedIn, has a Chrome extension, and its hiding is free. SocialLite has far more ratings and adds push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat.

What is a good alternative to SocialLite?

It depends on what you need. For the same approach, an app that shows social networks with Reels and Shorts hidden while DMs keep working, look at minulo, UNDOOMED, Awhile and LoomWeb. minulo covers the most networks of these (10, including TikTok, Threads, Pinterest and LinkedIn) and can lock the official apps; Awhile is Instagram only. If you only browse in Safari, ScrollGuard and Nope are extensions.

How is minulo different from SocialLite?

Both hide Reels and Shorts, keep DMs, and can lock the official apps and send you to their own view. On their App Store pages, minulo lists TikTok, Threads, Pinterest and LinkedIn, which SocialLite's page does not mention, and minulo also has a Chrome extension and a Mac app. SocialLite has push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat, which minulo does not, and far more ratings (about 8,000 in the US); minulo launched in September 2026.

Is there a free alternative to SocialLite?

Nope is fully free with no in-app purchases, but it only works in Safari on instagram.com and youtube.com. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free; Pro adds schedules and Locked Mode. SocialLite itself is a free download with in-app purchases.
