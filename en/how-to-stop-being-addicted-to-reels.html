Why it is hard to stop

The next Reel starts as soon as you swipe, there is no last one, and the feed adjusts to what you just watched. We go through each of these in why Reels and Shorts are so hard to stop watching. Willpower has to win every swipe; changing the setup only has to work once.

Check your real numbers

On iOS 27, go to Settings › Screen Time › Daily Average, tap Week and look at Instagram in your most used apps, then scroll down to Pickups. Write down this week's number so you can compare next week.

Fewer prompts, fixed hours

Go to Settings › Notifications › Instagram and turn off Allow Notifications, or put it in a Scheduled Summary. Then, on iOS 26, schedule Downtime under Screen Time, set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime so Instagram is dimmed at night or during study hours. Apple says limits can be ignored by default, so the passcode step matters. There is no timer for Reels alone; see how to limit only Reels on Instagram. Suggested posts in the feed are a separate problem; see how to hide suggested posts on Instagram.

Keep Instagram, lose Reels (minulo)

  1. Check your numbers. On iOS 27, open Settings, Screen Time and Daily Average, and note your Instagram time and Pickups.
  2. Hide Reels in minulo. Install minulo, sign in to Instagram inside it, and turn off Reels, Explore and suggested posts. DMs and your following feed keep working. Hiding is free.
  3. Lock the official Instagram app. In minulo's Block tab, allow Screen Time and choose Instagram. A free block lasts up to 2 hours, and you can start another when it ends.
  4. Reply to DMs in minulo. Reels friends send you open only after a pause, if you choose to watch.

How do I stop being addicted to Reels?

Make Reels harder to reach rather than relying on willpower: hide Reels in minulo and use Instagram there for DMs, lock the official app, turn off Instagram notifications and set Downtime with Block at Downtime for your weakest hours.

Can I turn off Reels on Instagram?

Instagram has no setting to turn off Reels. minulo hides Reels, Explore and suggested posts inside its own view while DMs keep working. More in Can you remove Reels from the Instagram app on iPhone?

Do I have to delete Instagram to stop watching Reels?

No. With minulo you keep DMs, your following feed and search, and the Reels tab isn't shown.

Are Reels bad for your brain?

A 2025 meta-analysis of 71 studies linked heavier short video use with poorer attention and more anxiety and stress. Most studies were cross-sectional, so it shows a link, not cause.
