What YouTube's help says happens to Home

YouTube's help gives two conditions. When you have no relevant or significant recent watch history, and you have said you don't want future recommendations based on that history, YouTube features like recommendations on the Home page are removed. It gives a new user who hasn't watched any videos yet as an example.

The help page on feeds says that feeds and the top bar are not visible if your watch history is paused or turned off. That top bar is where Subs, Music, Live and custom feeds sit in the iPhone app, so they go away together with the recommendations.

Search keeps working. Your search history is a separate setting on the same Controls tab, called "Include your searches on YouTube."

How to pause watch history on iPhone

  1. Sign in to the YouTube app.
  2. Tap your profile picture.
  3. Tap Settings, then Manage all history.
  4. Tap the Controls tab.
  5. Unselect Include the YouTube videos you watch.

If you are signed out, go to Settings › History & privacy and tap pause watch history.

To empty Home sooner, delete your history as well: in Manage all history, tap the delete icon next to a video, or tap DELETE to remove more at once. YouTube notes that using DELETE also deletes your search history for the time frame you pick.

How to turn watch history back on

  1. Tap your profile picture in the YouTube app.
  2. Tap Settings, then Manage all history.
  3. Tap the Controls tab.
  4. Select Include the YouTube videos you watch again.

YouTube's help only writes out the steps for pausing. Turning history back on is the same option selected again. On a computer, the same setting is on the Controls tab of My Activity.

YouTube's help says that once you start watching videos, Home shows recommended videos based on your preferences and activity. Videos you watched while history was off won't show up in your history, so they won't feed back in.

Does turning off YouTube watch history remove Home recommendations?

It can. YouTube's help says Home recommendations are removed when you have no relevant or significant recent watch history and have said you don't want recommendations based on it. If you still have recent history, delete it as well as pausing.

Why is my YouTube Home page empty?

If your watch history is paused and there is little or no recent history, YouTube removes recommendations from Home. YouTube's help says a new user who hasn't watched any videos yet sees the same thing.

Where did the Subs feed go after I paused my history?

YouTube's help on feeds says the feeds and the top bar are not visible while watch history is paused or turned off. Subs is one of those feeds. The help page points you to turning watch history back on.

How do I turn YouTube watch history back on?

In the YouTube app, tap your profile picture, then Settings, Manage all history, and the Controls tab. Select "Include the YouTube videos you watch" again. Home shows recommendations again once you start watching videos.

Can I keep my watch history and still hide Home recommendations?

Not with a YouTube setting we could find. In minulo you can hide Home recommendations and Shorts while your history stays on, and Subscriptions, Watch later and search keep working. It's free.
