What a dopamine detox originally meant

In a 2020 Harvard Health Publishing article, Dr. Peter Grinspoon explains that dopamine fasting was created by California psychiatrist Dr. Cameron Sepah. The article says many people have misunderstood the science, and that a dopamine “fast” doesn't actually lower your dopamine levels.

The original aim is to step back from behaviors you do on autopilot. By letting yourself feel bored or lonely, or by finding simpler things to enjoy, you get back the choice of whether to do them. The article notes that this sounds a lot like many mindfulness practices and good sleep hygiene, such as not looking at screens before bed.

A dopamine detox on your phone: drop only Reels and Shorts

Switching your phone off cuts you off from people, so it rarely lasts. Instead, pick just one thing to drop. When you look at the apps you use most in Screen Time, Instagram, YouTube and TikTok are often near the top, and inside them the time goes to endless Reels, Shorts and recommended feeds.

  1. Pick one thing to drop: In the Settings app, open Screen Time, look at the apps you use most, and note which screen in each app takes your time.
  2. Set an end-of-day window: On iOS 27, tap Screen Time Schedule, turn on Schedule, add an evening time window and choose which Time Allowance categories are available in it. On iOS 26, schedule Downtime for the evening and turn on Block at Downtime (this needs a Screen Time passcode). Add the apps you use to keep in touch to Always Allowed.
  3. Pick one day on the weekend: Spend one day a week without Reels and Shorts, and decide in advance what you'll do with that time. Unplanned time tends to fill up with the phone again.
  4. Remove the places Reels and Shorts appear: Keep your messages and take only Reels and Shorts off the screen. See the minulo steps below.

Does a dopamine detox lower dopamine?

No, according to the Harvard Health article. It says a dopamine “fast” doesn’t actually lower your dopamine levels. The original idea is practice at being less driven by stimuli.

Does a dopamine detox work?

This page could not confirm studies on the dopamine detox itself. In a 2025 trial that blocked mobile internet for two weeks, attention and mental health improved, but screen time went up again afterwards (see the digital detox page).

Do I have to go without my phone?

No. You can keep messaging and drop only endless screens like Reels and Shorts. That makes it easier to keep going.
