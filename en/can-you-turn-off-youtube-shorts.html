Is there a switch that turns Shorts off?

No. We found no setting in YouTube Help that turns Shorts off on an adult account. YouTube's help lists where viewers find Shorts: the Shorts tab at the bottom of the app, search results, the YouTube homepage, channel pages, Subscriptions and notifications. Each built-in option below covers only part of that list, and most of them lower Shorts rather than remove them.

Set the Shorts feed limit to 0

  1. Sign in to the YouTube app.
  2. Tap the You tab, then Settings.
  3. Tap Time management.
  4. Tap Shorts feed limit and choose 0 from the list.

When you reach the limit, YouTube shows a reminder, and its help says you are "prompted with an option to dismiss or ignore your set limit." So on an adult account it is a reminder, not a lock. The help only talks about the Shorts feed: it doesn't say whether the limit also applies to Shorts on Home, in search results or on channel pages, and it gives steps for the YouTube app only. See the Shorts feed limit explained.

Show fewer Shorts and the X on the Shorts shelf

On Home, scroll to a grid of Shorts, tap More at the top of the grid and choose Show fewer Shorts. YouTube's help describes it as feedback that shows fewer Shorts on pages such as Home and Watch Next. It gives no period, and you can undo it in My Activity under Other activity.

Some people also see an X on the Shorts shelf on Home. YouTube's help doesn't describe it, but SlashGear and GeekChamp quote the message as "Shelf will be hidden for 30 days". tbreak (April 2026) found it only on the mobile website, and GeekChamp (May 2026) says it depends on account, region and app version. On a computer, YouTube's help describes Not interested on a section of Home, again as feedback with no period. See how long the X hides the Shorts shelf.

Turn off watch history

  1. Tap your profile picture in the YouTube app.
  2. Tap Settings, then Manage all history.
  3. Tap the Controls tab.
  4. Unselect Include the YouTube videos you watch.

YouTube's help says that when you have no relevant or significant recent watch history and have chosen not to get recommendations based on it, recommendations on the Home page are removed. If you still have a lot of recent history, delete it as well. The trade-offs: your history stops being saved, YouTube's help on feeds says the top bar with Subs is not visible while history is paused, and the help doesn't say the Shorts tab or Shorts in search go away. Screen Time and supervised teen accounts

iPhone Screen Time can limit or block the whole YouTube app, but it can't target Shorts only, so the videos you wanted to watch are blocked too. Apple doesn't document blocking just the /shorts address in Safari.

On a supervised teen account, a parent can set how long the teen spends scrolling Shorts. In January 2026 YouTube said an option to set the timer to zero was coming soon, and in April 2026 YouTube said the zero option is now live for all parents. This applies only to supervised teen accounts.

Can you turn off YouTube Shorts?

There's no switch in YouTube, but minulo hides Shorts for free while normal videos, search and subscriptions keep working, though only inside minulo. In the YouTube app, the closest options are the Shorts feed limit at 0, which adults can dismiss, and Show fewer Shorts, which lowers Shorts rather than removing them.

Does setting the Shorts feed limit to 0 turn Shorts off?

No. At 0 minutes a reminder appears in the Shorts feed, and YouTube's help says you can dismiss or ignore it. It doesn't say Shorts leave Home, search or channel pages.

Can I turn off Shorts but keep normal YouTube videos?

Not in the YouTube app itself. Open YouTube in minulo with Hide the Shorts shelf and Hide the Shorts tab turned on; normal videos, subscriptions, Watch later and search keep working. Hiding is free.

Does turning off watch history turn off Shorts?

Not as far as YouTube's help says. It removes Home recommendations when you have no relevant recent history, but it doesn't say the Shorts tab or Shorts in search go away, and your history stops being saved.

Can any app turn off Shorts in the official YouTube app?

No. On iPhone no app can change what another app shows. minulo can lock the official YouTube app with Screen Time and send you to its Shorts-free view instead. While it is locked, YouTube notifications don't arrive.

Can parents turn off Shorts for a teen?

On a supervised teen account, a parent can set the Shorts timer to zero. YouTube announced the Shorts timer for teens in January 2026 and said in April 2026 that the zero option is now live for all parents.
