How to reset your YouTube recommendations on iPhone (2026)

We could not find a single reset button for recommendations in YouTube's help as of October 2026. What the help does say is that you can start fresh by deleting your watch and search history, and that deleted videos and searches no longer influence your recommendations. Not interested, Don't recommend channel and Show fewer Shorts are stored separately, and you clear them in My Activity. Recommendations build up again as you watch. Step 1: delete your watch and search history

  1. Sign in to the YouTube app and tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Settings, then Manage all history.
  3. Tap the delete icon next to a video or search, or tap DELETE to remove more at once and pick a time frame.

YouTube's help says that if you delete some or all of your watch history, YouTube won't base future recommendations on that content, and that search entries you delete no longer influence your recommendations. Using DELETE on either list removes both watch and search history for the time frame you choose.

On a computer, My Activity also has Auto-delete under YouTube History › Manage history, where you pick a time range for older history to be deleted automatically.

Step 2: clear Not interested and Don't recommend channel

Every time you tap More (⋮) and choose Not interested or Don't recommend channel, YouTube keeps that feedback. To clear all of it:

  1. Go to My Activity and sign in.
  2. Find Other Google activity in the left menu or under the My Activity banner.
  3. Find YouTube 'Not interested' feedback and select Delete.

Step 3: clear Show fewer Shorts

If you once tapped Show fewer Shorts at the top of a Shorts grid on Home, that choice is also kept in My Activity. Go to My Activity, open Other activity, and under Show fewer Shorts select Delete.

Only do this if you want more Shorts back. If you want fewer, leave it as it is.

What a fresh start does not do

It does not switch recommendations off. YouTube's help says Home shows recommended videos based on your preferences and activity once you start watching again, so the feed fills up again from what you watch next. Liked videos and your Google Account activity can also influence recommendations, according to the same help page.

If you want no recommendations on Home at all, YouTube's help says to delete and turn off your watch history. See what happens to Home when watch history is off.
