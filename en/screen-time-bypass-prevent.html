1. Stop the limit from being ignored

Apple's iPhone User Guide for iOS 26 says that by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once reached. Without a passcode, a limit you set is closer to a reminder. For app limits, the steps to remove the ignore option are in how to turn off Ignore Limit.

Downtime works the same way. Apple says that if you set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime, restricted apps can't be opened during downtime. With it off, you only see a reminder and can keep using the apps.

2. Let someone else choose the passcode

Apple says that if you forget your Screen Time passcode, you can reset it with your Apple Account, as long as you use the same account you entered when you set up Screen Time Passcode Recovery (forgot your Screen Time passcode). That protects your device, but it also means you can undo a limit you set on yourself.

To really hold yourself to it, ask a family member or friend to set the passcode, and don't put your own Apple Account in for recovery. In return, someone who can unlock it should be close by when you genuinely need it.

3. Make deleting and reinstalling harder

Apple's iOS 26 guide says you can go to Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions, turn it on, and tap iTunes & App Store Purchases to set restrictions for store purchases and redownloads. The guide doesn't list each option on that screen, so check yours for one that covers the app you want to keep. This also only means something if someone else holds the passcode.

The iOS 27 guide page on Content & Privacy Restrictions, checked on 2026-11-02, doesn't mention this menu. On iOS 27, check the screen on your iPhone.

4. Close the Safari route

Blocking the app doesn't stop instagram.com or youtube.com in Safari. On iOS 26, Apple's steps are Content & Privacy Restrictions › App Store, Media, Web, & Games › Web Content, select Limit Adult Websites, then tap Add Website below Never Allow and enter the address. On iOS 27, Apple says that if an app declares an associated website, the Time Allowance applies to both.

5. Check Always Allowed

Apple says apps in Always Allowed can be used at all times, regardless of your Screen Time schedules or Time Allowances on iOS 27, and even during downtime on iOS 26. If the app you want to stop is in that list, no limit will close it.

Where do I start if I keep getting around my own Screen Time?

Have someone else set the Screen Time passcode. Then turn on Block at Downtime, and check that the apps you want to stop aren't in Always Allowed.

Can I stop myself from deleting and reinstalling an app?

On iOS 26, Apple says Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions › iTunes & App Store Purchases sets restrictions for store purchases and redownloads. Apple's iOS 27 guide doesn't describe this menu.

Does Screen Time stop Instagram in Safari?

Not if you only limited the app. On iOS 26, add the address under Never Allow in Web Content. On iOS 27, Apple says a Time Allowance also applies to a website the app declares.

Why doesn't a passcode I chose myself work?

You know it, so you type it in at the limit screen. And with passcode recovery on your own Apple Account, you can reset it even if you forget. The limit only holds when someone else sets it.

Can I block Reels but keep DMs?

Screen Time only blocks whole apps. If you open Instagram inside minulo, the Reels and Explore tabs are hidden and DMs keep working. Hiding is free.
