How to see how much time you spend on Instagram (iPhone, 2026)

To see your time on Instagram, go to your profile › Menu › Your activity › Time management. Instagram shows your average daily time over the last 7 days, counted from when you open the app until you close it or switch to another app. For a day-by-day number, use iPhone Screen Time: Settings › Screen Time › Daily Average, then Day or Week. Instagram's Daily limit, on the same screen, only sends a reminder; it doesn't lock the app unless a parent supervises a Teen Account. Neither tells you how much of that time went to Reels.

How to check your time on Instagram

  1. Tap Profile or your profile picture at the bottom right.
  2. Tap Menu at the top right.
  3. Under How you use Instagram, tap Time management. One of Instagram's help pages puts it inside Your activity, so if you don't see it, open Your activity first.

You'll see the average time you spent on Instagram over the last week. Instagram counts from the moment you open the app until you close it or switch to another app. Instagram's help says this feature isn't available on computers.

How to see Instagram time in Screen Time

  1. Open the Settings app on your iPhone.
  2. Tap Screen Time, then Daily Average.
  3. Tap Week or Day. Instagram appears in your most used apps.

If Screen Time is off, turn on App & Website Activity first. Screen Time can also show how many times you picked up your phone and how many notifications you got, which helps if Instagram notifications are what keep pulling you back.

Why the two numbers don't match

Instagram shows an average over 7 days; Screen Time shows each day separately. If Share Across Devices is on, Screen Time adds your iPad and Mac to the total. And if you sometimes use Instagram in Safari, that is web time, not time in the Instagram app. So the two can be different without either being wrong.

How to set a daily limit on Instagram

  1. Go to Profile › Menu.
  2. Under How you use Instagram, tap Time management.
  3. Tap Daily limit, choose an amount of time, and tap the back arrow at the top left.

Instagram calls this a daily time limit reminder: when you reach it, you are told, and you can keep going. Teen Accounts get a 1-hour limit by default, and a supervising parent can block Instagram once it's reached. For a limit that actually stops the app, use Screen Time. How do I see how much time I spend on Instagram?

Go to your profile, tap Menu at the top right, then Time management under How you use Instagram (it may be inside Your activity). Instagram shows your average daily time over the last 7 days. For daily numbers, use iPhone Settings › Screen Time › Daily Average.

Can I see time spent on Instagram on a computer?

No. Instagram's help says the time spent feature isn't available on computers, only in the iPhone, iPad and Android apps.

How do I set a daily time limit on Instagram?

Go to Profile › Menu › Time management › Daily limit, choose a time and tap back. Instagram reminds you when you reach it.

Does Instagram's daily limit block the app?

No. For most people it is only a reminder. Only Teen Accounts supervised by a parent can be blocked when the limit is reached. To stop the app itself, use Screen Time.

Why is my Instagram time different from Screen Time?

Instagram shows a 7-day average from the Instagram app. Screen Time shows each day, and with Share Across Devices on, it includes your iPad and Mac. The numbers measure different things, so they often differ.
