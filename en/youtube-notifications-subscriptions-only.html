How to get YouTube notifications only from your subscriptions (iPhone, 2026)

YouTube sends notifications for channels you subscribe to, and it may also send recommended video notifications from channels you don't subscribe to. To stop the second kind, tap your profile picture, then Settings › Notifications, and turn off Recommended videos. Then set the bell next to each subscribed channel to All, Personalized or None. YouTube's help says not every new upload results in a notification, so the Subs feed is still the place to check for new videos.

Turn off recommended video notifications

  1. Open the YouTube app and tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Settings, then Notifications.
  3. Turn off Recommended videos.

YouTube's help describes these as personalized recommended video notifications from channels you are not subscribed to but may be interested in. Turning them off leaves notifications from your subscriptions as they are.

Choose All, Personalized or None for each channel

When you subscribe to a channel, YouTube sets its bell to Personalized. To change it, go to the channel page or a video from it and tap the bell next to Subscribed. To change many channels in one place, tap Subs in the top bar of the Home tab, tap All at the top right, and tap the bell next to each channel.

  • All: notifications for long-form uploads and live streams. YouTube says you may also get some personalized Shorts notifications based on your subscriptions and watch history.
  • Personalized: notifications for some uploads, Shorts and live streams. YouTube decides when using signals such as your watch history, how often you watch the channel, how popular a video is and how often you open notifications.
  • None: no notifications from that channel.

One catch from the help page: if a channel is set to All but you haven't watched or engaged with it for a while, its notifications only show inside the app, under the bell at the top right of Home.

Why you still miss some uploads

YouTube's help says notification settings differ slightly by device and that not every new upload results in a notification. It points to the Subscriptions feed for recently uploaded videos from channels you subscribe to. If you only want to know what your channels posted, opening Subs once a day is more reliable than waiting for alerts.

How do I stop YouTube notifications from channels I don't subscribe to?

In the YouTube app, tap your profile picture, then Settings and Notifications, and turn off Recommended videos. YouTube's help describes these as recommended video notifications from channels you are not subscribed to.

What is the difference between All and Personalized on a YouTube channel?

All sends notifications for long-form uploads and live streams, and may include some personalized Shorts notifications. Personalized sends notifications for some uploads, Shorts and live streams, chosen using signals like your watch history and how often you watch the channel.

Why am I not getting notifications for every upload?

YouTube's help says not every new upload results in a notification. If a channel is set to All but you haven't watched it for a while, its notifications only appear inside the app under the bell on Home. The Subs feed shows all recent uploads from your subscriptions.

Can I change notifications for all my subscriptions at once?

There is a single list, but each channel has its own bell. Tap Subs in the top bar of the Home tab, tap All at the top right, then tap the bell next to each channel.

Does minulo send YouTube notifications?

No. minulo does not send notifications for YouTube. While minulo has the official YouTube app locked, that app's notifications don't arrive either.

Can I turn off only Shorts notifications on YouTube?

We couldn't find a Shorts-only switch in YouTube Help. For each channel you choose All, Personalized or None: Personalized includes some Shorts, All can also bring some personalized Shorts notifications, and None turns off everything from that channel. Turning off Recommended videos under Settings, Notifications stops notifications from channels you don't subscribe to.
