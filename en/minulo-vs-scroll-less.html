minulo vs Scroll Less: a Scroll Less alternative that also covers TikTok (2026)

minulo is a Scroll Less alternative that hides Reels, Shorts and suggested posts on 10 networks, including TikTok, while your DMs keep working, and hiding is free. Scroll Less takes a different route: its App Store page says it blocks Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts inside the real Instagram and YouTube apps.

Scroll Less is made by Quintana Labs LLC. Its website says that when you start the Blocker, iOS asks you to start a broadcast for Scroll Less, and that this is how it detects Reels and Shorts, on your iPhone. It can also open the Instagram, YouTube and Reddit websites inside Scroll Less with feeds, Explore and related videos hidden. Its App Store page lists in-app purchases such as Scroll Less Premium ($4.99), Premium Annual ($29.99) and Lifetime ($14.99 or $49.99).

Is minulo a good Scroll Less alternative?

If you want Reels and Shorts gone on more networks than Instagram and YouTube, yes. minulo hides them on 10 networks, including TikTok, your DMs keep working, and hiding is free. If you want to stay in the official Instagram and YouTube apps and have Reels and Shorts blocked there, that is what Scroll Less is built for; minulo doesn't change the official apps. Scrolless: Feed Blocker is a different app with a similar name that works in Safari; see minulo vs Scrolless.

Does Scroll Less block Reels inside the official Instagram app?

Its App Store page says it blocks Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts inside the real apps. Its website says this works while the Blocker is on: iOS asks you to start a broadcast for Scroll Less, Reels and Shorts are detected on your iPhone, nothing on your screen is sent anywhere, and you can stop the broadcast from Control Center. We have not tested this ourselves. minulo works differently: it opens Instagram's website inside minulo with Reels hidden, and it can lock the official app with Screen Time.

Is Scroll Less free?

The download is free. The US App Store lists in-app purchases: Scroll Less Premium $4.99, Premium Annual $29.99, Lifetime $14.99 and $49.99, and “Premium Life Time Early December” $5.99. In Japan they are ¥400, ¥2,390, ¥2,500 and ¥8,000, and ¥1,000; in Korea, ₩3,400, ₩20,500, ₩25,000 and ₩77,000, and ₩9,900 (checked 2026-11-10). Neither its App Store page nor its website says which features work without paying; its website says current pricing is on the App Store page. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free.

Does Scroll Less work with TikTok?

TikTok isn't mentioned on its App Store page or its website (checked 2026-11-04). Its website says the Blocker works in the Instagram and YouTube apps, and website hiding works on Instagram, YouTube and Reddit. In minulo, TikTok's For You feed is hidden and messages keep working, and there is also a DMs-only setting for TikTok.

Does Scroll Less keep Instagram DMs?

Yes, according to its App Store page: Instagram DMs, story replies, search and commenting on your own posts still work. In minulo, Instagram DMs keep working while Reels are hidden, and Every tab but DMs leaves only messages and notifications.

Is Scroll Less available in Japanese or Korean?

It is on the Japan and Korea App Stores, but the App Store lists English as its only language. minulo is available in 13 languages, including Japanese and Korean.

Which one is more established?

Scroll Less. It has been on the App Store since March 2025 and has 25 US ratings with an average of 4.5 (2026-11-04). minulo launched in September 2026 and has 1 US rating so far. In Japan and Korea, Scroll Less has no ratings yet; minulo has 4 in Japan and 14 in Korea.
