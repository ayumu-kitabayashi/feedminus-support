minulo vs Unfeed: which app to hide Reels and Shorts on iPhone (2026)

Pick minulo if you want the most for free: hiding is free, no subscription needed, and it hides Reels, Shorts, Explore and suggested posts on 10 networks while your DMs keep working, with a DMs-only setting and locking the official apps for up to 2 hours also free. Unfeed hides Reels, Explore, For You and Shorts for free on Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X, and may suit you better if you want its free focus timer, a pause before apps open and streak achievements; hiding suggested posts, a DMs-only mode and locking the real apps are part of Unfeed Pro.

Unfeed: Block Reels & Shorts is an iPhone app by Greenbird Company Limited, released on the App Store on 2026-09-29. Its listing says it opens Instagram, TikTok, YouTube and X without the feed: Reels, Explore, For You and Shorts are blocked, while direct messages, search, profiles, the people you follow and posting still work, and you sign in on each app’s own site. minulo works the same basic way, showing each network’s website inside the app, but covers 10 networks. Unfeed: Scroll Blocker by Andrii Bashta is a different app and is not covered here.

Is Unfeed free?

Unfeed is a free download, and its listing says blocking Instagram Reels and Explore, TikTok For You, YouTube Shorts and X For You is free, along with Focus now, Pause before opening, Activity, achievements and a widget. Hiding suggested posts and ads, DMs only, locking the real apps, schedules and Sleep are part of Unfeed Pro, listed on the US App Store at $5.99 and $49.99 (monthly or yearly; which is which isn’t stated). In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts on 10 networks is free with no subscription and no time limit; Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode, Work mode, Hard Mode, a Daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.

Do Unfeed and minulo keep DMs working?

Yes. Unfeed’s listing says direct messages, search, profiles, the people you follow and posting still work, and a DMs only mode is part of Pro. In minulo, DMs keep working, and a setting that shows only DMs on Instagram and TikTok is free.

Is Unfeed the same app as Unfeed: Scroll Blocker?

No. Unfeed: Scroll Blocker is a different app by Andrii Bashta (App Store id 6788027178). This page compares Unfeed: Block Reels & Shorts by Greenbird Company Limited (id 6814847686).
