Where the Subs feed is in the YouTube app

  1. Open the YouTube app. It opens on the Home tab.
  2. In the top bar of Home, tap Subs.
  3. Tap a channel picture at the top to go to that channel.

YouTube's help describes Subs as the feed with the newest videos from the channels you're subscribed to, with a list of your subscribed channels at the top. The same top bar holds other feeds, such as Music, Live and, in the US in English, custom feeds.

Why turning off history is not the same as subscriptions only

A common tip is to pause watch history so Home has nothing to recommend. YouTube's help does say Home recommendations are removed when you have no relevant recent history and have said you don't want recommendations based on it.

The help page on feeds adds a catch: feeds and the top bar are not visible if your watch history is paused or turned off. Subs is in that top bar, so pausing history to empty Home also takes away the Subs feed in the app. YouTube's help doesn't describe another way to reach it while history is off. See what happens to Home when watch history is off.

Cutting down what Home shows

If you keep history on and use Subs, you can still trim Home. Tap More (⋮) next to a video and choose Not interested, or Don't recommend channel for a channel you don't want. To see fewer Shorts, scroll to a grid of Shorts on Home, tap More at the top of the grid, and tap Show fewer Shorts. These change what you get. Home keeps recommending.

What minulo does and doesn't do

Why did Subs disappear from the top bar?
