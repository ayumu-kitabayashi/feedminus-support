Make Downtime run past your wake-up time

  1. Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then Always Allowed, and keep only what you need in the morning, such as Phone, Messages and Clock.
  2. Tap Downtime, turn on Scheduled, and set an end time about an hour after you get up.
  3. Set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime. Apple's iOS 27 user guide doesn't list this option.

Apple says that with Block at Downtime on, restricted apps can't be opened during downtime. On iOS 27 this becomes Screen Time Schedule. If your class starts at different times on different days, use Customize Days.

Keep notifications quiet until you are up

A Sleep Focus filters notifications and calls during your sleep schedule, so a pile of likes isn't waiting on the lock screen. Apple describes Sleep Focus as filtering notifications and calls, not locking apps, so pair it with Downtime or a block.

Check messages in the morning without Reels

  1. Hide Reels and For You in minulo. Install minulo, sign in to Instagram and TikTok inside it, and turn off Reels, Explore, suggested posts and For You. Hiding is free.
  2. Start a block when you wake up. In minulo's Block tab, allow Screen Time, choose the official Instagram and TikTok apps and start a block. On the free plan it lasts up to 2 hours, which covers getting ready and the commute.
  3. Read DMs in minulo. Tap Open in minulo on the block screen and check messages there, with the Reels feed hidden.
  4. Make it automatic if you want. Use a Downtime schedule for free, or minulo Pro's Sleep mode, which keeps the apps locked until your wake time every morning.

Why do I check my phone as soon as I wake up?

For many people the phone is the alarm, so it is already in hand, and the apps on the home screen open straight to a feed. With Instagram Reels and TikTok For You hidden in minulo, checking messages when you wake up doesn't turn into 30 minutes of scrolling in bed (hiding is free). Moving the alarm and hiding the feed both cut that first tap.

Can iPhone block Instagram until a set time in the morning?

Yes. A Downtime schedule with Block at Downtime on blocks apps outside Always Allowed until the end time you set.

Does Sleep Focus stop me opening Instagram?

No. A Focus filters notifications. To stop apps opening, use Downtime with Block at Downtime, or lock the apps with minulo.

Can I still read messages in the morning?

Yes. Keep Messages in Always Allowed. For Instagram DMs, open Instagram inside minulo, where Reels are hidden. The official app's notifications don't arrive while minulo blocks it.

Is there a free way to do this?

Downtime and Sleep Focus are built into iPhone. minulo hides Reels and For You for free and locks the official apps for free, up to 2 hours per block.
