How to stop checking Instagram and TikTok first thing in the morning (iPhone, 2026)

On iPhone, a Downtime schedule that runs until after you get up can keep Instagram and TikTok closed in the morning, and a Sleep Focus keeps notifications quiet.

Most of the morning scroll starts with the alarm: you turn it off, the phone is already in your hand, and the next tap is Instagram. Downtime blocks the whole app until the end time you set, and Sleep Focus filters notifications without locking anything. A separate alarm clock means the phone isn't the first thing you touch.

Make Downtime run past your wake-up time

  1. Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then Always Allowed, and keep only what you need in the morning, such as Phone, Messages and Clock.
  2. Tap Downtime, turn on Scheduled, and set an end time about an hour after you get up.
  3. Set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime. Apple's iOS 27 user guide doesn't list this option.

Apple says that with Block at Downtime on, restricted apps can't be opened during downtime. On iOS 27 this becomes Screen Time Schedule. If your class starts at different times on different days, use Customize Days.

Keep notifications quiet until you are up

A Sleep Focus filters notifications and calls during your sleep schedule, so a pile of likes isn't waiting on the lock screen. Apple describes Sleep Focus as filtering notifications and calls, not locking apps, so pair it with Downtime or a block. To let only messaging apps through, see a Focus that allows only messaging apps.

Why do I check my phone as soon as I wake up?

For many people the phone is the alarm, so it is already in hand, and the apps on the home screen open straight to a feed. Moving the alarm and hiding the feed both cut that first tap.

Can iPhone block Instagram until a set time in the morning?

Yes. A Downtime schedule with Block at Downtime on blocks apps outside Always Allowed until the end time you set. For the rest of the workday, see how to stop scrolling your phone at work.

Does Sleep Focus stop me opening Instagram?

No. A Focus filters notifications. To stop apps opening, use Downtime with Block at Downtime.

Can I still read messages in the morning?

Yes. Keep Messages in Always Allowed. For Instagram DMs, open Instagram inside minulo, where Reels are hidden. The official app's notifications don't arrive while minulo blocks it. Since version 1.0.19, Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) so Instagram DM notifications still arrive while Instagram is blocked, except during Sleep mode, Work mode or after the daily limit.

Is there a free way to do this?

Yes. Downtime and Sleep Focus are built into iPhone, and a separate alarm clock keeps the phone out of your hand when you wake up.
