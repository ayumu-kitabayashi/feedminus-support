What Brick does well

Brick's point is that the off switch is not on your phone. Its website says Screen Time limits are easy to skip, while Brick needs a rescan to end a session, and Strict Mode closes the usual ways out such as deleting the app. It is a one-time purchase, it has 58,193 US ratings averaging 4.9, and Brick says it ships worldwide. If you have tried app blockers and always turn them off, a physical device is a real difference.

The trade-offs: you pay $59 and wait for shipping, you have to keep the device somewhere you can reach when you need your apps back, and a blocked app is blocked completely, so Instagram DMs and Snapchat chats stop too.

The free version: Screen Time with someone else's passcode

  1. Set your limits in Settings › Screen Time. In iOS 27 you can use a Screen Time Schedule and Time Allowances for categories such as Social Media.
  2. Hand your iPhone to a friend or family member and let them create the Screen Time passcode (Settings › Screen Time › Manage Screen Time › Lock Screen Time Settings).
  3. Ask them to keep it and not tell you.

Apple says the passcode must be entered before any Screen Time setting can change. Apple also recommends adding Screen Time Passcode Recovery with an Apple Account. If you add your own account, you can reset the passcode yourself, which defeats the purpose. Let the other person enter their own Apple Account there instead. Apple says that without the recovery account, you can't reset or remove the passcode and Apple can't provide it.

Is there a free alternative to Brick?

Yes. iPhone Screen Time is built in and free. Set your limits, then let a friend or family member create the Screen Time passcode and keep it, so you can't change the settings on your own. ScreenZen is also free (its only in-app purchases are tips) and can lock its own settings.

How much does Brick cost?

On Brick's website in October 2026, Brick is $59, Brick Night is $129, and both together are $169. Brick says these are one-time purchases with no subscription, and the App Store app is free.

Can I use Brick without the Brick device?

Not for its main feature. You start and end a session by tapping your phone on the device. If you need to get out without it, the app includes 5 Emergency Unbricks that unblock all apps.

Which app is hardest to turn off without hardware?

Opal says its Deep Focus mode can't be bypassed or cancelled once it starts. Screen Time with a passcode only someone else knows is also hard to undo, as long as you don't add Passcode Recovery with your own Apple Account, since that lets you reset it yourself.
