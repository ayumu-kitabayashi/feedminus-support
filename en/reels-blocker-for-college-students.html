Best way to keep Instagram for group chats but drop Reels (college students, 2026)

Short answer: use Instagram inside an app that opens it in its own browser with Reels hidden and DMs working (minulo, UNDOOMED or Stillfeed). If you keep opening the official app out of habit, lock it with Screen Time. Safari extensions work if you are fine using Instagram in Safari. Screen Time on its own blocks your group chats along with Reels.

Which one fits you

  • You only need the group chats. Use a messages-only view: minulo's Every tab but DMs (free), UNDOOMED's Messages Only (Pro) or Stillfeed's DMs Only (Pro).
  • You want friends' posts too, just no Reels. minulo, UNDOOMED and Stillfeed all do this for free.
  • You have an Android phone, or switch between iPhone and Android. UNDOOMED is the one of these with an Android app.
  • You keep opening the official app on autopilot. Lock it with Screen Time. minulo turns the block screen into a shortcut to its Reels-free Instagram (Open in minulo), so the chats are still one tap away.
  • You need voice messages, calls or posting Stories. Those need the official app. A screen-broadcast app keeps you in it, but blocks the whole app when Reels appear.
  • You do not want another app. Use instagram.com in Safari with Nope (free) or ScrollGuard, or use Screen Time and accept that the chats are blocked too.

Group chat notifications

With minulo, notifications come from the official Instagram app. If you leave the official app unlocked, they arrive as usual and you can reply in minulo. While the official app is locked with Screen Time, its notifications do not arrive, so lock it only when you can miss them, for example while studying.

What is the best way to keep Instagram for group chats but drop Reels?

Open Instagram inside an app that hides Reels and keeps DMs, such as minulo, UNDOOMED or Stillfeed. For messages only, minulo's Every tab but DMs is free; UNDOOMED and Stillfeed offer a messages-only mode in Pro. If you keep going back to the official app, lock it with Screen Time.

Can I remove Reels from the official Instagram app?

On iPhone, no third-party app can change what another app shows. Apps either show Instagram in their own browser or in Safari, or watch the screen and block the whole app when Reels appear. minulo can lock the official app with Screen Time and send you to its Reels-free Instagram instead.

Why not just use Screen Time?

Screen Time blocks the whole Instagram app, so your group chats are blocked together with Reels, and it has no setting that blocks only Reels. It works well combined with an app that keeps the chats available elsewhere.

Is there a free option?

Yes. Hiding Reels while keeping DMs is free in minulo, UNDOOMED and Stillfeed, and minulo's messages-only setting is free too. Nope, a Safari extension for instagram.com and youtube.com, says it is fully free.

Does it work for YouTube Shorts and TikTok too?

minulo hides YouTube Shorts and TikTok's For You feed, and keeps YouTube search, subscriptions and TikTok messages. UNDOOMED and Stillfeed cover YouTube; UNDOOMED lists TikTok as unavailable, and TikTok is not mentioned on Stillfeed's App Store page.
