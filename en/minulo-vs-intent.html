minulo vs Intent: an Intent alternative that hides Reels and keeps DMs free (2026)

Intent and minulo both open social sites inside their own app with Reels and Shorts hidden. Intent asks you to pick a purpose before you open a network and covers 12 networks, including Xiaohongshu and NAVER, but its Messages feature needs Intent Pro. Pick Intent if you like choosing a purpose first or need those two networks. Pick minulo if you mainly want your DMs without the feed and don't want to pay for that.

Intent, listed on the App Store as “Intent: Block Reels & Shorts” and made by Evergoods Holdings LLC, is built around choosing why you are opening a network: messages, search or timed browsing. It hides supported Reels, Shorts and recommendation entry points, and its listing says it will not quietly drop you into an unrestricted feed. You sign in on each platform's own website inside the app. Several other apps on the App Store are also called Intent, such as a habit tracker and a pause-before-you-open app; this page is about the one that blocks Reels and Shorts.

Pick Intent if

  • you use Xiaohongshu or NAVER;
  • you like picking a purpose (messages, search or timed browsing) before each visit;
  • you want session timers, focus and sleep routines, or caption drafts;
  • you want a cheaper Pro plan ($2.99 a month or $19.99 a year) or use an iPad.

Pick minulo if

  • you want your Instagram or TikTok DMs without the feed for free;
  • you want the official apps locked so you open them in minulo instead;
  • you use a Mac;
  • you want a plain app that just hides Reels, Shorts and suggested posts on the networks you open.

minulo shows each network's website rather than the official app, so screens look a little different from the app. While minulo locks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive. minulo is new and has few ratings so far.

Is minulo a good Intent alternative?

Yes, if what you want most is your DMs without Reels. In Intent, Messages are part of Intent Pro, while in minulo DMs keep working for free and there is a free setting that shows only DMs on Instagram and TikTok. Intent covers more networks, including Xiaohongshu and NAVER, has a cheaper Pro plan, and asks you to choose a purpose before each visit, which some people find helpful.

Is Intent free?

Partly. Its US App Store listing says search and timed browsing are free, and that Messages, Notifications, Post, Profile and advanced customization need Intent Pro, which costs $2.99 a month or $19.99 a year (checked 2026-11-04). In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free and DMs keep working without paying; Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

Which apps does Intent work with?

Its listing names Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, Threads, Facebook, Xiaohongshu, Reddit, X, Pinterest, NAVER, Snapchat and LinkedIn, and says web features vary by platform, region and account (checked 2026-11-04). minulo works with Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn and Snapchat.
