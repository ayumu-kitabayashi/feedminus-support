What Snapchat for Web is made for

Snapchat's help article on getting Snapchat for Web tells you to go to web.snapchat.com on a computer. Snap announced it in July 2022 as a way to keep chatting from your laptop, first for Snapchat+ subscribers in a few countries. Snapchat's current help article on getting it doesn't mention a subscription.

  • Browsers: the latest Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari.
  • Account: keep the Snapchat app on your phone up to date and log in with the same account.
  • One computer at a time: logging in on another computer logs you out of the first one.
  • App-only features: Snapchat lists viewing photo and video Snaps in Memories, managing your Stories and their analytics, changing your username and updating your privacy settings as things you still do in the mobile app.

Spotlight is not left out of the website. Snapchat says you can view Spotlight on Snapchat for Web at web.snapchat.com, so the website alone doesn't get you away from short videos.
