Opal alternatives that still let you use Instagram DMs (2026)

Opal, Brick and iPhone Screen Time block or limit the whole app, so while Instagram is blocked its DMs are blocked too. one sec and ScreenZen add a pause before an app opens instead. If you want Reels and Shorts gone but DMs available, an app that hides only those parts, such as minulo, fits better; minulo can also lock the official apps and send you to its Reels-free view.

What is a good Opal alternative if I still need Instagram DMs?

Apps that hide only Reels and Shorts leave DMs working. minulo shows Instagram and 9 other networks with Reels, Shorts and suggested posts hidden, and it can lock the official Instagram app so you open minulo instead. Hiding is free.

Does Opal block Instagram DMs?

Opal blocks the apps you choose, so while Instagram is blocked you cannot open it at all, which includes your DMs. Opal's description talks about app limits and app blockers; it does not describe hiding parts of an app.

Is a pause app like one sec better than blocking?

one sec says it delays access instead of blocking, which it describes as psychologically different, and reports that app usage drops by 57% on average. After the pause the whole app opens, Reels included. minulo removes Reels and Shorts instead, so what you open is already without them.
