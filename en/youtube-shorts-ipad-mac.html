How to use YouTube without Shorts on iPad and Mac (Safari, 2026)

On a Mac, you can hide YouTube Shorts with minulo's Mac app in Safari or Chrome. On iPad, YouTube has no setting that turns Shorts off, and minulo, which hides Shorts for free on iPhone, has no iPad version.

YouTube has no setting that turns Shorts off on iPad or on a Mac. YouTube's help offers ways to see fewer of them instead. In the YouTube app on iPad you can tap Show fewer Shorts on a Shorts grid on Home, and set a Shorts feed limit, even to zero, though the reminder can be dismissed. On youtube.com in Safari on a Mac you can mark a section on Home as Not interested, and if you turn off watch history and have no recent history, YouTube removes recommendations from the homepage. On iPad, Screen Time can also block websites by URL.

Is there a setting to turn off Shorts?

We could not find one in YouTube's help as of October 2026, for the app or for youtube.com. The help pages talk about seeing fewer Shorts and limiting the Shorts feed, not removing Shorts. The steps below are what YouTube itself offers, split by device.

On iPad: Show fewer Shorts

  1. Open the YouTube app and go to Home.
  2. Scroll to a grid of Shorts.
  3. Tap More at the top of the grid.
  4. Tap Show fewer Shorts.

To undo it, go to My Activity, choose Other activity in the left menu, and select Delete under Show fewer Shorts.

On iPad: set a Shorts feed limit

  1. Sign in to the YouTube app.
  2. Tap the You tab, then Settings.
  3. Tap Time management, then Shorts feed limit.
  4. Pick a time. Zero is one of the options.

When you reach the limit in the Shorts feed, YouTube shows a reminder and gives you an option to dismiss or ignore it. YouTube's help page describes this setting for the YouTube app only and says nothing about youtube.com.

On a Mac (Safari): Not interested and Don't recommend channel

  1. On youtube.com, find the video, playlist, post or section on Home that you don't want.
  2. Click More next to its title.
  3. Click Not interested.

For a channel that keeps showing up, click More next to one of its videos or Shorts and select Don't recommend channel. Both kinds of feedback can be cleared later in My Activity under YouTube 'Not interested' feedback.

On a Mac (Safari): turn off watch history

  1. Go to My Activity (myactivity.google.com) and sign in.
  2. Click the Controls tab in the left sidebar.
  3. Select Turn off.

YouTube's help says that if you have no relevant or significant recent watch history and have chosen not to get recommendations based on it, features like recommendations on the YouTube homepage are removed. This is a Google Account setting, so it is not limited to the Mac. Videos you watch while it is off are not saved to your history. The help page does not say whether Shorts also go away in search results or on channel pages.

On iPad: block a website with Screen Time

Apple says you can block specific websites by URL. Go to Settings › Screen Time › Content & Privacy Restrictions and turn it on, then tap App Store, Media, Web, & Games › Web Content. Select Limit Adult Websites, tap Add Website below Never Allow, and enter the address. We have not tested whether entering the Shorts address of youtube.com blocks only Shorts pages, so check it on your own iPad before relying on it.

Can you turn off YouTube Shorts on iPad?

Not with a YouTube setting, as far as YouTube's help shows in October 2026. In the YouTube app you can tap More on a Shorts grid on Home and choose Show fewer Shorts, and you can set a Shorts feed limit under You, Settings, Time management. The limit can be zero, but YouTube lets you dismiss or ignore the reminder.

Can you hide Shorts on youtube.com in Safari on a Mac?

Yes, with minulo's Mac app, which hides Shorts in Safari and Chrome. YouTube itself has no switch for it. On youtube.com you can click More next to a video or a section on Home and choose Not interested. If you turn off watch history in My Activity and have no relevant recent history, YouTube removes recommendations from the homepage.

Does the Shorts feed limit work on youtube.com?

YouTube's help page gives steps for the YouTube app only and does not describe the setting for youtube.com in a browser, so we could not confirm it there.

How do I undo Show fewer Shorts?

Go to My Activity, choose Other activity in the left menu, and select Delete under Show fewer Shorts.

No. minulo has an iPhone app and a Mac app, but no iPad version. On iPhone it opens YouTube's website with the Shorts shelf, the Shorts tab and Home recommendations hidden, for free.
