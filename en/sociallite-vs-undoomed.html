SocialLite vs UNDOOMED: two apps that hide Reels and keep your DMs (2026)

SocialLite and UNDOOMED both show your social networks inside their own app with short video feeds filtered out and keep DMs, and a third option, minulo, does the same for free and also covers TikTok.

SocialLite is the bigger one: 17,763 US ratings at 4.7 stars (checked 2026-11-11), push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat, and it can block the official apps and send you to SocialLite. UNDOOMED is smaller (108 ratings, 4.3 stars), runs on iPhone and Android, and has a Messages Only mode for Instagram and LinkedIn. Neither one lists TikTok. If you need TikTok, or want messages only without paying, minulo is a third option. If you are also looking at ScrollGuard, see ScrollGuard alternatives that keep Instagram DMs.

Pick SocialLite if

  • you want Instagram or Snapchat notifications while Reels stay hidden;
  • you want the app with by far the most ratings and the longest list of reviews;
  • you also use an iPad (its release notes mention iPad support).

Pick UNDOOMED if

  • you use Android, or want the same app on iPhone and Android;
  • you want messages only for LinkedIn as well as Instagram;
  • you want hourly or daily caps with a PIN;
  • you would rather pay once: it lists a $29.99 lifetime Pro next to its monthly and yearly plans.

Where minulo fits

minulo works the same basic way as both apps: it opens each network's official website inside the app and hides the parts you choose. The differences are TikTok and Snapchat (minulo covers both, 10 networks in all), a free messages-only setting (Every tab but DMs for Instagram and TikTok) and locking the official apps, so tapping the usual Instagram icon shows a block screen with an Open in minulo button. Locking is free for up to 2 hours per block. Sleep mode, Work mode, longer blocks, Hard Mode and a Daily limit are in minulo Pro. For Reddit, see does SocialLite support Reddit.

Limits, so you can decide honestly: minulo can't change the official apps, so Reels and Shorts are hidden only inside minulo. While minulo blocks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. Posting Stories, voice messages and calls aren't available, because the web versions of these networks don't have them. minulo runs on iPhone and Mac (the Mac app works in Safari and Chrome), not Android, and it is new, with few ratings. Which is better, SocialLite or UNDOOMED?

It depends on what you need. If you need TikTok, or want messages only without paying, minulo is a third option. SocialLite has far more ratings (17,763 in the US, average 4.7, checked 2026-11-11) and push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat. UNDOOMED runs on Android as well as iPhone, has a Messages Only mode for Instagram and LinkedIn, and its paid plans cost less. For Snapchat with only chats and Stories, see how to use Snapchat only for chats and Stories.

Do SocialLite or UNDOOMED work with TikTok?

Neither lists it. SocialLite's App Store description and release notes don't mention TikTok, and UNDOOMED lists TikTok and Snapchat as unavailable. minulo opens TikTok with For You, Explore and Create hidden while messages keep working, for free. To keep only your TikTok messages, see how to use TikTok for DMs only on iPhone.

Is SocialLite or UNDOOMED on Android?

UNDOOMED is on iPhone and Android. The sources we used describe SocialLite on iPhone and iPad only. minulo is not on Android yet.

Which one is cheaper?

UNDOOMED. Its Plus plan is $1.99 a month and Pro is $2.99 a month, $17.99 a year or $29.99 lifetime in the US. SocialLite needs a subscription for new users: its pricing page lists $9.99 a month or $29.99 a year in the US, and the App Store also lists other offers from $3.99 to $47.90 (checked 2026-11-10). UNDOOMED is free to download.

Which one collects less data?

On their US App Store privacy labels (checked 2026-10-23), SocialLite lists Identifiers under Data Used to Track You, and UNDOOMED lists nothing there. UNDOOMED's Data Not Linked to You is a device ID, product interaction and crash data. Apple notes that developers write these labels and Apple does not verify them.
