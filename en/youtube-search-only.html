YouTube’s way: delete and turn off watch history

YouTube's help on recommendations says: "If you don't want video recommendations on Home, you can delete and turn off your watch history." Its watch history page says that when you have no relevant or significant recent watch history and have turned it off, features like recommendations on the YouTube homepage are removed.

To pause watch history on iPhone: sign in to the YouTube app, tap your profile picture, tap Settings, then Manage all history, tap the Controls tab and unselect Include the YouTube videos you watch. For what else changes on Home, see what happens to Home when you turn off watch history.

What that costs you

Videos you watch stop being saved to your history, so finding something you watched last week gets harder. If you still have plenty of recent history, pausing alone may not empty Home, which is why YouTube pairs it with deleting. We found no setting in YouTube's help that hides Home while keeping your history.

Can you hide the YouTube Home feed on iPhone?

Not with a switch in the YouTube app, as far as YouTube's help shows. Deleting and turning off watch history removes Home recommendations. In minulo, Everything on home but search leaves only the search box, for free.

Why is my YouTube Home page empty?

If your watch history is off and you have little or no recent history, YouTube removes recommendations from Home. Turning history back on brings them back as you watch.
