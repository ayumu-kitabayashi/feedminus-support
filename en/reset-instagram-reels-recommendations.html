How to reset suggested content on Instagram

  1. Go to your profile and tap the menu at the top right.
  2. Under What you see, tap Content preferences.
  3. Tap Reset suggested content, then Next.
  4. Follow the steps. You can review the accounts you follow and unfollow any you no longer want to see, and review your ad topics.
  5. Tap Reset suggested content to finish.

Instagram's help says the reset cannot be undone, does not change who you follow or the ads you see, and does not delete your data.

What happens after the reset

Meta says your recommendations "will start to personalize again over time" based on the content and accounts you interact with. Instagram's help says the same for likes, shares and other interactions. We could not confirm in Instagram's help how long this takes. In our own use, if we went back to watching the same kinds of Reels, the Reels tab started to feel familiar again within days to a couple of weeks.

Meta also says its ranking uses many predictions, some based on behavior, so likes and follows are not the only things that count. We could not confirm in Instagram's help exactly how much simply watching a reel counts. So in the first days after a reset, it is worth tapping Not interested on Reels you don't want instead of just watching them.

A reset changes which Reels you see, not how many. Instagram has no setting for adults that turns Reels off. The Following feed removes suggested posts from your feed, but the Reels tab stays.

Can you reset Instagram Reels recommendations?

Yes. Go to your profile, tap the menu, then Content preferences and Reset suggested content. It clears your recommendations across Explore, Reels and Feed at the same time. There is no option in Instagram's help to reset Reels only.

How do I reset my Instagram algorithm?

Use Reset suggested content under Content preferences in Instagram's settings. Instagram's help says it cannot be undone, and your suggestions become personalized again as you like, share and interact with content.

Will resetting unfollow anyone or delete my data?

No. Instagram's help says resetting suggested content does not change who you follow or the ads you see, and does not delete your data. During the reset you can choose to review your following list and unfollow accounts yourself.

Why do the same Reels come back after a reset?

Because recommendations learn again from what you interact with after the reset. If you watch and like the same kinds of Reels, you will see more of them again. Tap Not interested on what you don't want, or add words with Don't suggest posts with certain words.
