Privacy Policy

This is an English translation of the Japanese privacy policy. If they differ, the Japanese version prevails.

In short: minulo does not know who you are.
There is no account registration. The developer's server exists only for "Refer a friend," and a referral code and proof of purchase are sent to it only when you use that feature. To improve the app, minulo sends only anonymous usage data, the details of app errors, and whether you came from an App Store search ad to an analytics tool (PostHog). It never sends the pages you viewed, the text you typed, or your social media IDs and passwords. You log in to each service directly on that service's page, and your ID and password never pass through minulo.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect information that can identify you personally. We do not collect your name, email address, location, or advertising identifier, and no advertising SDK is built in. We also do not track you (link your activity across other companies' apps and websites).

Only in the iPhone/iPad version and the Android version, to improve the app, we send anonymous usage data to the analytics tool PostHog. We send only the following.

  • Types of actions, such as opening the app or how many screens of the getting-started steps you went through
  • On which service (Instagram, YouTube, etc.) and at which entry point (Reels, Shorts, etc.) you turned back
  • Use of features, such as turning a block on or off or creating a schedule. When you turn a hiding rule on or off, we send only the name of that rule (such as "Reels"), and not the page you were viewing or its content
  • The app's display language, the device's language setting (a language code with a region, such as "ja-JP"), the device's "Region" setting (a country or region code such as "JP"; from 1.0.11; this is not determined from location or IP address), and the iOS or Android version
  • Stages of purchasing, such as viewing the pricing screen or making a purchase
  • When the app crashes or a process does not work properly, the details of the error (with URLs, names starting with "@", and email addresses removed), the place in the program where the error occurred (file name, line, and function name), and the app and iOS or Android version
  • (iPhone/iPad version only) Whether you came from an App Store search ad and, if so, the ad's numbers (campaign, ad group, and keyword numbers). This uses Apple's system (AdServices), which asks Apple at the first launch. It does not use the advertising identifier, and it does not track you across other companies' apps or websites
  • The age range you answered in the getting-started steps (a range such as "25–34"), how you heard about minulo (App Store search, AI, a friend, social media, etc.), how much time you spend on social media per day (a range such as "1–2 hours"), and the time of day you tend to scroll (choices such as "before bed"). You can continue without answering any of these
  • When you use the work hours block, its start and end times, the number of days of the week, and the number of apps added (we do not send which apps; on iPhone, Apple hides them, so we cannot send them in the first place). We do not send the times for bedtime
  • Social media time survey (optional): About three weeks after you install minulo, a question, "Since installing minulo, how has your social media time changed?", appears once on the home screen. Only if you answer, we send your answer (decreased, no change, or increased), the number of days since you installed it, the "time you spend on social media per day" you answered in the getting-started steps, and the time you spent on social media inside minulo and the number of times you turned back from a blocked screen (counted by minulo itself). The device's usage time shown after you answer (such as iPhone Screen Time) is only displayed on the screen and is not sent off the device. If you close the question, nothing is sent. The answers collected are used, as numbers combined across everyone, in articles, research, and the like
  • The result of checking whether your social media login is kept on the device (only whether the login was restored from a saved copy, and the number of cookies at that time; we do not send the contents of cookies, IDs, or passwords)

To be clear about what we do not send: we do not send your date of birth or exact age, the URLs, titles, or content of pages you viewed, text you typed (such as schedule names), your social media IDs and passwords, the apps you selected in Screen Time, or app usage data read by the Android version. The apps you selected in Screen Time are hidden by Apple in the first place, so minulo cannot see them. The apps you selected and the usage data in the Android version are used only on the device (5-2).

The identifier used for this measurement is a random string created by the app. It is not linked to your name, email address, or advertising identifier.

The Mac version and the Chrome and Safari extensions do not include any analytics tool. The extensions contain no code that communicates with outside servers.

The Mac version connects to the outside in only one place. Once a day, it checks whether a new version is available, using a version number file placed on this website. It sends nothing from your side—no user identifier, settings, or records are included. You can turn this check off in the app's settings.

On this website (minulo.app), to learn which articles or websites people came from before going to the App Store, we send the page opened, the referring domain, the display language, the type of device (one word, such as iPhone or Mac), and whether the App Store button was pressed to PostHog. We do not use cookies; we use only a temporary number that disappears when you close the tab, and we do not create records for each person.

Only if you sign up for the Android version waitlist, the email address you enter is held in Google Forms (a Google service). It is used only to send you a single notice when the Android version becomes available on Google Play; we do not contact you otherwise or provide it to third parties. It is not linked to the app's or this website's measurement. If you would like it deleted, please contact us at the address below.

2. Where records are stored

The app records only the following. All of it is stored only in a database on your device.

  • Which service you opened and when
  • How many times moving to feeds or short videos was stopped
  • Settings (display language, hard mode status, the expiry of "Open for 5 minutes," Pro purchase status, etc.)

The developer has no means of viewing or obtaining these. If you have device backup (iCloud Backup, etc.) turned on, the records are included in Apple's backup, like other apps' data. This handling follows Apple's privacy policy. On Android, if you have Google backup turned on, the records may likewise be included in Google's backup, and that handling follows Google's privacy policy.

3. Logging in to each service

minulo displays the web pages of the services you choose inside the app. You log in directly on that service's page. The ID and password you enter are sent directly to that service. minulo never receives them, stores them, or sends them to the developer.

Cookies for keeping you logged in are stored in an area that iOS or Android manages separately for each app. They cannot be read by other apps or by the developer.

4. About communication

The app communicates with the outside only in the following cases.

  • Communication with the services you open. This is the same communication as when you open the same site in a browser, and it does not go through minulo's servers. The destinations are limited in advance to the host names of supported services, and you cannot move to hosts that are not on the list.
  • The sending of anonymous usage data and errors described in 1. (PostHog), and the check of whether you came from a search ad (Apple).
  • (iPhone/iPad version only) Checking whether a new version is available on the App Store (once a day; it only reads Apple's public information and sends nothing from your side).
  • (iPhone/iPad version only) Only when you use "Refer a friend," the app communicates with minulo's referral server (minulo-api.vercel.app). It sends only the referral code, a passphrase for verifying that code (created automatically by the app), and, when a referred person makes a purchase, Apple's signed proof of purchase (to verify the conditions for the reward). It does not send your name, email address, social media accounts, or the pages you viewed.

5. About Screen Time permission

When you use the feature that blocks the apps themselves, minulo asks for iOS Screen Time (Family Controls) permission.

The apps you select are stored on the device as tokens issued by Apple whose contents cannot be read on their own. Because of this system, the developer cannot see which apps you selected. This is how Apple designed it.

5-2. About permissions in the Android version

The Android version of minulo uses the following permissions to block the apps themselves. For each of them, you can use the other features even if you do not grant it.

  • Usage access: It reads the name of the app currently in front and the time you spent in the apps you selected. It is used only to stop the apps you selected and to count the daily limit, and it is not sent off this device.
  • Display over other apps: Used only to bring minulo's screen to the front when you open an app you selected.
  • Notifications: Used for a persistent notification showing that a block is on (Android rules require one while the app runs in the background) and for notices such as the end of a break.

The list of apps you selected is stored only on the device. The Android version does not use the camera or microphone. You can revoke the permissions at any time in Android's Settings.

About the camera and microphone

In the iPhone version (from 1.0.11), the camera and microphone are used only when you take and send photos or videos in Snapchat opened inside minulo. The photos, videos, and audio you capture are sent directly to Snapchat; minulo does not receive them and does not store or send them. You can revoke camera and microphone permission at any time in the iPhone's Settings. Even if you do not grant it, the other features work as usual.

6. About the Mac version and browser extensions

The Mac version of minulo and the Chrome and Safari extensions send nothing at all to the outside. They contain no code that communicates. The Safari content blocker and the Mac version's URL redirection, by design, cannot read page content. Some of the Chrome and Safari extensions, and the script the iPhone version inserts into pages, refer to the text of displayed elements to identify things such as "Suggested," but they neither store nor send it.

The only thing stored is your choice of "which rules to use." In the Chrome extension, this choice is stored in Chrome's sync feature (chrome.storage.sync), and in the Mac app, on your Mac. If you use the sync feature, the settings carry over to Chrome signed in with the same Google account, but this is a Google feature, and nothing is sent to our servers. In the Safari extension, it is stored only on your Mac.

The Mac app has a feature that reads only the URL of the page open in your browser and, when it is an entry point to a feed or short videos, redirects you to a place you chose. This feature does not read page content. It requires macOS "Automation" permission and does not work without it.

7. Deleting records

When you delete the app, all records on the device are erased. Your login status for each service is also erased at the same time. In the Android version, nothing remains outside the app. In the iPhone version, the "Refer a friend" referral code and its passphrase, and the marker for people who have been using it for free from before, remain in the iPhone's Keychain (a secure storage area on the device) and carry over when you reinstall the app. All of these exist only on this device and are not sent off it.

As described in 6, the Chrome extension's settings are stored in Chrome's sync feature. Because this is stored in a different place from the records on the device, when you stop using the extension, please also check the settings on the Chrome side.

8. Use by children

This app is not intended for people under 13. The getting-started steps ask for an age range (a range such as "17 and under"), but answering is optional, and we do not ask for your date of birth or exact age.

9. Revisions

If we change this policy, we will announce it on this page and update the revision date. If the handling of data changes, we will also update the App Store privacy label and the Google Play Data safety section.
