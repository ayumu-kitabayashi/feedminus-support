minulo vs LoomWeb: Two Apps That Hide Reels and Shorts (2026)

If you want an alternative to LoomWeb that also covers TikTok, Threads, Reddit and other networks, pick minulo: hiding Reels and Shorts is free and there is no account. LoomWeb is a browser app that hides Reels, Shorts and “For You” feeds on Instagram, YouTube, X and Facebook. It has an Android version, more ratings in the US and a cheaper Pro plan. minulo covers 10 networks, keeps your DMs, can lock the official apps with Screen Time, and has a Mac version.

Updated 2026-10-27

This page is written by Ayumu Kitabayashi, the developer of minulo, so keep that in mind. Everything about LoomWeb comes from its App Store pages (US, Japan and Korea), its website socialalien.tech/loomweb and its Google Play page, checked on 2026-10-27. Features and prices can change.

Pick LoomWeb if

  • you want X’s “For You” feed or Grok gone, or a friends-only Facebook feed;
  • you also use an Android phone;
  • you want a cheaper Pro plan, or a one-time lifetime purchase;
  • you want time limits, widgets and Shortcuts icons in the same app, or prefer an app with more US ratings (35, average 4.8).

Pick minulo if

  • you also use TikTok, Threads, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn or Snapchat;
  • you want everything that hides feeds to stay free, with no limit on setups;
  • you keep opening the official Instagram app out of habit, and want its icon to lead to the Reels-free view instead;
  • you want the app in Japanese, Korean or another language besides English, or want the same thing on a Mac.

What is the difference between LoomWeb and minulo?

Both open social media in their own app and hide Reels, Shorts and recommended feeds, and both keep DMs. LoomWeb covers Instagram, YouTube, X and Facebook, has an Android version and a cheaper Pro plan with a lifetime option, and has more US ratings. minulo covers 10 networks including TikTok, Threads and Reddit, keeps all hiding free, can lock the official apps with Screen Time, and has a Mac version and a 13-language app.

How much does LoomWeb cost?

LoomWeb is free to download, and the free plan includes your first Weave on all supported sites. On the US App Store (checked 2026-10-27), Pro is $2.99 a month, $19.99 a year or $49.99 for life; in Japan it is ¥500, ¥3,000 or ¥8,000. For comparison, minulo’s Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US, and hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts in minulo is free.

Do I need an account?

LoomWeb’s website says “No sign up required”. minulo has no account or sign-up either; you sign in on each network’s own page, so minulo never sees your password.

Which one covers TikTok?

minulo. It hides TikTok’s For You feed and keeps TikTok messages. LoomWeb’s App Store page names Instagram, YouTube, X and Facebook, and not TikTok.

Is LoomWeb on Android?

Yes. Its website links to a Google Play page, “LoomWeb: Browse with Intention” by Social Alien. minulo has no Android version.

Does LoomWeb keep Instagram DMs?

Yes. Its App Store description lists “Limit to DMs only” for Instagram as a free feature. In minulo, Instagram DMs keep working while Reels are hidden, and Every tab but DMs leaves only messages and notifications, also for free.
