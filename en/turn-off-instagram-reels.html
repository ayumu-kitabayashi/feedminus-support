Is there a setting to turn off Reels on Instagram?

We could not find one in Instagram's official help pages as of September 2026. To hide Reels and keep DMs on iPhone, open Instagram in an app such as minulo (hiding is free). The Following feed removes suggested posts from your feed, but the Reels tab stays.

Can Screen Time block only Instagram Reels?

No. Screen Time App Limits work on the whole app, and website blocking works on whole addresses. Blocking Instagram this way also blocks your DMs.

Can I move the Reels tab or change the tab order on Instagram?

We found no setting for that. Instagram has tested its own tab orders: in October 2025 Adam Mosseri said a small group in India could opt in to having Reels and DMs as the first two tabs, and opt right out again.

Why can't an app remove Reels inside Instagram?

iOS keeps each app separate. A third-party app can block or limit another app through Screen Time, but it cannot change what that app shows.
