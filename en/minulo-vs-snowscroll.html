minulo vs snowscroll: a snowscroll alternative that hides Reels and keeps DMs (2026)

snowscroll and minulo work in a similar way: both open the social sites inside their own app with the feeds hidden, keep your messages, and can use Screen Time so the official apps send you to the cleaner version. Pick snowscroll if you want to cut deeper on its six networks, for example the home feed, Stories or comments, and don't mind paying from the start. Pick minulo if you want hiding to be free, or need Threads, Pinterest, Reddit or Snapchat too.

snowscroll, listed on the App Store as “block the feed: snowscroll” and made by Juhyun Nam, calls itself “a calm, focused browser for your social apps.” It loads the mobile sites of Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, TikTok and LinkedIn and removes Reels, Shorts, Explore and For You, while your messages, search and the people you follow stay. Its “shield” uses Screen Time so that opening the real Instagram app takes you to snowscroll instead.

Is minulo a good snowscroll alternative?

Yes, if you want to try it without paying. Both apps open the social sites inside their own app with Reels and Shorts hidden, keep your messages, and can use Screen Time to send you from the official app to the cleaner version. snowscroll has no free version, while hiding is free in minulo, and minulo also covers Threads, Pinterest, Reddit and Snapchat. snowscroll can go further on its six networks, for example hiding the home feed, Stories or comments.

Is snowscroll free?

No. Its US App Store listing says it is a paid app with no free version, sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, and the in-app purchase list shows “snowscroll Pro” at $9.99, $49.99 and $69.99 (checked 2026-11-04). The listing doesn't say which price is which plan. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free, and Pro is $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year.

Which apps does snowscroll work with?

Instagram, YouTube, Facebook, X, TikTok and LinkedIn, according to its App Store listing (checked 2026-11-04). minulo works with Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn and Snapchat.
