How to turn off the TikTok For You page on iPhone (and keep your DMs)

We could not find a setting in TikTok's help that turns off the For You feed. It is the first feed you see when you open TikTok. What TikTok offers instead: Refresh your For You feed, Not interested, Filter keywords, Manage topics, and a daily screen time limit that you get past by typing a passcode shown on the screen. All of these change what For You shows, but none of them removes it. To use TikTok without For You, open it in an app that hides it. In minulo, hiding For You is free and your messages keep working.

How to refresh your For You feed

  1. In the TikTok app, tap Profile at the bottom.
  2. Tap the Menu ☰ button at the top.
  3. Tap Settings and privacy, then Content preferences.
  4. Tap Refresh your For You feed and follow the steps on the screen.

In some regions you can also start it from a post: press and hold the post or tap Share, then tap Why this video › Adjust your For You › Refresh your For You feed. TikTok then shows you some popular posts, and your likes and comments on them shape the new recommendations. A refresh can't be undone, and it only affects For You.

How to filter keywords out of For You

  1. Tap Profile, then the Menu ☰ button.
  2. Tap Settings and privacy › Content preferences › Filter keywords.
  3. Tap Add keyword, type a word or hashtag, and choose the feeds to filter it from, such as For You and Following.
  4. Tap Save. Turn on Smart filter if you also want similar words and variations removed.

You can add up to 100 keywords. TikTok says not every variation is caught and some keywords can't be filtered.

How to tell TikTok you're not interested

  1. On a post in For You, press and hold, or tap Share.
  2. Tap Not interested. TikTok shows you fewer posts like it.
  3. To filter that post's hashtags as well, tap Details, choose an option, tap Details again, select the hashtags and tap Submit.

For broader changes, Manage topics under Content preferences has a slider for each topic. TikTok says it only affects For You and can take some time.

How to set a daily screen time limit on TikTok

  1. Tap Profile, then the Menu ☰ button.
  2. Tap Settings and privacy › Time and well-being › Screen time.
  3. Turn on Daily screen time and pick a limit.

When you hit the limit, TikTok notifies you and you can close the app or type the passcode shown on the screen to keep going. For users aged 13 to 17 it is on by default at 1 hour. On the same screen, Sleep hours mutes TikTok's push notifications after the time you set.

What doesn't turn off For You

  • Refreshing the feed. It resets what For You recommends. The feed is still there and learns from you again.
  • Not interested, keyword filters and topics. They remove some posts. For You keeps playing everything else.
  • Switching to Following or Friends. You choose it each time, the app still opens on For You, and TikTok points you back to For You when those feeds run out.
  • Turning off personalized feeds. TikTok's help says this is only available in some regions, such as the EU. For You then becomes a Popular feed of popular posts, so there is still a feed to scroll.
  • TikTok's daily limit. You get past it by typing a passcode shown on the screen.
  • iPhone Screen Time. It limits the whole app, so your messages stop too.
