Why there is no switch for the Reels tab

Instagram's own statements point the other way. On October 1, 2025, Mosseri wrote that "Reels and DMs have driven most all our growth at Instagram" over the last few years, and that Instagram was exploring making them the first two tabs. He described it as an option for a small group of people in India, said photos from accounts you follow stay in the Home tab, and said people who opt in can opt right out.

Instagram's iPad app was built the same way. When it launched, Instagram said the app drops you into Reels when you open it, with Stories at the top and messages one tap away. A Following tab there offers All, Friends and Latest views.

None of these announcements mention a way to remove the Reels tab, and we found no setting for it in the sources below as of October 2026.

What Instagram does let you change

  1. Open Instagram and go to Home.
  2. Tap Instagram at the top left.
  3. Choose Following, or Favorites for accounts you picked.

Meta says Following shows posts from the people you follow, and both Following and Favorites are in chronological order. This changes what Home shows you. The Reels tab in the bottom bar stays where it is, one tap away.

Can Screen Time hide just the Reels tab?

No. Apple's Screen Time sets time limits for individual apps and for categories such as Social Networking. It has no way to limit one tab inside an app. A limit on Instagram covers everything in it, so DMs are limited too, and Apple says limits can be ignored once reached unless you block them with a Screen Time passcode.
