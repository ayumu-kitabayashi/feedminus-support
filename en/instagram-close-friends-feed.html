Is there a Close Friends feed?

We could not find one in Instagram's help as of October 2026. Close Friends works in one direction: you make a list, and when you share a story you tap Close Friends at the bottom so only people on that list see it. Instagram says stories shared this way disappear after 24 hours, people on your list know they are on it, and nobody can ask to be added. You may also be able to share a reel with the list.

When someone else has put you on their list, Instagram shows a green ring around their profile photo and a Close Friends label on their story. That is a marker on a story, not a separate feed you can open.

How to see only your Favorites

  1. Open Instagram and go to your feed.
  2. Tap Instagram at the top, then tap Favorites.
  3. Tap Favorites list in the top right, tap Search, and find the person.
  4. Tap Add account next to their name. Tap Remove to take someone off.

You can also get there from your profile: tap Menu in the top right, then Favorites below What you see. Meta says you can add up to 50 accounts and that Favorites shows posts in time order. Removing someone from Favorites does not unfollow them.

What Favorites does not change

Favorites is a view of the Home feed. Instagram's help says it moves posts from those accounts higher in your feed, and Meta says the Favorites view shows their latest posts. Neither says it removes the Reels tab, Explore or suggested posts elsewhere in the app. If you open Instagram to check on a few close friends and end up in Reels, Favorites alone will not stop that.
