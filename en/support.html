Frequently asked

I cannot sign in
You sign in on each service’s own official login page. minulo never obtains or stores your ID or password.
If the screen stays blank, you are usually not signed in yet — tap “Sign in” and that service’s login page opens. If you use two-factor authentication, follow that service’s usual steps.
I cannot choose which apps to block
Blocking the official social apps needs Screen Time permission on your iPhone. Check that Settings → Screen Time is on.
iOS shows the permission dialog only once. If you missed it, you can grant it again from “Open Settings” inside the app.
I cannot turn Lock Mode off
While Lock Mode is on, leaving it takes three confirmation steps (copy out a sentence, answer some mental arithmetic, wait 60 seconds). It takes a few minutes.
This is deliberate, and there is no way around it — it exists precisely because you know you will want to unlock later. You can still delete minulo itself at any time.
Something that used to be hidden is showing again
When a service changes its screens, content that was hidden can reappear. The hiding rules are checked automatically every week, but please tell us if you notice something — each case is handled as it is confirmed.
I have to post to social media for work
Posting and replying work inside minulo. The feed and short-form video are not shown, so posting for work does not turn into an hour of scrolling.
Where are my records kept?
Everything is kept on your iPhone only. minulo has no accounts, and the developer cannot see your records.
Which apps you chose to block is hidden by Apple’s own privacy protections, so that is not visible to the developer either.
The service I use is not supported
Ten services are supported today: Instagram, YouTube, X, Threads, Pinterest, TikTok, Facebook, Reddit, LinkedIn and Snapchat. More are added over time — send requests to the address below.
