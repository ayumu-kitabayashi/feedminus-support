How to set up an iPhone Focus that allows only messaging apps (and what happens to Instagram DMs)

An iPhone Focus filters notifications by person and by app. To let only messaging through, go to Settings › Focus, pick or create a Focus, tap Apps › Allow Notifications From and add Messages and the chat apps you use. Instagram is a single app to a Focus: if you add it, every kind of Instagram notification comes through, not only DMs, and if you leave it out, DM notifications are silenced too. To get only DMs, allow Instagram in the Focus and then turn off the other notification types inside Instagram. A Focus filters notifications. It does not stop you from opening Instagram.

Set up a messages only Focus

  1. Open the Settings app and tap Focus.
  2. Tap an existing Focus such as Personal or Work, or tap + and then Custom to make a new one. Give it a name, a color and an icon, tap Next, then Customize Focus.
  3. Tap Apps (or Choose Apps), tap Allow Notifications From, tap +, and pick Messages and the chat apps you need, for example WhatsApp or LINE. Add Instagram only if you want its DMs (see below).
  4. On the same screen, turn off Time Sensitive Notifications if you don't want other apps breaking through.
  5. Tap People (or Choose People) and add anyone whose calls and messages should always reach you.
  6. Optional: choose a Home Screen page without social apps, or tap Add Schedule so the Focus turns on at a set time, place or while using a certain app.

Apple notes that if you turn on Share Focus Status, people who try to contact you, in Messages for example, may see that you have notifications silenced. You can change this in Settings › Focus › Focus Status.

What a messaging only Focus does to Instagram DMs

A Focus decides app by app. Apple's guide lets you allow or silence an app, and does not describe a way to allow only some notifications from one app. That gives you two outcomes:

  • Instagram not in the allowed list: Instagram DMs are silenced along with likes, comments and everything else from Instagram while the Focus is on.
  • Instagram in the allowed list: DMs come through, and so does every other notification Instagram sends.

To get close to DMs only, allow Instagram in the Focus and then turn off the notification types that are not about messages inside Instagram, under your profile › Menu › Notifications. Our guide to Instagram notifications for messages only walks through it. Those Instagram settings apply all the time, not only during the Focus.

Focus filters let apps change what they show during a Focus, for example which mail account is used. We could not confirm that Instagram offers a Focus filter.

A Focus doesn't block apps

Apple describes Focus as a way to silence or allow notifications and to let people know you're busy. Nothing in the Focus settings stops an app from opening, so you can still open Instagram and end up in Reels. The Home Screen option helps by moving the icon you tap out of habit out of sight. To actually block an app, use Screen Time, or the block in minulo below.

Can an iPhone Focus allow only messaging apps?

Yes. In Settings › Focus, choose a Focus, tap Apps, tap Allow Notifications From and add only Messages and your chat apps. Turn off Time Sensitive Notifications if you don't want other apps to break through.

Will Instagram DMs come through a messages only Focus?

Only if Instagram is in the Focus's allowed apps. A Focus allows or silences an app as a whole, so allowing Instagram lets all of its notifications through. To keep just DMs, also turn off the other notification types inside Instagram.

Does a Focus block Instagram?

No. A Focus filters notifications. You can still open Instagram while it is on. You can link a Home Screen page without Instagram to the Focus, and use Screen Time or an app blocker if you want the app itself blocked.

What does Time Sensitive Notifications do in a Focus?

Apple says it lets all apps send time-sensitive notifications immediately. If you want only the apps you chose to reach you, turn it off on the Apps screen of that Focus.

Does minulo send Instagram DM notifications during a Focus?

No. minulo sends no notifications. While minulo locks the official Instagram app, that app's notifications don't arrive either. For DM alerts, keep the official app unlocked and allowed in your Focus.
