How to switch to Following and stay there

  1. Open X and go to Home.
  2. Tap Following at the top, or swipe left from For you.
  3. Close X while you are still on Following.

X's help says that when you close X on the For you or Following tab, it reopens on whichever timeline you had open last. If you check the For you tab even once and then close the app, you are back on For you next time, so switch back before closing.

Can you delete the For you tab on X?

We could not find a way in X's help as of October 2026. Home always has both tabs. X also says it adds recommended posts, accounts to follow and popular content to your timeline, and sends recommendations by push notification and in the Notifications tab, so you can still run into posts from accounts you don't follow.

Mute the topics that pull you in

If one topic keeps dragging you into For you, mute it. Go to Settings and privacy › Privacy and safety › Mute and block › Muted words (you can also get there from the Notifications tab). Add a word, hashtag or @name, choose Home timeline, Notifications or both, and choose how long: forever, 24 hours, 7 days or 30 days.

X's help notes that muted words still show up in search, and that notifications you turned on for a specific account are not muted.
