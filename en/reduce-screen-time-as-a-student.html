How to reduce screen time as a student on iPhone without missing the group chat (2026)

To cut screen time as a student without missing the group chat, hide Reels and Shorts with minulo while Instagram DMs keep working (hiding is free), and use Screen Time to block social apps during class and study hours with Messages still allowed. This page is for students who want to plan phone time around class and study hours.

Most of a student's phone time isn't the problem. Group chats, class apps and maps are things you need. The hours that slip away are usually in Reels, Shorts and TikTok. Apple's Screen Time can block apps at set times, but Apple says limits can be ignored by default once reached, so the setup below includes the step that makes them stick.

Step 1: see where the time goes

On iOS 27, go to Settings › Screen Time › Daily Average and tap Week. The report lists your most used apps and, further down, how many times you picked up the phone. Start with the one or two apps at the top. For context, studies that read college students' phone logs found averages of about 4 to 7 hours a day; see average screen time for college students.

Step 2: block social apps during class and study hours

On iOS 26, go to Settings › Screen Time › Downtime and tap Scheduled. Choose Customize Days to set different hours for class days and weekends. During downtime only calls, messages and the apps you choose to allow are available, and you get a reminder 5 minutes before it starts.

By default you can keep using apps during downtime. To make it a real block, set a Screen Time passcode, then turn on Block at Downtime. Blocked apps appear dimmed with an hourglass icon. Some students ask a roommate to set the passcode, because a code you know is easy to type at midnight.

On iOS 27, Apple replaces this with Screen Time Schedule and Time Allowances: you make time windows for the day and choose which categories, such as Social Media, are available in each. A Time Allowance also covers a website the app declares as associated.

Step 3: keep classmates reachable

Under Screen Time › Always Allowed, add the people and apps you need even during blocked hours, such as Messages, your class app or a family member. If your class group chat lives in Instagram DMs, allowing Instagram also allows Reels, which is the gap minulo fills.

Step 4: batch notifications

Go to Settings › Notifications › Scheduled Summary, turn it on, pick the times, and choose which apps go into the summary. You can also turn off Allow Notifications for any single app.

Step 5: keep DMs, lose the feed (minulo)

How can a student reduce screen time without deleting apps?

Block social apps during class and study hours with a Downtime schedule and Block at Downtime, keep Messages in Always Allowed, and hide Reels and Shorts in minulo so the apps you keep don't pull you into feeds.

What is the best Screen Time setting for studying?

A Downtime schedule (iOS 26) or a Screen Time Schedule (iOS 27) set to your study hours. Turn on Block at Downtime with a passcode, because Apple says limits can be ignored by default.

Will I miss group chat messages during Downtime?

Messages and calls stay available, plus any app you add to Always Allowed. Instagram DMs are blocked unless you allow Instagram. minulo lets you use Instagram DMs with Reels hidden.

Can I set different hours for weekends?

Yes. In Downtime, choose Customize Days and set a schedule for each day of the week.

Is minulo free?

Downloading minulo and hiding Reels and Shorts are free. Locking the official apps is free for up to 2 hours per block. minulo Pro adds longer blocks, schedules and Hard Mode.
