When it usually happens

You send a report, wait for the reply, and pick up the phone. Two reels later the reply has arrived and you haven't seen it. Or a hard email is open on the screen and checking Instagram feels easier than starting it. These gaps are short, and Reels and Shorts are made to fill a minute or two.

Set up a Work Focus

Apple's guide says a Focus lets you choose which apps can send notifications while it is on, and can switch to a customized Home Screen page to help limit distractions.

  1. Open Settings > Focus and tap Work (or add a new Focus).
  2. Under Apps, choose the apps allowed to notify you, such as work chat, email and calendar. Leave social apps out.
  3. Choose a Home Screen page that only has work apps on it.
  4. Set the Focus to turn on during your working hours.

A Focus quiets apps but doesn't block them. If you open Instagram yourself, it opens.

Block social apps during work hours

Screen Time can stop apps from opening: an App Limit for Instagram, TikTok and YouTube with Block at End of Limit, or a Downtime schedule for your working hours with Block at Downtime. If you know the passcode, it is a speed bump you can tap past, but for some people that one extra step is enough. It blocks DMs and everything else in those apps. Move the phone

The cheapest change is distance. During focused work, put the phone in a bag or a drawer, out of reach, and keep work messages on the computer. When picking it up means standing up, fewer of those small checks start.

A workday setup

  1. Morning. The Work Focus turns on when you start, and the phone goes in your bag.
  2. Breaks. Check messages on purpose, at lunch and mid-afternoon.
  3. Social media for work. Use minulo, so the feed isn't there when you post or reply.
  4. Evening. The Focus turns off. If evenings are when you lose time, a free minulo block (up to 2 hours) or a Downtime schedule covers that too.

How do I stop scrolling on my phone at work?

Make the feed harder to reach during work hours. Turn on a Work Focus that keeps social apps quiet and shows a Home Screen page without them, set a Screen Time limit for those apps, and keep the phone out of reach. If you need Instagram for work, hide Reels and Explore instead of blocking the whole app.

Does a Work Focus block apps on iPhone?

No. A Focus controls which apps can send notifications and can switch your Home Screen page, but you can still open any app. To stop apps from opening, use a Screen Time App Limit or Downtime with blocking.

Why do I keep checking my phone at work?

Usually because of short gaps: waiting for a reply, a loading screen, or a task you don't want to start. The phone is within reach, and Reels and Shorts are made to fill a minute or two. Moving the phone and quieting social apps removes many of those prompts.
