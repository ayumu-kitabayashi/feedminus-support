How to switch to the Following feed

  1. Open Instagram and go to your feed (the home tab).
  2. Tap Instagram at the top left.
  3. Tap Following.

Posts and reels from accounts you follow appear with the newest at the top. Meta says there are no suggested posts in this view and that it goes back 30 days, so scrolling to the end catches you up on what you missed.

How to set up Favorites

Favorites is a shorter list for the people you never want to miss.

  1. Tap Instagram at the top left of your feed, then tap Favorites.
  2. Tap Favorites list at the top right.
  3. Tap Search, find the account and tap Add account. To take someone off, tap Remove.

Meta says you can add up to 50 accounts and people are not notified when you add or remove them. Posts from your favorites also show up higher in your main feed.

Can Following be the default?

We could not find a setting for this in Instagram's help as of October 2026. The help page on Feed says that when you exit and reopen Instagram or refresh your feed, the posts predicted to be most relevant to you appear toward the top. So expect to land on the regular feed and choose Following again.

If what you want is fewer suggested posts in the regular feed, you can snooze them for 30 days from Options › Not interested on a suggested post. See how to hide suggested posts on Instagram.

What Following does not change

Following only changes your feed. The Reels tab and Explore keep recommending content from accounts you don't follow. You can change what they show with Not interested or reset your recommendations, but the tabs stay. Instagram has no setting that removes them; see how to hide the Explore page.

What is the difference between Following and Favorites?

Following shows posts from everyone you follow. Favorites shows only the accounts you add to your Favorites list, up to 50, and their posts also appear higher in your main feed. Both are in time order and open from the Instagram menu at the top of your feed.
