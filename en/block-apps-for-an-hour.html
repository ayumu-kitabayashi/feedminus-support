Why Screen Time can't block an app for exactly one hour

Apple describes App Limits as daily time limits. You set them in Settings, Screen Time, App Limits, Add Limit: pick a category or single apps, tap Next and set the amount of time allowed. That counts your use across the day, so it can't say "no Instagram from 3 to 4 pm". Apple also notes that, by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once they are reached.

Downtime is closer. Under Settings, Screen Time, Downtime, Apple offers Turn On Downtime Until Tomorrow or Scheduled. The first starts right away but runs until the next day, and the second needs start and end times set ahead. On iOS 27 the same idea becomes Screen Time Schedule with Time Allowances by category, which Apple says needs iOS 27 or later on all your Apple devices.

Block a set window with Downtime

  1. Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then Always Allowed, and add the apps you need while you study, such as your messaging app.
  2. Go back, tap Downtime, turn on Scheduled, choose Every Day or Customize Days, and set the start and end times.
  3. Set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at Downtime.

Apple says that with Block at Downtime on, restricted apps can't be opened during downtime and appear dimmed with an hourglass. With it off, you only see a reminder. This works when your study hour is the same every day. For a one-off hour, it means editing the schedule each time. If one chat app has to stay open, see block only Instagram during Downtime.

Lock the apps for one study session with minulo

  1. Install minulo, open the Block tab and allow Screen Time.
  2. Choose the official Instagram, TikTok or YouTube app. Apps you don't choose stay open.
  3. Start the block. On the free plan it lasts up to 2 hours, and you can start another as soon as it ends.
  4. If you need the app for a moment, tap Take a break and pick the minutes. The block turns back on by itself when time is up.

One free block covers an hour of reading or a few 25-minute rounds. minulo doesn't count rounds or ring at the break, so keep using your own timer for that.
