ScrollGuard alternatives that hide Reels and keep your Instagram DMs (2026)

ScrollGuard hides Reels, Stories, Explore and endless feeds on social media websites, mainly through Safari on iPhone, and keeps your messages. If you want the same idea in an app of its own, minulo, SocialLite and UNDOOMED open social networks in their own view with Reels and Shorts hidden while DMs keep working, and minulo can also lock the official apps and send you there. Nope is a free Safari extension for Instagram and YouTube. If you would rather slow yourself down than hide anything, one sec adds a pause before an app opens.

What is a good ScrollGuard alternative on iPhone?

If you like ScrollGuard because it hides Reels but keeps DMs, the closest alternatives are apps that do the same in their own view: minulo, SocialLite and UNDOOMED. If you want to stay in Safari, Nope is a free extension for Instagram and YouTube. minulo covers 10 networks, including Threads, Pinterest and Snapchat, and hiding is free.

Does ScrollGuard work inside the official Instagram app on iPhone?

Not according to its own website. ScrollGuard says its Android version runs on top of the native apps, while on iPhone it works through web apps and Safari. On the App Store it is described as a Safari extension. minulo also does not change the official app; it can lock the official Instagram app with Screen Time and send you to minulo, where Reels are hidden.

Is ScrollGuard free?

Yes. ScrollGuard is a free download, and its website says the core blocking works without paying. On the App Store, Premium adds unlimited custom filters. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free on all 10 networks; Pro adds schedules, Hard Mode and longer blocks.

What does ScrollGuard do better, and where is minulo different?

ScrollGuard has an Android version, a Strict Mode that locks settings with a password you can give to someone else, and an adult content filter, and it has 119 US ratings while minulo has none yet. minulo is an app of its own rather than a Safari extension, lists Threads, Pinterest and Snapchat, which ScrollGuard does not list for iPhone, can lock the official apps, and when you open a Reel a friend sent in DMs, it stops before the endless video feed starts, with an option to watch for 5 minutes. minulo has no Android version.
