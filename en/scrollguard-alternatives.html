What's an app like ScrollGuard for iPhone that hides Reels but isn't a Safari extension?

minulo. It is an app of its own: open Instagram inside minulo, tap the gear next to Instagram and turn on Hide the Reels tab and Hide Explore. Reels are gone while DMs, feed and Stories stay, and a Reel a friend sends in DMs stops before the endless feed starts. Hiding is free, no subscription needed.

What is a good ScrollGuard alternative on iPhone?

If you like ScrollGuard because it hides Reels but keeps DMs, the closest alternatives are apps that do the same in their own view: minulo, SocialLite and UNDOOMED. If you want to stay in Safari, Nope is a free extension for Instagram and YouTube. minulo covers 10 networks, including Threads, Pinterest and Snapchat, and hiding is free. For a closer look at the two, see minulo vs ScrollGuard. For other Safari extensions, see minulo vs SocialFocus and minulo vs #blockit. NoReels and snowscroll are other apps that hide Reels and Shorts: see minulo vs NoReels and minulo vs snowscroll.

Does ScrollGuard work inside the official Instagram app on iPhone?

Not according to its own website. ScrollGuard says its Android version runs on top of the native apps, while on iPhone it works through web apps and Safari. On the App Store it is described as a Safari extension. minulo also does not change the official app; it can lock the official Instagram app with Screen Time and send you to minulo, where Reels are hidden.

Is ScrollGuard free?

Yes. ScrollGuard is a free download, and its website says the core blocking works without paying. On the App Store, Premium adds unlimited custom filters. In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free on all 10 networks; Pro adds Sleep mode, Work mode, Hard Mode and blocks longer than 2 hours.

Is ScrollGuard safe?

On the US App Store (checked 2026-11-02), ScrollGuard’s privacy label lists only Data Not Linked to You: usage data (product interaction) used for analytics. Nothing is listed under Data Used to Track You. Its website says feed detection and filtering happen on your device. Apple notes that developers write these labels and Apple does not verify them, and we have not tested the app ourselves. See also SocialLite vs ScrollGuard.

What does ScrollGuard do better, and where is minulo different?

ScrollGuard has an Android version, a Strict Mode that locks settings with a password you can give to someone else, and an adult content filter, and it has 129 US ratings while minulo has 2 (checked 2026-11-11). minulo is an app of its own rather than a Safari extension, lists Threads, Pinterest and Snapchat, which ScrollGuard does not list for iPhone, can lock the official apps, and when you open a Reel a friend sent in DMs, it stops before the endless video feed starts, with an option to watch for 5 minutes. minulo has no Android version.
