YouTube: Shorts feed limit

According to YouTube Help, sign in to the YouTube app, tap the You tab, then Settings › Time management › Shorts feed limit, and select your time limit. The list includes the option to set it to zero. When you reach the limit, a message appears reminding you, and you can dismiss or ignore your set limit. What happens to Shorts in Subscriptions and search when you set it to 0 is covered in YouTube's Shorts feed limit explained.

Instagram: no Reels-only limit

We couldn't find a setting in Instagram's official help that limits only Reels. Its daily limit is a reminder for all of Instagram and doesn't lock the app. More in How to limit only Instagram Reels time.

TikTok: daily screen time

TikTok's daily screen time limit covers all of TikTok, and when time is up you can keep watching by entering the passcode shown on screen. TikTok says every account belonging to a user under 18 is automatically set to a 60-minute daily limit. The steps are in TikTok screen time limit: how to set it and make it stick. If you only open TikTok for messages, it may be simpler to use TikTok for DMs only than to time it.

iPhone Screen Time: it blocks the whole app

In Apple's iOS 26 guide, App Limits sets a daily time for a category of apps (for example, Games or Social Networking) or for individual apps. In the iOS 27 guide, Time Allowances set a daily time for categories like Entertainment, Games or Social Media, and if an app declares an associated website, the Time Allowance applies to both. Either way you can't pick out Reels, and when the app is blocked your DMs are blocked too.

The iOS 26 guide also notes that by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once reached. A passcode you chose yourself is easy to type back in, so see What Ignore Limit does in Screen Time, and how to remove it.

Remove the short-video feeds instead of timing them (minulo)

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. On the home screen, tap Add a service and choose Instagram. You log in on Instagram's own login page. Inside minulo, the Reels and Explore tabs and suggested posts are hidden.
  3. If you use YouTube, open it in minulo too. The Shorts shelf and Shorts tab are gone, while regular videos, search and subscriptions stay. The steps are in how to get rid of YouTube Shorts on iPhone but keep normal videos.
  4. Open the Block tab and choose the official Instagram, YouTube or TikTok app to start a block. Free blocks last up to 2 hours each.
  5. If you open the official app during a block, a Blocked screen appears. Tap Open in minulo to switch to a version without Reels and reply to your DMs.

Hiding is free and has no time limit. Locking the official apps is free for up to 2 hours per block. minulo Pro (US $9.99 a month or $39.99 a year) adds Sleep mode (it locks apps every night from your bedtime to your wake time), Work mode (it locks the apps you pick during your work hours on the days you choose), longer blocks and Hard Mode.

Here are minulo's weak points too. Reels and Shorts are hidden only inside minulo, and the official apps don't change. While minulo blocks an official app, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. You can't post Stories, send voice messages or make calls. minulo is an iPhone app, with a Mac version that works with Safari and Chrome. There is no Android version yet. Can I set a time limit only on short videos?

Few limits work on short videos alone, so minulo hides Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts and TikTok For You instead of timing them, while DMs and regular videos keep working (hiding is free). YouTube's Shorts feed limit sets a time for the Shorts feed only, but when you reach it a reminder appears and you can dismiss or ignore the limit. Instagram's and TikTok's limits cover the whole app.

Where do I set a time limit for Instagram Reels?

We couldn't find a Reels-only time limit in Instagram's official help. Its daily limit is a reminder for the whole app.

If I set the YouTube Shorts feed limit to 0, will Shorts disappear?

According to YouTube Help, when you reach the limit in the Shorts feed a reminder appears and you can dismiss or ignore your set limit. It makes you pause rather than stopping you.

Can Screen Time block only short videos?

No. Screen Time works on whole apps or categories, so blocking Instagram blocks your DMs too.

What can I do instead of a time limit?
