Setup in about two minutes

  1. Install minulo and open Instagram inside it. You log in on Instagram's own page; minulo does not see your password.
  2. Reels, Explore and suggested posts are hidden right away. For messages only, turn on Every tab but DMs in the Instagram rules.
  3. Add YouTube and TikTok the same way if you use them.
  4. Optional: let minulo lock the official apps with Screen Time while you study.

Will I still get group chat notifications?

Notifications come from the official Instagram app. If you keep the official app unlocked, notifications arrive as usual and you can reply in minulo. While minulo blocks the official app with Screen Time, its notifications do not arrive, so only lock it when you don't need them, for example during study time.

Can I hide Reels but keep YouTube for lectures?

Yes. In minulo, YouTube Shorts are hidden while search, subscriptions and normal-length videos stay.

Is it free for students?

Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free on every network. minulo Pro (optional) adds schedules, longer blocks and multiple block lists.
