1. Turn on the break reminder

  1. In the YouTube app, tap your profile picture.
  2. Tap Settings, then General.
  3. Turn on the switch next to Remind me to take a break, pick how often, and tap OK.

According to YouTube Help, the video pauses until you close the reminder or resume playback, so closing it lets you keep watching. It starts on for users aged 13 to 17 and off for users 18 and over. It works only on phones, not tablets. Closing the app, signing out, switching devices or pausing a video for more than 30 minutes resets the timer.

2. Use the Shorts feed limit for Shorts

  1. Tap the You tab, then Settings.
  2. Tap Time management, then Shorts feed limit, and pick a limit. Zero is one of the options.

YouTube Help says that when you reach the limit, a reminder appears in the Shorts feed and you can dismiss it or ignore your limit. What still shows up at zero is covered in YouTube Shorts Feed Limit: How to Set It to 0. Paying for Premium doesn't hide them; see does YouTube Premium remove Shorts.

3. Cut down Autoplay and Home recommendations

Tap your profile picture, then Settings at the top right, then Autoplay, and switch it off. The next video then won't start by itself. Google says Autoplay can be set differently on each device.

On Home, YouTube Help says that if you have little relevant watch history and have chosen not to get recommendations based on it, features like recommendations on the YouTube homepage are removed. See How to Turn Off YouTube Autoplay and Recommendations and YouTube Watch History Off: What Happens to Home.

4. Set a time limit with Screen Time (whole app)

In Apple's iOS 27 guide, Screen Time › Time Allowances sets how long you can use each category of apps per day, such as Entertainment or Social Media. On iOS 26, you tap App Limits › Add Limit and set a time for a category or for individual apps.

Either way the limit covers the whole YouTube app, so lectures and subscriptions are blocked too. Apple's iOS 26 guide also notes that by default, Screen Time limits can be ignored once reached. To lock apps only during class hours, see how to stop checking Instagram in class.

Where do I turn on the YouTube break reminder?

YouTube Help says to tap your profile picture, then Settings, then General, turn on Remind me to take a break and pick how often. It works only on phones.

Can I quit just YouTube Shorts?

In the YouTube app you can set the Shorts feed limit to zero, but it is a reminder you can dismiss or ignore. In minulo, the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab are gone and regular videos still play.
