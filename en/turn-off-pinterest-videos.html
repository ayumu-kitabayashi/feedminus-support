Turn off autoplay in the Pinterest app

  1. Open the Pinterest app and tap your profile picture at the bottom right.
  2. Tap the settings (nut) icon at the top right.
  3. Tap Social permissions.
  4. Switch off Autoplay videos on cellular data.

Pinterest's help also gives a second route for the app: tap your profile picture at the bottom right, then your profile picture at the top left, then Privacy and data, and switch off Auto-play videos on cellular data. The page doesn't make clear to us which device each route is for, so if you don't see Social permissions, try Privacy and data.

Pinterest says video Pins start playing on your phone once half of the Pin is in view, and sound follows your device settings.

Turn off autoplay on a computer

  1. Log in on pinterest.com.
  2. Click the more-options icon at the bottom left and choose Settings.
  3. Click Social permissions in the left menu.
  4. Under Autoplay videos, switch off Autoplay videos on desktop.
  5. Click Save at the bottom of the page.

Can you remove video Pins from your Pinterest feed?

We could not find a way in Pinterest's help as of October 2026. Pinterest says video Pins appear in your home feed and in search results, and the only video settings it describes are the autoplay switches above. Turning autoplay off means a video waits for a tap, but the Pin is still in your feed.

Refine your recommendations (profile picture › more-options icon at the top right) has two tabs. AI content lets you see fewer AI-generated Pins, by topic or all at once with Show AI content. Activity turns off recommendations based on Pins you engaged with. Pinterest doesn't say either one affects video Pins, and changing them doesn't change the ads you see.

How do I turn off autoplay videos on Pinterest?
