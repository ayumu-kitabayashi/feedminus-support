What you get without paying in SocialLite

As a new user, nothing. SocialLite's FAQ says new signups need a paid subscription for access and there is no universal free tier; subscriptions and access already granted to an account keep their existing terms. A free trial isn't guaranteed and depends on your eligibility, plan and country (checked 2026-11-10). What the subscription covers is what its App Store description lists: it hides short video feeds, recommendations, Explore, Live and Shopping tabs and ads, keeps DMs, and can block the official apps. The price you see can differ from someone else's, because the App Store page shows several offers.

How to hide Reels for free instead

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. Open Instagram inside minulo and sign in on Instagram's own page. minulo does not receive your password.
  3. Reels, Explore and suggested posts can be hidden from the gear next to Instagram. For messages only, turn on Every tab but DMs.
  4. Add YouTube and TikTok the same way. Shorts and For You can be hidden for free too.
  5. Optional: in the Block tab, let minulo lock the official apps with Screen Time, free for up to 2 hours per block.

Where SocialLite is still the better pick

SocialLite has push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat, which minulo doesn't, and far more ratings (17,607 in the US, average 4.7, checked 2026-11-10). If notifications matter more to you than the price, it is worth trying SocialLite first.

Limits, so you can decide honestly: minulo can't change the official apps, so Reels and Shorts are hidden only inside minulo. While minulo blocks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. Posting Stories, voice messages and calls aren't available, because the web versions of these networks don't have them. minulo runs on iPhone and Mac (the Mac app works in Safari and Chrome), not Android, and it is new, with few ratings.
