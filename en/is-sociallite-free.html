What you get without paying in SocialLite

SocialLite's App Store description lists what the app does (it hides short video feeds, recommendations, Explore, Live and Shopping tabs and ads, keeps DMs, and can block the official apps), but it doesn't split those features into free and paid. The only way to know for your account is to download it and see what the app asks you to pay for. The price you see can also differ from someone else's, because the App Store page shows several offers.

How to hide Reels for free instead

  1. Install minulo from the App Store.
  2. Open Instagram inside minulo and sign in on Instagram's own page. minulo does not receive your password.
  3. Reels, Explore and suggested posts can be hidden from the gear next to Instagram. For messages only, turn on Every tab but DMs.
  4. Add YouTube and TikTok the same way. Shorts and For You can be hidden for free too.
  5. Optional: in the Block tab, let minulo lock the official apps with Screen Time, free for up to 2 hours per block.

Where SocialLite is still the better pick

SocialLite has push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat, which minulo doesn't, and far more ratings (16,883 in the US, average 4.7, checked 2026-11-04). If notifications matter more to you than the price, it is worth trying SocialLite first.

Limits, so you can decide honestly: minulo can't change the official apps, so Reels and Shorts are hidden only inside minulo. While minulo blocks an official app, that app's notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own. Posting Stories, voice messages and calls aren't available, because the web versions of these networks don't have them. minulo runs on iPhone and Mac (the Mac app works in Safari and Chrome), not Android, and it is new, with few ratings. Is there a free app like SocialLite?

Nope is completely free but only works in Safari on instagram.com and youtube.com. Among apps that work like SocialLite, minulo, Stillfeed, Reelmute and Awhile hide Reels for free and charge only for extras. minulo also has a free messages-only setting for Instagram and TikTok.

Can I get notifications with a free option?

In minulo, yes, as long as you leave the official app unblocked: notifications come from the official app as usual and you can reply inside minulo. While minulo blocks the official app, its notifications don't arrive. SocialLite has its own push notifications for Instagram and Snapchat.

What does minulo Pro add?

Sleep mode (set bed and wake times), Work mode (set workdays and hours), blocks longer than 2 hours, Hard Mode and a Daily limit. Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts stays free with no time limit.
