What YouTube's own comment settings do

YouTube Help's page on comment settings is written for channel owners. A creator can choose to turn comments on, pause them or turn them off for a specific video, and can hide particular users across all videos on the channel. When a creator turns comments off, viewers can't comment on that video. Those are the creator's choices. The page doesn't describe any option for you, as a viewer, to hide comments on other people's videos.
