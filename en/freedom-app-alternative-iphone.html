What Freedom does on iPhone

The US App Store page for Freedom: Screen Time Control says it blocks the apps and websites on your blocklist during a session, with a pre-made or custom blocklist, sessions you can schedule or repeat, a Locked Mode, and blocks that sync across your devices. Freedom's support site says apps are blocked with Apple's built-in Screen Time controls, and websites through a local VPN configuration called the Freedom Profile, which works across browsers.

That is a good tool if you want the internet quiet for a set time. The catch for many students is that Instagram, Snapchat and TikTok are also where the group chat lives. When Freedom blocks Instagram, the DMs go with it.

When Freedom is still the better pick

Choose Freedom if you need websites blocked across every browser, if you want the same session on your phone and computer, or if you want a full block with no way in. minulo doesn't block websites, and it is built for the opposite case: you keep using Instagram and YouTube for messages and subscriptions, and only the endless feeds are gone.

Replace a Freedom session with minulo
