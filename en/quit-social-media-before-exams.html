Why exam weeks go wrong

You sit down after dinner with a past paper. The class group chat pings, you open Instagram to read it, someone has sent a reel, and an hour later you are still on question 2. Your willpower didn't suddenly fail. The chat you need and the feed you don't are in the same app, one tap apart.

How to deactivate Instagram for exams

Instagram lets you deactivate your account for a while. Its help page says your photos, comments and likes are hidden until you reactivate by logging back in, and that you can only deactivate your profile once a week.

  1. In Instagram, open Settings and tap Accounts Center.
  2. Tap Manage account (or Accounts, depending on your Accounts Center), then Manage next to your profile.
  3. Tap Deactivation or deletion, choose Deactivate account and follow the steps.

The catch: to read a DM you have to log in, and logging in reactivates the account. Deactivating suits a full break when classmates can reach you some other way, such as iMessage, WhatsApp or LINE.

Delete the app or just log out?

Deleting the app keeps your account and DMs, and you can reinstall it any time. It helps because the icon is gone, so opening Instagram becomes a decision. It doesn't stop instagram.com in Safari. To block that too, add it to Screen Time's web content limits (steps in How to block Instagram on iPhone).

Logging out only adds one step, your password. If your login is saved, it adds almost nothing.

Lock it with Screen Time during study hours

An App Limit with Block at End of Limit, or a Downtime schedule with Block at Downtime, stops Instagram from opening at the times you choose. It only holds if you don't know the Screen Time passcode, so some students ask a parent or a sibling to set it. It blocks DMs too, and the website still opens unless you limit it as well. The steps are in How to block Instagram on iPhone.

A plan for the last two weeks

  1. Decide which chats you really need. Tell friends you will reply at a set time, such as 9 pm.
  2. Move social apps you don't need for chats (TikTok, YouTube) off the Home Screen, or delete them.
  3. For Instagram, hide Reels and Explore if people message you there. If nobody does, deactivate.
  4. Block the official apps for each study session, 1 to 2 hours at a time.
  5. Put the day your exams end in your calendar and undo everything then. Knowing when it ends makes it easier to start.

Should I deactivate Instagram before exams?

Deactivate if you don't need Instagram for messages. Your profile is hidden until you log back in, and Instagram only allows it once a week. If your class chat is on Instagram, logging in to read it reactivates the account, so hiding the feed and keeping DMs works better.

Can I still use Instagram DMs while my account is deactivated?

No. To read DMs you have to log in, and logging back in reactivates the account. If you need DMs, keep the account and remove the feed instead, for example with minulo's free Every tab but DMs setting.

How often can I deactivate Instagram?

Instagram's help says you can only deactivate your profile once a week. Logging back in reactivates it.

Is it better to delete Instagram or log out during exams?

If your password is saved, deleting the app helps more than logging out because the icon is gone. Neither stops instagram.com in Safari, and both cut you off from DMs in the app. A Screen Time limit with a passcode someone else knows is harder to undo.
