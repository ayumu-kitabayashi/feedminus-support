Screen Time limit not working for Instagram on iPhone? How to fix it (2026)

When a Screen Time limit for Instagram doesn't stop you, there are five usual causes: limits can be ignored by default, you are opening instagram.com in Safari, iOS 27 changed how limits work, devices in Family Sharing are not up to date, or Instagram is on the Always Allowed list. Check them in this order.

Why is my Instagram Screen Time limit not working?

Most often because Screen Time limits can be ignored by default. Set a Screen Time passcode and turn on Block at End of Limit so the app can't be used without the passcode. Also check Safari (instagram.com), Always Allowed and, on iOS 27, Time Allowances.

Why can I still open Instagram in Safari?

An app limit applies to the app. On iOS 26, add instagram.com under Never Allow in Content & Privacy Restrictions > Web Content. On iOS 27, a Time Allowance also covers a website the app declares as associated.

Can Screen Time limit only Instagram Reels?

No. Screen Time limits apply to whole apps (categories on iOS 27), and website blocking applies to whole addresses. To stop only Reels, use an app that hides them.

Why don't Screen Time limits work on my child's iPhone?

Update every device in your Family Sharing group to the latest software and set Screen Time from the parent's device. The new iOS 27 controls only work when every device in the group runs iOS 27 or later.
