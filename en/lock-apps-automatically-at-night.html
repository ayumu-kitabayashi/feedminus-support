How to lock Instagram, TikTok and YouTube automatically at night on iPhone (2026)

minulo locks the official Instagram, TikTok or YouTube app with Screen Time for free, up to 2 hours per block, and minulo Pro starts the block by itself every night.

With Pro, Sleep mode locks them every night from your bedtime to your wake time. Among iPhone's own settings, Downtime with Block at Downtime is the one that stops apps at set times. Apple's iOS 27 user guide doesn't list this option. Apple describes Sleep Focus as filtering notifications and calls, Require Face ID only asks for your own face or passcode, and in Apple's Shortcuts guide we couldn't find an action that closes or blocks another app. So Sleep Focus or a Shortcuts automation alone won't keep Reels closed at night.

Downtime: the built-in way to lock apps at a set time

  1. Open Settings, tap Screen Time, then Always Allowed, and add the messaging apps you need at night. Leave Instagram, TikTok and YouTube out.
  2. Go back, tap Downtime, turn on Scheduled and set the days and times.
  3. Turn on Block at Downtime. Apple's iOS 27 user guide doesn't list this option. Apple says you can choose this once you have set a Screen Time passcode.

Apple's guide says that during downtime, only calls, messages and apps you choose to allow are available. With Block at Downtime off, you only see a reminder. On iOS 27 the same idea is called Screen Time Schedule, which works by Time Allowance categories, and Apple says it needs iOS 27 or later on all your Apple devices. For keeping one chat app open, see block only Instagram during Downtime. If Instagram still opens, see Downtime not blocking Instagram.

Does Sleep Focus lock apps?

Apple says a Sleep Focus helps reduce distractions before and during bedtime: it can filter out notifications and phone calls, and signal to others that you're not available. When you set up a Sleep Focus, it follows the sleep schedule you set in the Health app.

  1. Go to Settings, tap Focus, then Sleep.
  2. Tap Apps to choose which apps can still send notifications. Leave Instagram, TikTok and YouTube out.
  3. Choose a Lock Screen or Home Screen page to switch to while the Focus is on, and use a page without those apps.

That takes the apps out of sight and quiets them, but Apple's guide doesn't say a Focus stops an app from opening. If you want the app itself closed at night, use Downtime or a blocker. For something to do in place of the phone, see what to do instead of scrolling before bed.

Require Face ID: a lock, but one you can open

You can touch and hold an app on the Home Screen and tap Require Face ID (or Touch ID or Passcode). Apple says locking an app requires Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode to open it, and it relocks after you quit. Hide and Require Face ID also moves it to the Hidden folder in App Library.

It is your own face, so it adds a step but doesn't stop you, and it is not tied to a time of night. Apple also notes that children under 13 in a Family Sharing group can't lock or hide apps.

Can a Shortcuts automation close Instagram at night?

Apple's Shortcuts guide says automations run automatically when a trigger happens, such as a specific time of day. On iOS 26, open the Shortcuts app, tap Automation, tap the add button, then Create Personal Automation, and choose a trigger such as Time of Day. On iOS 27, tap a shortcut, tap Edit, then Automation, and choose the trigger there. Apple's iOS 27 guide lists Time of Day, Sleep and App among the automations that can run without asking.

What we couldn't find in Apple's guide is an action that closes, locks or blocks another app. So an automation can fire at 11 pm, but it has no built-in way to keep Instagram shut. For the lock itself, use Downtime or a blocker app.

Does Sleep Focus block apps?

Apple describes Sleep Focus as filtering notifications and calls and showing that you're unavailable. Its guide doesn't say a Focus stops an app from opening. You can switch to a Home Screen page without Instagram while it's on.

Can a Shortcuts automation close or lock an app at a set time?

Automations can run at a time of day, but we couldn't find an action in Apple's Shortcuts guide that closes, locks or blocks another app. Use Downtime or a blocker app for the lock.

Does Require Face ID stop me from opening Instagram at night?

No. It asks for Face ID, Touch ID or your passcode each time you open the app, so you can still open it yourself, and it is not tied to a time of night.
