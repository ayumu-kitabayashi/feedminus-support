minulo vs Kahf Guard: a Reels blocker or a family content filter (2026)

These two apps solve different problems. Pick Kahf Guard if you want a filter for the whole phone or the whole family: it blocks adult content, gambling and dating sites at the DNS level, blocks apps and websites, enforces safe search, and lists Reels and Shorts blocking for YouTube, Instagram and Facebook, with personal plans at $5 a month or $48 a year. Pick minulo if what you want is Reels, Shorts and TikTok's For You hidden while your DMs keep working, on 10 networks, without paying.

Kahf Guard, listed on the App Store as “Kahf Guard: Parental Control” and sold by Halalz, describes itself as a Muslim-focused screen time and content blocker. Its listing includes an app blocker, a website blocker for all browsers, “Block Reels and Shorts: Stop infinite scrolling on YouTube, Instagram, and Facebook”, DNS-level blocking of adult content, gambling, dating sites and ads, safe search on Google, Bing and DuckDuckGo, YouTube Restricted Mode, a habit tracker, parental controls for several devices, and uninstall protection with a PIN and a 24-hour cooldown. It also has an Android version. Its iOS listing doesn't explain how Reels and Shorts are blocked on iPhone, and doesn't say whether Instagram DMs keep working.

Is minulo a good Kahf Guard alternative?

For hiding Reels and Shorts while keeping DMs, yes. minulo hides Reels, Shorts and TikTok's For You on 10 networks and keeps DMs, and hiding is free. It is not a content filter or parental control app, so if you want adult content, gambling and dating sites blocked, safe search enforced or uninstall protection, Kahf Guard does those things and minulo does not.
