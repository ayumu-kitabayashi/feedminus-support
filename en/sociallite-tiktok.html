Why TikTok is the gap

Most apps in this category started with Instagram and YouTube. Of the two best-known ones, SocialLite doesn't mention TikTok on its App Store page and UNDOOMED lists it as unavailable. So if TikTok is the app you open at night, check the network list before you pay for anything.

Hide For You with minulo

  1. Install minulo and add TikTok from the home screen.
  2. Sign in on TikTok's own page inside minulo.
  3. Tap the gear next to TikTok and turn on Recommendations (Hide For You, Explore and Create). Hide suggested accounts is there too.
  4. To keep only messages, choose Every tab but DMs. That hides Following as well.
  5. Optional: lock the official TikTok app with Screen Time, free for up to 2 hours per block.

Messages and Following open straight from the minulo home screen. While the official TikTok app is locked, its notifications don't arrive, and minulo sends none of its own.

Does SocialLite support TikTok?

Its US App Store description and release notes don't mention TikTok. They name Instagram, YouTube, Snapchat, X, Reddit and others.

Does UNDOOMED support TikTok?

No. UNDOOMED lists TikTok and Snapchat as unavailable. It covers Instagram, Threads, Reddit, Facebook, X, YouTube and LinkedIn.

Can I turn off For You in the TikTok app?

TikTok has no setting that removes it. Restricted Mode filters some videos but For You keeps playing, and Daily screen time is a reminder you can pass with an on-screen passcode.

Which app hides TikTok For You and keeps messages?

minulo does, for free: TikTok opens with For You, Explore and Create hidden, and messages and Following keep working. Reelmute also says it hides For You, with DMs-only as a Premium feature.

Is there one app for Instagram and TikTok?

minulo covers both, plus YouTube, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn and Snapchat. If you only use Instagram and want notifications, SocialLite is still a strong choice.
