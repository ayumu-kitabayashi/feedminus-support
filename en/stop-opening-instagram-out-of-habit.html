How to stop opening Instagram out of habit on iPhone, without deleting it (2026)

You don't have to delete Instagram to stop opening it out of habit. On iPhone you can keep the app and make the usual icon lead somewhere without Reels.

Most people who want less Reels don't decide to scroll; their thumb opens Instagram on its own. Deleting the app works until you need a group chat, and a Screen Time app limit blocks DMs along with everything else.

If you want the icon to open Instagram without Reels

I'm the developer of minulo, so this part is about my own app. With minulo and Screen Time, tapping the Instagram icon shows a block screen with an Open in minulo button, which opens Instagram without Reels while DMs keep working.

  1. Install minulo and open Instagram inside it once, logging in on Instagram's own page.
  2. Allow Screen Time when minulo asks, and choose the official Instagram app (and YouTube or TikTok if you like) to lock.
  3. Next time your thumb taps the Instagram icon, you get the block screen. Tap Open in minulo to reach Instagram without Reels, Explore or suggested posts.

Hiding Reels is free. Free blocks of the official app last up to 2 hours at a time and can be started again any time; blocks longer than 2 hours, Sleep mode and Work mode are part of minulo Pro.

Do I have to delete Instagram?

No. The official app can stay installed and be locked with Screen Time, so tapping the icon doesn't open the feed.

Can I still get to my DMs quickly?

Yes. Tap Open in minulo on the block screen; your DMs and group chats work there.

Is this the same as an app limit?

No. An app limit blocks all of Instagram once time is up. Hiding only Reels, Explore and suggested posts keeps the rest usable.
