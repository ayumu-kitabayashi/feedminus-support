Our pick is minulo: it hides Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts on iPhone while your DMs keep working, and hiding is free. Several other apps do the same in different ways.

Hiding stays free. Pro (optional) adds Sleep mode, which blocks social apps from your bedtime until morning, Work mode, which locks the apps you pick during your work hours on the days you choose, plus Hard Mode, a daily limit and blocks longer than 2 hours.

Several iPhone apps hide Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts while keeping DMs. Most work in one of two ways: an in-app browser (minulo, SocialLite, UNDOOMED, Awhile, LoomWeb) or a Safari extension (Nope; ScrollGuard works both as a Safari extension and in its own app). minulo and SocialLite can also lock the official apps with Screen Time and send you to their Reels-free view. Stillfeed (free, since version 1.2.6) and Reelmute (Premium) can lock the official apps too.

Which one is the best?

It depends on what you need. SocialLite has by far the most ratings (17,607 in the US, checked 2026-11-10) and sends push notifications. If you only use Instagram, Awhile is simple, and Nope is completely free in Safari. If you want more networks, minulo covers the most (10, including TikTok and Snapchat), and hiding is free. Fluid Friction and Stillfeed list 8, and UNDOOMED 7.

What do the App Store ratings say?

On the US App Store (November 2026), SocialLite has 17,607 ratings (checked 2026-11-10), SocialFocus 326, ScrollGuard 127, UNDOOMED 108 and Dull 58; the others have fewer than 40. minulo is new and has 2 US ratings so far. The number of ratings shows how many people have tried an app, not whether it hides what you need, so check the networks and price columns too.

Which app hides Instagram Reels but keeps DMs?

SocialLite, UNDOOMED, Awhile, LoomWeb, Nope and minulo all say DMs keep working while Reels are hidden. Awhile covers Instagram only; Nope covers Instagram and YouTube; UNDOOMED, LoomWeb and minulo cover several networks. minulo supports 10, including TikTok and Snapchat.

Can any of these apps remove Reels inside the official Instagram app?

Only WallHabit says it can block parts of apps such as Reels and Shorts, and its description does not explain how. The other apps show Instagram in their own browser or in Safari rather than changing the official app. Some can lock the official app instead: minulo and SocialLite with Screen Time, Stillfeed for free since version 1.2.6, and Reelmute with Premium. When you try to open a locked Instagram app, minulo sends you to its own Instagram, where Reels are hidden.

Which option is completely free?

Nope, StillTabs and FeedOff have nothing to buy: no subscription and no in-app purchases (Nope works only in Safari, on Instagram and YouTube; StillTabs covers 5 networks, without TikTok; FeedOff covers 6). In minulo, hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free on all 10 networks, with no subscription needed and no time limit; Pro is optional and adds Sleep mode, Work mode, Hard Mode and a Daily limit.

What is a good SocialLite alternative?

SocialLite has by far the most ratings (17,607 in the US, checked 2026-11-10). If you want another app that keeps DMs and hides short videos, minulo covers the most networks (10). minulo also covers TikTok, Threads, Pinterest and LinkedIn and has a Mac app that also works in Chrome, but it is new and has only 1 US rating so far.

Is minulo safe to log in with?

You log in on each network's official web page inside minulo. minulo does not receive or store your ID or password, and it has no account of its own. Usage analytics are anonymous and not linked to you.

Does it work on Android or iPad?

minulo is iPhone only: there is no Android or iPad version. On a computer, use the Mac app (to use it in Chrome, add the extension from inside the Mac app).

Do I still get notifications from the official app while it's blocked?

No. While the official app is blocked with Screen Time, its push notifications don't arrive either, so you check your messages inside minulo. SocialLite can send Instagram and Snapchat notifications, so it suits people who don't want to miss them.

Does SocialLite block the Instagram app itself?

Yes, according to its release notes SocialLite has app blocking and linking. minulo works in a similar way: it locks the official apps with Screen Time and sends you to minulo instead. Neither app can change what the official Instagram app shows, because iPhone apps can't modify other apps.
