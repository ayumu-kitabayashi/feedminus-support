Set the Shorts feed limit to 0

In the YouTube app, go to You › Settings › Time management › Shorts feed limit and choose 0 minutes. It is the quickest free step, but it is a reminder, not a lock: an adult account can dismiss the limit and keep watching. YouTube's help gives the steps for the YouTube app only. On Home you can also tap More on a grid of Shorts and choose Show fewer Shorts.

Hide Shorts with minulo and keep normal videos

  1. Install minulo, tap Add a service on the home screen and choose YouTube.
  2. Tap the gear next to YouTube and turn on Hide the Shorts shelf, Hide the Shorts tab and Send Shorts links to the normal player. Home recommendations is there too, if you only want subscriptions.
  3. Subscriptions, Watch later and search open straight from the minulo home screen.
  4. Optional: in the Block tab, lock the official YouTube app with Screen Time, free for up to 2 hours per block. Its Open in minulo button takes you to YouTube without Shorts.

Is there a free app to block YouTube Shorts on iPhone?

Yes. minulo, Stillfeed and Reelmute hide Shorts for free in their own app and charge only for extras. Nope is completely free but works only on youtube.com in Safari. ShortformBlock and Untethered are also free Safari extensions that block Shorts, in Safari only; see minulo vs ShortformBlock and minulo vs Untethered.

Can I remove Shorts but keep normal YouTube videos?

Not in the YouTube app itself. Open YouTube in minulo with the Shorts shelf and Shorts tab hidden; normal videos, subscriptions, Watch later and search keep working.

Does setting the Shorts limit to 0 block Shorts?

It shows a limit screen once you reach 0 minutes, but an adult account can dismiss it and keep watching. YouTube's help describes it for the YouTube app.

Can any app remove Shorts from the official YouTube app?

No. On iPhone no app can change what another app shows. minulo can lock the official YouTube app with Screen Time and send you to its Shorts-free view instead. While it is locked, YouTube notifications don't arrive.

Is Screen Time a good free option?

It is the strictest free option with a passcode someone else sets, but it blocks the whole YouTube app, including the videos you wanted to watch.
