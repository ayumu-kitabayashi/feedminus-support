Turn off autoplay in the YouTube app on iPhone

  1. Tap your profile picture.
  2. At the top right of the screen, tap Settings.
  3. Tap Autoplay.
  4. Switch autoplay off.

You can also tap the Autoplay switch at the top of the video player on any watch screen. If autoplay is on and you only want to stop the next video, tap Cancel at the end of the video.

  • Autoplay can be set differently on each device. Turning it off on your computer doesn't turn it off in the iPhone app.
  • For users aged 13 to 17, autoplay is off by default. On a supervised account, you may not be able to change it if a parent or guardian has turned it off.
  • On a mobile network, autoplay stops on its own after 30 minutes without activity. On Wi‑Fi, it stops after 4 hours.

Remove recommendations from the YouTube Home feed

To pause watch history in the YouTube app on iPhone:

  1. Sign in to the YouTube app.
  2. Tap your profile picture.
  3. Tap Settings, then Manage all history.
  4. Tap the Controls tab.
  5. Unselect "Include the YouTube videos you watch."

YouTube's help says Home recommendations are removed when you have no relevant or significant recent watch history and have said you don't want recommendations based on that history. That is why it pairs turning history off with deleting it. In Manage all history, tap the delete icon next to a video, or use DELETE to remove more at once. Using DELETE also deletes your search history for the time frame you choose.

Tune what YouTube recommends

If you want to keep recommendations but see less of something, tap More (⋮) next to a video on Home and choose Not interested, or choose Don't recommend channel for a channel you don't want. To see fewer Shorts, scroll to a grid of Shorts on Home, tap More at the top of the grid, and tap Show fewer Shorts. You can clear this feedback later in Google's My Activity.

How do I turn off autoplay on YouTube on iPhone?

In the YouTube app, tap your profile picture, tap Settings at the top right, tap Autoplay and switch it off. You can also use the Autoplay switch at the top of the video player.

How do I turn off recommendations on the YouTube homepage?

YouTube's help says that if you don't want video recommendations on Home, you can delete and turn off your watch history. To pause it on iPhone, tap your profile picture, then Settings, Manage all history, the Controls tab, and unselect "Include the YouTube videos you watch." Home recommendations are removed when you have no relevant recent watch history and have said you don't want recommendations based on it.

Why does YouTube still autoplay after I turned it off?

Autoplay is set separately on each device. Turning it off on your computer doesn't turn it off in the YouTube app on your iPhone, so check Settings, then Autoplay, in the app itself.

How do I stop YouTube from playing another video at the end?

Turn off Autoplay, or tap Cancel at the end of a video while Autoplay is on. YouTube's help also says the next video won't play automatically if you scroll past the video player or start typing, for example a comment or a search.

Can I hide YouTube recommendations without deleting my watch history?

The way YouTube's help gives for removing Home recommendations is deleting and turning off watch history. Not interested and Don't recommend channel only change what gets recommended.
