📸 Can I hide Instagram Reels and still DM my friends?

Yes. When you open Instagram inside minulo, the Reels and Explore tabs and suggested posts are not shown. Your DMs, your friends' posts, your own posts and profiles keep working. You choose what to hide one item at a time, and hiding is free.

💬 Can I use Instagram only for DMs and group chats?

Yes. Turn on "Every tab but DMs" for Instagram (TikTok has the same setting). Only your DMs and the notifications tab stay; Reels, Explore, the home feed and suggested posts are gone. Most people who use minulo are students who keep Instagram for class group chats.

📱 Can an app remove Reels inside the official Instagram app?

On iPhone, no third-party app can change what another app shows. minulo handles the "the real app is one tap away" problem with Screen Time: it locks the official Instagram, YouTube or TikTok app, and tapping that icon shows a block screen whose "Open in minulo" button leads to minulo's version without Reels or Shorts. You don't need to delete the official apps.

▶️ Can I hide YouTube Shorts and still watch normal videos?

Yes. When you open YouTube inside minulo, the Shorts shelf on the home screen and the Shorts tab are not shown. Search, your subscriptions and regular-length videos keep working. Hiding Shorts is free.

🔒 What happens when I tap the regular Instagram or TikTok icon?

minulo uses iPhone Screen Time to stop the official apps you choose from opening. When you try to open one, a "Blocked" screen appears, and its "Open in minulo" button takes you to minulo instead. Choosing apps and turning blocking on are free.

🔔 Do I get Instagram DM notifications?

While minulo is blocking the official Instagram app, Instagram notifications don't arrive, and minulo doesn't send notifications in their place. If you want DM notifications, don't block the official app. Notifications then arrive as usual, and you can reply from the DMs inside minulo, where Reels are hidden.

📱 Besides Instagram and YouTube, which apps can I clean up?

minulo supports 10 services: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn and Snapchat. For each service, you choose what to hide one item at a time, such as Reels, Shorts, suggested posts or Explore tabs.

🔑 Do I have to give minulo my Instagram password?

minulo never receives your ID or password. You sign in directly on each service's official sign-in page. minulo does not collect or store your ID, password or the contents of your DMs, and all of your records stay on your iPhone.

🔐 Do I have to log in to Instagram again every time I open it?

No. An earlier bug logged you out of Instagram every time you closed the app. We fixed it in version 1.0.15, released in late October 2026. If you still keep having to log in again, update minulo to the latest version in the App Store. If it still happens after updating, let us know with "Send feedback" in the app's settings. We'll look into it right away.

✋ Can I still watch a Reel a friend sends me in DMs?

When you open a Reel or Short sent to you in a DM, minulo pauses before the endless vertical video feed begins. If you really want to watch it, tap "Open for 5 min" to watch for five minutes. After five minutes, blocking turns back on automatically.

🆓 Is hiding Reels free? What does Pro add?

Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts, and blocking the official apps, are free. A free block lasts up to 2 hours, and you can start another one whenever it ends. minulo Pro adds Sleep mode (set bed and wake times; until morning your blocked apps stay shut and social media inside minulo closes too), Work mode (set workdays and hours), Hard Mode, which makes turning blocking off take effort, a Daily limit for social media (once it is used up, it opens again after 5 a.m.), and blocks that last until you turn them off. Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency. Pro is a monthly or yearly subscription, and the yearly plan comes with a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. 💼 I post on Instagram for work. Can I still use minulo?

Yes. Posting photos, commenting and replying to DMs work inside minulo. Posting Stories, voice messages and calls are not available because the web versions of these services do not have them, but "Take a break" opens the official app for the minutes you choose.

🔁 Are FeedMinus and minulo the same app?

Yes, they are the same app. FeedMinus was renamed minulo in September 2026. The App Store page, the features and what is free have not changed. If you installed it under the old name, updating the app keeps everything and shows the new name.

🌏 Which languages is minulo in? Is there an Android version?

minulo is an iPhone app available in 13 languages: English, Japanese, Korean, Traditional Chinese, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese, Italian, Indonesian, Thai, Vietnamese and Russian. You can choose a language in Profile > Settings > Language. There is no Android version at the moment. A Mac version for Safari and Chrome is available. The Android version is in testing; join the waitlist to get one email when it is out.

🆚 How is minulo different from SocialLite?

Both hide Reels and Shorts, keep your DMs, and can lock the official apps with iPhone Screen Time and send you to their own view. SocialLite has far more ratings (16,883 in the US as of November 4, 2026). minulo also covers TikTok, Threads, Pinterest and LinkedIn, which SocialLite's App Store page does not mention, and has a Mac app that also works in Chrome. Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts in minulo is free. minulo launched in September 2026 and is still new.

⏱️ YouTube lets me set the Shorts limit to 0. Do I still need minulo?

If you only use YouTube in its app and are happy to stop yourself when the limit appears, YouTube's own Shorts feed limit may be enough. For adults the limit can be dismissed, and it does not cover youtube.com or other networks. minulo hides Shorts in its own view, also covers Instagram, TikTok and 7 more networks, and can lock the official apps.

🆚 How is minulo different from Opal?

Opal blocks whole apps, so while Instagram is blocked, its DMs are blocked too. minulo hides only Reels, Shorts and suggested posts, so DMs and friends' posts keep working. Both use iPhone Screen Time to lock apps; minulo sends you to its own Reels-free view instead of a closed door.
