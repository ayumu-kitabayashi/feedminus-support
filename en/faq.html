minulo FAQ

minulo is an iPhone app that keeps your social media working while hiding Reels, Shorts and recommended posts. Here are answers to the questions people ask most.

Can I hide Instagram Reels and still use DMs?

Yes. When you open Instagram inside minulo, the Reels and Explore tabs and suggested posts are not shown. Your DMs, your friends' posts, your own posts and profiles keep working. You choose what to hide one item at a time, and hiding is free.

Can I hide only YouTube Shorts?

Yes. When you open YouTube inside minulo, the Shorts shelf on the home screen and the Shorts tab are not shown. Search, your subscriptions and regular-length videos keep working. Hiding Shorts is free.

What happens to the official Instagram and TikTok apps?

minulo uses iPhone Screen Time to stop the official apps you choose from opening. When you try to open one, a "Blocked" screen appears, and its "Open minulo" button takes you to minulo instead. Choosing apps and turning blocking on are free.

Which social media services does minulo support?

minulo supports 10 services: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn and Snapchat. For each service, you choose what to hide one item at a time, such as Reels, Shorts, suggested posts or Explore tabs.

Is my password safe?

minulo never receives your ID or password. You sign in directly on each service's official sign-in page. minulo does not collect or store your ID, password or the contents of your DMs, and all of your records stay on your iPhone.

What happens to Reels that friends send me in DMs?

When you open a Reel or Short sent to you in a DM, minulo pauses before the endless vertical video feed begins. If you really want to watch it, tap "Open for 5 min" to watch for five minutes. After five minutes, blocking turns back on automatically.

Is minulo free? What does Pro add?

Hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts, and blocking the official apps, are free. minulo Pro adds schedules that switch automatically by day and time, Locked Mode, which makes turning blocking off take effort, and multiple block lists. Prices are shown in the App Store in your local currency, and the yearly plan includes a one-month free trial.

Can I use minulo if I post to social media for work?

Yes. Posting photos, commenting and replying to DMs work inside minulo. Posting Stories, voice messages and calls are not available because the web versions of these services do not have them, but "Take a break" opens the official app for the minutes you choose.

Are FeedMinus and minulo the same app?

Yes, they are the same app. FeedMinus was renamed minulo in September 2026. The App Store page, the features and what is free have not changed. If you installed it under the old name, updating the app keeps everything and shows the new name.

Which languages does minulo support? Is there an Android version?

minulo is an iPhone app available in English, Japanese, German, French, Spanish, Portuguese and Italian. You can choose a language in Profile > Settings > Language. There is no Android version at the moment.
