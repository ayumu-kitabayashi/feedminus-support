minulo vs Stillfeed: two iPhone apps that hide Reels and Shorts and keep your DMs (2026)

Pick minulo if you want Instagram for DMs only without paying, or also use Threads or Pinterest: in minulo, hiding Reels and Shorts on 10 networks is free, with no subscription and no time limit, your DMs keep working, and the DMs-only view is free too. Stillfeed is the better pick if every network you use is among its 8 (Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit, Snapchat, LinkedIn, Facebook and TikTok), you don't need a messages-only view (its DMs Only is in Pro), or you want a free Family mode for a teen's phone. Since Stillfeed 1.2.6 (November 9), both apps can lock the official apps with Screen Time for free.

The differences are how many networks they cover, whether a messages-only view costs money, and what happens with the official apps.

Pick Stillfeed if

  • every network you use is among Instagram, YouTube, X, Reddit, Snapchat, LinkedIn, Facebook and TikTok;
  • you want a listing that spells out exactly what is removed on YouTube, down to Shorts in search results and /shorts/ links;
  • you like a weekly count of what was blocked;
  • you want a free Family mode that sets limits and locks the social apps on a teen's phone.

Pick minulo if

  • you also use Threads or Pinterest, which are not among Stillfeed's 8 networks;
  • you want Instagram for DMs only without paying;
  • you keep falling back into the official app, and want its block screen to have a button that opens the Reels-free version;
  • you'd rather use an app whose privacy label has no “Data Used to Track You” section.

Which one should a student who needs Instagram for group chats use?

Both keep Instagram DMs while Reels are hidden, and that part is free in both. If you want Instagram to show nothing but messages, Stillfeed's DMs Only is part of Pro, while minulo's Every tab but DMs setting is free. If every network you use is among Stillfeed's 8 and you don't need a messages-only view, Stillfeed covers what you need.

Does Stillfeed work with TikTok?

Yes. Since version 1.2.3 (October 27), its App Store description includes TikTok: For You, Discover, LIVE and mini dramas send you to Following. In minulo, TikTok's For You feed is hidden and messages keep working, and there is also a free DMs-only setting for TikTok.

What happens when I tap the real Instagram icon?

Both apps can lock it now. Since version 1.2.6 (November 9), Stillfeed's free “Lock the real apps” locks your own Instagram, TikTok or YouTube with Screen Time; its App Store page doesn't say what the block screen shows. minulo also locks the official app with Screen Time for free, up to 2 hours per block, and tapping the icon shows a block screen with an Open in minulo button that leads to Instagram without Reels.

Which one is more established?

Both are new. Stillfeed has been on the App Store since July 2026 and minulo since September 2026. Stillfeed had 1 US App Store rating (5.0) on 2026-11-10, and minulo had 1 on 2026-11-01.
