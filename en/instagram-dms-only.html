What happens when I open a Reel a friend sent in DMs?

Inside minulo, a Reel you open from a DM pauses before the vertical video feed starts. Tap Open for 5 min if you want to watch it; after five minutes, blocking turns back on by itself. In the Instagram app, a Reel opened from a chat plays in the Reels viewer, and one swipe takes you to the next one. So when you open a Reel to reply to a friend, the next one is not already playing. YouTube Shorts sent in a DM pause the same way.

What if one group chat sends too many notifications?

Group chats work in minulo's DMs-only view, so you can read and reply there as usual. If one group is too loud, mute just that chat in the Instagram app: open the chat, tap the name at the top, then Mute, and turn on Mute messages. Instagram doesn't tell people you muted them. Your other chats keep notifying you.

How do I get Instagram notifications only for messages?

In the Instagram app, tap your profile, then Menu at the top right, then Notifications. Below Pause push notifications, open each notification type that isn't about messages and turn it off, and leave messages on. We couldn't confirm the exact names of every type in Instagram's help, so go by what each one is about. Also check that Allow Notifications is on for Instagram in iPhone Settings › Notifications. minulo sends no notifications of its own, and while minulo blocks the official app, that app's notifications don't arrive either. Can I still see Stories if I keep only DMs?

With Every tab but DMs on, only DMs and notifications stay. If you want friends' Stories too, leave Every tab but DMs off and turn on Reels tab, Explore tab and Suggested posts instead. Reels, Explore and suggested posts are hidden, Stories stay at the top of the feed, and DMs keep working. Posting Stories isn't available in minulo, because Instagram's website doesn't have it.

Isn't the Following feed or snoozing suggested posts enough?

If posts from strangers are the only problem, Instagram's own settings help a little. Tap Instagram at the top left of your feed and choose Following: you get posts and reels from accounts you follow, newest first, with no suggested posts. Instagram doesn't remember this choice, so the next time you open the app you are back on the regular feed. You can also snooze suggested posts for 30 days from Options › Not interested on a suggested post, but they come back after 30 days, and Reels and Explore are not covered. Either way, the Reels tab and Explore stay on screen.

Touch and hold the Instagram icon

On the Home Screen, touching and holding an app opens its Quick Actions menu, and each app decides what goes in it (Apple's examples are Camera and Maps). We couldn't find a list of Instagram's quick actions in Instagram's help, and older articles disagree: VentureBeat reported a direct message shortcut in 2015, while a Gadget Hacks guide lists Switch Accounts, Camera, New Post and View Activity, with no messages item. Touch and hold the icon on your own iPhone and look. If a messages item is there, it is the quickest way in; if not, this trick doesn't apply. If you hold too long, the icons start to jiggle, so let go a little sooner and try again.

Reply from the Lock Screen

Apple's guide says that touching and holding a notification shows it with quick actions, if the app offers them.
