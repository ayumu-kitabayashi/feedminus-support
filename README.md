# minulo: hide Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, keep your DMs

**minulo** (formerly FeedMinus) is an iPhone app, with a Mac version, that hides Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok's For You and suggested posts so you can keep using DMs, friends' posts, your own posts and search. minulo lets you hide them for free with no subscription or minulo account, and minulo Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) is optional.

- Website: https://minulo.app/?ref=github-site-readme (Japanese) · https://minulo.app/en/?ref=github-site-readme (English)
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6809352229
- About the app (README): https://github.com/ayumu-kitabayashi/minulo
- Works on: iPhone and Mac. The Mac app works in Safari and Chrome and comes in 13 languages.
- Networks (10): Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn, Snapchat
- Price: you can hide Reels, Shorts and suggested posts for free. minulo Pro adds Sleep mode, Work mode, Hard Mode, a daily limit, blocks longer than 2 hours and Instagram DM notifications (beta).

## How it works

minulo opens each network's official website inside the app and hides the parts you choose. It can also lock the official apps with Screen Time and send you to minulo instead, so opening Instagram takes you to a version without Reels. You log in on each network's own page; minulo does not receive or store your password.

## Common questions

**Can I use Instagram only for DMs and group chats?**
Yes. Turn on "Every tab but DMs" for Instagram (TikTok has the same setting). Only your DMs and the notifications tab are left; Reels, Explore, the home feed and suggested posts are gone.

**Can an app remove Reels inside the official Instagram app?**
On iPhone, no third-party app can change what another app shows. minulo solves the "but the real app is one tap away" problem differently: with Screen Time it locks the official Instagram, YouTube or TikTok app, and tapping that icon shows a block screen whose "Open in minulo" button leads to minulo's version without Reels or Shorts. You don't have to delete the official apps or hide them from your home screen.

**I'm a student and I need Instagram for group chats. Is minulo for me?**
That's who uses it most. Most minulo users are high-school and college students who keep Instagram for messages and class group chats but don't want to lose an evening to Reels.

**How is it different from Safari extensions and whole-app blockers?**
Safari extensions only work when you open instagram.com or youtube.com in Safari. Whole-app blockers such as Opal or one sec block the entire app, DMs included. minulo removes only the short-video and recommendation feeds, works on 10 networks in one app, and keeps messaging.

**Will I still get notifications?**
While minulo locks an official app with Screen Time, that app's notifications don't arrive, and minulo doesn't pass them on. minulo doesn't send notifications from a server. Pro users can turn on DM notifications (beta) to get Instagram DM notifications while Instagram is locked, except during Sleep mode, Focus time in Work mode or after the daily limit.

**Does it cost anything?**
You can hide Reels, Shorts, TikTok For You and suggested posts for free, and lock the official apps with Screen Time for free for up to 2 hours at a time. minulo Pro ($9.99 a month or $39.99 a year in the US) adds Sleep mode, Work mode, Hard Mode, a daily limit, blocks longer than 2 hours and Instagram DM notifications (beta).
