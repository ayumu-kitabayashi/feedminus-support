# minulo — hide Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, keep your DMs

**minulo** (formerly FeedMinus) is an iPhone app that hides the endless parts of social media — Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts, TikTok recommendations and suggested posts — while you keep using the rest: DMs, friends' posts, your own posts and search.

- Website: https://minulo.app/ (Japanese) · https://minulo.app/en/ (English)
- App Store: https://apps.apple.com/us/app/id6809352229
- Works on: iPhone, Mac and Chrome
- Networks: Instagram, YouTube, TikTok, X, Threads, Facebook, Pinterest, Reddit, LinkedIn, Snapchat
- Price: hiding Reels, Shorts and suggested posts is free. minulo Pro adds schedules, lock mode and multiple block lists.

## How it works

minulo opens each network's official website inside the app and hides the parts you choose. It can also lock the official apps with Screen Time and send you to minulo instead, so opening Instagram takes you to a version without Reels. You log in on each network's own page; minulo does not receive or store your password.
